Mahavir Sanctuary

Explore the Untamed Wilderness of Uttar Pradesh.

About the Sanctuary

Mahavir Sanctuary, situated in Uttar Pradesh, India, is a pristine destination that provides a crucial habitat for a wide variety of wildlife and allows visitors to fully immerse themselves in nature.

This sanctuary offers a tranquil setting for those interested in discovering the natural wonders of Uttar Pradesh, making it a must-visit for wildlife lovers and eco-conscious travelers.

Wildlife and Conservation

Known for its diverse range of plant and animal species, the sanctuary provides a peaceful setting for visitors to discover the native wildlife. It is a popular spot for bird enthusiasts and photographers interested in capturing the untouched beauty of Northern India.

Ecological Importance

Preserving endangered species and promoting a harmonious ecosystem in the heart of Uttar Pradesh.

Nature Exploration

An ideal spot for nature enthusiasts in search of tranquility and unspoiled natural landscapes far from city life's hustle and bustle.

Quick Facts

Location

Uttar Pradesh, Northern India.

Category

Wildlife Sanctuary & Nature Reserve.
