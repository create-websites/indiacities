Cuttack: The Silver City

A thousand-year-old cultural hub located in the Mahanadi River delta.

Overview

Cuttack, one of Odisha's twin cities, was once the political and commercial hub of the state. With a history spanning over a thousand years, the city is built around ancient origins. Barabati Fort.

Situated at the center of the Mahanadi River Cutting through the city, the tributaries Kathjori, Kuakhai, and Birupa embrace the delta. Cuttack is the central hub of the region. Oriya film industry.

A Tapestry of Faith

Cuttack boasts a variety of religious sites catering to Hindus, Muslims, Christians, and Sikhs.

Hindu Temples

  • Katak Chandi Temple
  • Paramahansa Nath Temple
  • Dhabaleswar Temple (with unique hanging bridge)
  • Panchamukhi Hanuman Temple
  • Gada Chandi Temple
  • Bhattarika Temple

Other Shrines

  • Sikh: Gurdwara Daatan Sahib
  • Muslim: Qadam-E-Rasool, Shahi Mosque, Juma Masjid, Ujale Khan Mosque
  • Christian: Holy Rosary Church, Oriya Baptist Church

Historical Landmarks

Barabati Fort

Standing on the banks of the Mahanadi, the remains of this nine-story medieval fortress, complete with moat and gate, still stand tall.

Stone Revetment

A remarkable feat of engineering from 11th-century Odisha discovered along the shores of the Cuttack River.

Museums & Parks

The Netaji Museum Must-see cultural spots include Janakinath Bhawan, Freedom Fighters Memorial, Biju-Pattnaik Park, and the Deer Park.

Shopping & Crafts

Cuttack is known as a top shopping hub in the state, celebrated for its detailed arts and crafts.

Silver Filigree (Tarakasi)

The city's oldest and most renowned craft, which has bestowed upon Cuttack the title of the 'Silver City'.

Other Specialties

Items crafted from animal horns, brass, and traditional silk and cotton fabrics.

Visitor Info

Climate

Summer: Touches 40°C
Winter: Dips below 10°C

Sports Hub

Barabati Stadium is a prominent location for international cricket matches, along with the Jawaharlal Nehru Indoor Stadium, Satyabrata Stadium, and a SAI training center in the city.

Key Facts

Altitude: 14.62 meters
Significance: Commercial Capital of Odisha
