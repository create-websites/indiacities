Dalhousie

A charming hill station nestled among five hills on the majestic Dhauladhar range.

About the Hill Station

Dalhousie is a favorite tourist destination in the Chamba district Himachal Pradesh, this city is constructed on five hills and located on the Dhauladhar mountain rangeIt is located at an elevation of 6,000 to 9,000 feet above sea level.

The town was named after Lord Dalhousie, the British Viceroy who designated it as his summer getaway. Nowadays, the town's allure is amplified by its stunning Victorian and Scottish architecture seen in its many bungalows and churches.

Climate & Best Time to Visit

The optimal time to visit and experience peak tourist season is from May to SeptemberDalhousie experiences a consistent cold climate year-round, with weather changes occurring with each season.

  • May - July: Warm afternoons, but cold at other times.
  • June - September: Heavy rain and thunder.
  • December - January: Experiences snowfall.

Economy

Tourism is the major industry, contributing approximately 3% to the state's GDPThe sector employs between 5,000 and 8,000 people every year, with around 600 hotels in operation.

Education

Dalhousie is a hub for boarding schools, which primarily draws students from Punjab and Haryana. Key institutions in the area include Dalhousie Public School and Hilltop Public School.

Places of Interest

Karelanu

Renowned for its natural spring water, it is widely recognized for its healing properties. Subhash Chandra Bose of tuberculosis during his stay in the region.

Gandhi Chowk

An unusual intersection where eight roads converge, linking key locations such as Subhash Chowk, Sadar Bazaar, and Khajjiar.

Scenic Attractions

Top spots include Dainkund Peak, Bakrota Hills, the Aread potato fields near Alla, and the beautiful Khajjiar.
