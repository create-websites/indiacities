Great Himalayan National Park

A site designated as a UNESCO World Heritage site, protecting the untouched beauty of the Western Himalayas.

Park Snapshot

Area: 765 sq. kms
Altitude: 1,300m To 6,100m
Best Time: Apr-June & Sept-Nov
Climate: -5ºC to 30ºC

History & Ecosystem

Established in 1984 to safeguard endangered species, the Great Himalayan National Park (also referred to as Jawahar Lal Nehru National Park) is situated in the upper reaches of the Himalayas in Himachal Pradesh.

It is one of the few remaining untouched Western Himalayan ecosystems, with a significant area permanently covered in glaciers and ice. This results in a distinctive biological diversity found only in this part of India.

Biodiversity & Wildlife

The Snow Leopard

The Snow Leopard, a highly endangered species, reigns over these mountains as the park's most elusive attraction, despite their inability to growl loudly because of their weak vocal tissues.

Avian Wonders

Over 203 bird species call this place home, including the elusive Western Tragopan, the vibrant Monal, and the Koklass pheasant.

Mammalian Diversity

Observe rare species like the Nilgiri Tahr, Serow, Ghoral, Bharal, Brown Bear, Musk Deer, and Red Fox.

Lower Reaches

You can spot the Indian Pika and Great Indian Flying Squirrel in the easily reachable lower regions of the park.

Trekking & Exploration

The majority of the park remains untouched because of its challenging terrain, giving it an exhilarating and rugged atmosphere. Hiking in this area demands top physical fitness and the right gear, such as tents and sleeping bags.

The optimal time for trekking is in April and May when the snow starts to thaw.

Please be advised that horse or mule transport is prohibited within the park boundaries, and all exploration must be conducted on foot.

Travel Guide

By Air

The nearest airport to Kullu town is Bhuntar Airport (Kullu), just 10 km away, with easy access to taxis.

By Rail

The closest narrow gauge railhead to Kullu is located at Jogindernagar, around 95 kilometers away.

By Road

Head east at Aut on the Kullu-Manali highway, Aut is located 30 km away from Kullu.

Important Tips

Make sure to see a doctor before your journey and obtain any necessary permits. Stop by the Larjee Information Centre for park information.
