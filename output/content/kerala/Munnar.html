Munnar: The Green Paradise

A hill retreat from the colonial era known for its lush tea plantations and fog-covered peaks.

Quick Facts

Area: 557 sq kms
Altitude: 1,600 meters
Best Time: September to May
Rainfall: 270 cm (Annual)
Languages: Malayalam, Tamil, English
STD Code: 04865

History of Munnar

Munnar has a rich history dating back to the colonial period, when Scottish planters first uncovered its beauty. It quickly became a popular destination for British residents seeking relief from the hot plains, with the Duke of Wellington being one of the first prominent figures to explore the area.

The land was mainly cultivated for tea plantations, with historical records tracing back to the 10th century. Prehistoric artifacts indicate the presence of a Stone Age society in this area. Messrs Finlay Muir & Company bought the land in 1895, later merging with Tata-Finlay Ltd. in 1976. Nowadays, it serves as a center for various adventure activities such as trekking, paragliding, and mountaineering.

Must-Visit Attractions

Anaimudi Peak

The tallest mountain in South India, reaching approximately 2700 meters, is a paradise for nature enthusiasts, providing a serene mountain ambiance and perfect locations for camping and hiking.

Echo Point

It is believed that hearing your echo call back three times at this natural echo phenomenon is considered lucky.

Rajamala

At an altitude of 2700 meters above sea level, this area is the native home of the elusive Nilgiri Tahr, making it an ideal location for relaxation and rejuvenation.

Kundala

Situated 20km away from Munnar, featuring a charming man-made dam and a golf course under Tata Tea Ltd.'s ownership.

Scenic Water & Views

Power House & Kuthumkal Waterfalls

Power House plunges 2000 meters over jagged rocks, while Kuthumkal conjures a captivating misty ambiance from its stunning summit.

Malankara Reservoir

Located on the Thodupuzha-Moolavattom road, this reservoir is a favorite destination for boating and fishing enthusiasts.

Observatory Hills

Situated in Nedumkandam, this location boasts incredibly clear skies, perfect for stargazing and spotting constellations and planets after dark.

Travel Guide

By Air

Nearest airport is KochiMadurai and Coimbatore airports provide excellent connectivity to the region.

By Rail

Munnar has no railway station. Use railheads at Kochi, Madurai, or Kottayam and complete the journey by taxi or bus.

By Road

Munnar is well-connected to neighboring cities by state transport buses, despite the winding roads.
