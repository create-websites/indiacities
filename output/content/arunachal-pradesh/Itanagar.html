Itanagar: The Land of Dawn

A hill retreat hidden at the foot of the towering Eastern Himalayas.

City Overview

Itanagar, a hill resort, serves as the capital of Arunachal Pradesh and is surrounded by the powerful. Eastern Himalayas on one side and the plains of the Brahmaputra River on the south.

Light, earthquake-resistant, wooden-framed buildings and traditional huts dot the green hills of the township, with the Governor's residence perched on one peak and a stunning Buddhist Temple on another.

The area is inhabited by friendly martial tribes and boasts stunning views of lush greenery and towering peaks.

Major Attractions

Ganga Lake (Gekar Sinyi)

A serene forest lake, perfect for boating and picnics, is nestled at the end of a 6 km winding drive through primeval jungles, bamboo groves, and orchid-massed trees. Crossing the ridge after an uphill climb, visitors are greeted with this placid green oasis.

Itafort (Brick Fort)

The city gets its name from a historic fort constructed in the 14th century, which is believed to consist of around 8 million bricks.

Buddha Temple

A fresh shrine with a yellow roof provides sweeping views of Itanagar Town, with a tree planted by His Holiness the Dalai Lama on its premises.

Culture & Nature

Jawaharlal Nehru State Museum

It features the rich socio-cultural legacy of indigenous tribes, with exhibits ranging from archaeological discoveries from Itafort to demonstrations on traditional dwellings, celebrations, fabrics, and weaponry.

Polo Park

A fascinating botanical garden and scenic viewpoint with views of the town, including a small zoo and a distinctive cane thicket resembling a spiky palm bush.

Namdhapa National Park

A popular wildlife destination housing a unique mix of tigers, leopards, clouded leopards, and snow leopards.

Handicrafts & Shopping

Itanagar is a great place for shopping for handicrafts as visitors can discover a diverse selection.

  • Tribal Shawls
  • Hand-woven Carpets
  • Carved Baskets
  • Cane & Bamboo Work
