Bangalore: The Garden City

The Silicon Valley of India boasts a delightful climate, beautiful parks, and historic landmarks.

Quick Facts

Area: 2190 sq. km
Altitude: 920 m - 949 m above sea level
Best Time: September to February
Languages: Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, English
Summer: 21.2°C - 33.4°C
STD Code: 080

Overview & History

Bangalore, recently rechristened as 'Bengaluru', the capital of Karnataka and one of the rapidly growing cities in India, is said to have derived its name from folklore. "Bendha KaaLu" (beans that were boiled) after an elderly woman nourished a hungry King Veera Ballala. Despite its popularity, historical evidence of the name can be found in 9th-century temple inscriptions in Begur. BengaLooru existing much earlier.

Established in the 1500s by Kempe Gowda, a leader of the Vijayanagar Empire, this city sprawls across the Deccan Plateau. With its elevated position, it boasts one of the most favorable climates in all of India, leading to its nickname as the "City of Perfect Weather." 'Garden City of India'.

Sightseeing In Bangalore

Bangalore Palace

Built in 1887, this architectural marvel was designed to resemble Windsor Castle, showcasing fortified structures and intricately carved wooden interiors.

Shimmering Lakes

The urban hub boasts a variety of leisure destinations such as Ulsoor Lake, Sankey Tank, and Yediyur Lake, offering a peaceful retreat from city life.

Art & Heritage

Visit the Venkatappa Art Gallery to view masterpieces by M.F. Hussain and R. Venkatappa, or check out the Karnataka Chitrakala Parishat for a diverse collection of traditional artifacts.

Amusement & Speed

Thrill-seekers can enjoy Go-karting at the Kingfisher Speedzone in Whitefield, or experience over 40 fun rides spread across 30 acres at Neeladri Amusement and Water Park.

Popular Excursions

Devarayanadurga (70 km)

Abundant greenery at an elevation of 1188m. Ideal for outdoor adventures, exploring historic twin temples, and enjoying a refreshing natural spring.

Shivaganga (Height: 1368m)

An incredible hill that resembles various Hindu deities, such as Ganesha, Shivalinga, or a cobra, depending on the perspective. Provides stunning panoramic views.

Bhimeshwari (100 km)

A small village perfect for fishing and angling, renowned for its rich biodiversity and riverside accommodations.

How to Reach

By Air

The Bangalore Airport, located 6 km from the city center, serves domestic and international flights to and from London, Paris, Singapore, and other major Indian cities.

By Rail

Key stations in Bangalore are Bangalore City and Bangalore Cantt, with daily service to New Delhi provided by the Karnataka Exp.

By Road

Linked to Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra through a network of quality state and national roads.

Shopping

Visit MG Road, Brigade Road, or Commercial Street for authentic silk, sandalwood products, and jewelry.
