Ranchi: The Waterfall City

A serene industrial center, graced with lush scenery and abundant natural assets.

City Overview

Ranchi, the capital of Jharkhand, is quickly becoming a major industrial hub and is often referred to as the 'Manchester of the East.' The city boasts abundant natural resources, accounting for half of the state's mineral production. It is renowned as the residence of cricket icon Mahendra Singh Dhoni and played a pivotal role in the statehood movement of Jharkhand.

Climate & Best Time to Visit

Surrounded by hills and lush tropical forests, Ranchi experiences a delightful climate all year round, making it the former summer capital of Bihar.

Summers: 20°C to 42°C
Winters: 5°C to 25°C

Best Time: September to May

The City of Waterfalls

Hundru Falls

An enchanting destination where the Subarnarekha River cascades from a 320-foot height, making it a top choice for picnics in the area.

Jonha Falls

The stepped waterfall, also called Gautamdhara, boasts a 500-step descent and is surrounded by a Buddha temple and the Kanchi River.

Dassam Falls

The Kanchi River, originating near Taimara village and cascading from a height of 144 feet, creates an ideal spot for river rafting and swimming.

Panch Gagh & Hirni

The Panch Gagh falls and the picturesque Hirni falls are also stunning gems, celebrated for their lush landscapes.

Major Landmarks

Rock Garden

The city's top attraction is a popular spot with stunning granite sculptures, waterfalls, and swings nestled in a picturesque natural setting.

Sun Temple

Situated 40 kilometers away from Ranchi, this stunning architectural wonder resembles an 18-wheeled chariot being pulled by seven white horses.

Ranchi Hill & Pahari Mandir

The Pahari Mandir is a significant landmark in the town, dedicated to Lord Shiva and providing stunning panoramic views from its hilltop location.

Tagore Hill

Known for its connection to Rabindranath Tagore, who is rumored to have penned numerous works in this tranquil setting.

Visitor Info

Economic Hub

A rapidly growing industrial city attracting business travelers with its mineral wealth.

Education

Home to several reputed educational institutions.

Nearby Spots

  • Gonda Hill & Kanke Dam
  • Jagannathpur Hill (Kalinga style temple)
  • Kanchi River Water Sports
