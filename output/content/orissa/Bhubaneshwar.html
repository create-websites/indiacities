Bhubaneshwar: The Temple City

An age-old city where rich history, lifelike sculptures, and stunning architecture come together.

City Overview

Ancient Bhubaneshwar, known as the Temple City of India, serving as the political capital since 1948, is not only the largest city but also the capital of Odisha. Once known by various names like Toshali, Kalinga Nagari, and Ekamra Khetra, it is now considered one of the 'twin cities' alongside Cuttack, and is quickly becoming a major IT center.

Climate

The city has a tropical climate with distinct dry and wet seasons.

Summer: Shoots up to 40°C - 45°C
Winter: Dips to a minimum of 10°C

Average Annual Rainfall: 154 cm

Major Pilgrimage Attractions

Bhubaneshwar completes the religious Golden Triangle The temples in Eastern India, including those in Puri and Konark, are constructed in the unique Kalinga architectural style.

Lingaraj Temple

This 11th-century masterpiece, dedicated to Lord Shiva, rises on a 55m-high carved surface and is an essential pilgrimage site visited before the Jagannath Temple at Puri.

Mukteshwara Temple

A gem from the 10th century that highlights the evolution of Kalinga architecture, renowned for its stunning arched gateway. torana, and incredibly realistic sculptures.

Dhauli Giri

Situated 8 kilometers from here, this location was the scene of the Kalinga War. It is home to Ashokan edicts and Buddhist symbols, commemorating the moment when Ashoka the Great adopted the path of peace.

Khandagiri & Udayagiri

Two hills with intricate rock-cut caves from an ancient Jain monastery, including the renowned Hati Gufa and the two-story Rani Gufa.

Cultural & Institutional Landmarks

Museums

The Odisha State Museum and the Museum of Tribal Art & Artefacts offer deep insights into the region's heritage and tribal traditions.

Nandankanan Zoo

A popular destination for nature enthusiasts, renowned for its dedication to wildlife preservation and wide variety of animal species.

Unique Sites

Look out for Traffic Mahadev, a tiny Shiva temple that doubles as a road divider within the city.

Visitor Info

Architecture

Constructed in the classic Kalinga fashion, representing the rich heritage of Oriya culture throughout the ages.

Holy Places

  • Rajarani Temple
  • Ram Mandir
  • ISKCON Temple
  • Brahmeswar Temple

Key Facts

Bhubaneshwar and Cuttack, often referred to as the 'twin cities,' serve as a significant economic and religious center in Eastern India.
