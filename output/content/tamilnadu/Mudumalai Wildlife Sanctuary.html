Mudumalai Wildlife Sanctuary

South India's First Wildlife Reserve

Sanctuary Details

Area

32,155 Hectares

Altitude

350 - 1266m

Best Time

Feb to June

Temp

4.8°C - 24.3°C

Wildlife & Fauna

Mudumalai is home to a vast number of elephants, along with tigers, leopards, hyenas, sloth bears, and the Nilgiri Tahr. Bird enthusiasts can observe more than 200 species, such as the Crested Hawk Eagle and Malabar Grey Hornbill.

Flora

The landscape features a variety of tropical evergreen forests, moist teak forests, and grassy woodlands, with valuable timber trees such as Rosewood, Teak, sandalwood, mango, and banyan trees.

How to Reach

Air: Coimbatore Airport (160km).

Rail: Udhagamandalam Railway Station (65km).

Road: Gudalur is the nearest town (16km).
