Kozhikode: Harbour of Honesty

An ancient Malabar region where lush greenery, refreshing breezes, and rich trade history come together.

City Overview

Kozhikode, also widely known as CalicutThe city, known for the honesty of its inhabitants, has long been a key hub for trade and commerce. It is lovingly referred to as the. "Harbour of Honesty" and sometimes the "Cock Fort."

Known for its lush green landscape and refreshing sea winds, this region is renowned worldwide for its cultivation and shipment of pepper, coconut, coffee, rubber, and lemongrass oil. Additionally, it holds significance as the hometown of influential Indian figures such as V. K. Krishna Menon and P.T. Usha.

Best Time to Visit: September to February

Major Attractions

Mananchira Square

An architectural wonder encircles the Mananchira tank, with traditional structures, a musical fountain, and historical sites such as the Public Library and Mishkal Mosque bordering the lush green carpet.

Kappad Beach

A stone monument stands on the historic Indian shore where Vasco da Gama first arrived on May 27, 1498, marking this significant moment in history.

Thali Temple

A respected Hindu temple that was well-known for holding the annual 'Revathi Pattanam' competition, where artists exhibited their talents in front of the Samuthiri rulers.

Dolphin Point

Situated close to Kozhikode Beach, this location provides a delightful sight of dolphins frolicking in the ocean, making it ideal for morning sightseers.

Experience & Exploration

Regional Science Centre

Situated in Jaffarkhan Colony, this spot includes a planetarium and interactive models to discover the cosmos, as well as educational games and scientific tools for kids.

Thusharagiri Waterfall

A stunning waterfall renowned for its beauty and peaceful atmosphere, making it a favorite destination for outdoor enthusiasts.

Pisharikavu Temple

The temple, home to ancient murals, springs to life during its yearly festival in April-May.

Quick Info

Kozhikode borders Wayanad, Malappuram, and Kannur and is renowned as a prominent spice trading center worldwide.
