Kottayam: The Gateway to Backwaters

A bustling metropolis where vibrant commerce intertwines with the tranquil beauty of lush green landscapes.

Quick Facts

Area: 2203 Sq.Km
Altitude: Sea level
Best Time: August to March
Languages: Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi, English
Summer: 22.5°C - 35°C
STD Code: 0481

History & Overview

Kottayam, located to the south of Cochin, is a captivating city that blends a vibrant commercial hub with serene backwaters and picturesque greenery for a peaceful escape.

The city is well-known for its stunning churches, some of which are nearly 700 years old and adorned with intricate carvings and paintings. Among them, the most renowned is St. Mary's Church, which houses the remains of Alphonsa and Father Chavara.

Major Attractions

Vembanad Lake

Situated 12 km to the west of the city, this is the largest freshwater lake in Asia, with expansive shores and islands that harbor unique plant and animal species, making Kottayam a top backwater destination.

Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary

Vembanad Lake's marshy area is a haven for both resident and migratory birds like the Siberian stork, darter, and egret, making it a birdwatcher's paradise.

Vaikom Temple

A significant Hindu pilgrimage site, renowned for its distinctive architectural design, where followers come together daily to participate in sacred ceremonies.

Thazhathangadi Mosque

Constructed by Malik Dinar over 1,000 years ago, this mosque is revered as a historic pilgrimage destination, known for its stunning architecture.

Things to Do

Backwater Cruises

Embark on a luxury houseboat journey through the picturesque Vembanad Lake to witness the beauty of nature. Sail through the Kerala backwaters to explore unique destinations and experience traditional life, creating unforgettable memories.

Historic Churches

Valiyapalli Church

Constructed in the 16th century by immigrants from Jerusalem, this landmark is renowned for its Persian crosses, consisting of two massive granite structures transported from Cranganmore.

Travel Guide

By Air

The closest airport to Cochin offers regular flights to Delhi and other major cities.

By Rail

Kottayam railway station, located just 2 km from the bus stand, serves as a key transportation hub connecting major cities in Kerala and national destinations through trains such as the Kerala Express.

By Road

Two nearby bus stops in the city center provide both KSRTC and interstate services to neighboring states.

By Ferry

The ferry terminal, located 3 km from the railway station, offers connections to Alleppey, Munnar, and Ambalap
