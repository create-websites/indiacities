Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary

Explore Goa's family-friendly wilderness nestled in the Western Ghats.

Sanctuary Snapshot

Area: 8 square kilometers
Location: Ponda Taluka, Central Goa
Best Time: October to March
Timings: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Closed Thursdays)

About Bondla

The Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary, situated just 52 km from Panaji at the base of the Western Ghats, may be deemed 'rather small' according to official criteria, but it remains a favorite spot for visitors due to its attractions such as the Mini Zoo, Deer Safari Park, and Botanical Gardens.

The sanctuary contains both moist deciduous forests and small sections of evergreen trees, making it an ideal location to spot Goa's state animal, the ________. Gaur, along with the state tree, the Terminalia Crenulata (Matti).

Major Attractions

Zoological Park

A small zoo located in a natural setting with elephants, panthers, lions, sloth bears, and different kinds of reptiles.

Deer Safari Park

Drive a vehicle through the park to observe sambar and spotted deer in their natural habitat.

Formal & Botanical Gardens

Vibrant lawns filled with unique trees, bright roses, and blooming plants situated amidst the forest.

Nature Education Centre

A center for interpretation featuring exhibits, a library, and video movies available upon request.

Wildlife & Nature Trails

Birdwatching

There are over a hundred bird species in Bondla. Birdwatchers can find the best spotting locations by contacting the Park Range Office.

Animal Sightings

Observing the water-holes with watchtowers can lead to sightings of gaur, sambar, and wild boar, as well as possible encounters with jungle cats, porcupines, and ant eaters.

Archaeological Exhibits

Explore the ancient relief designs featuring the deity Gajalaxmi, dating back to the sixth through eighth centuries.

How to Reach

By Road

Off NH 4A, there are two branches at Mollem (20 km) and Tiska (14 km) respectively. Panjim is located 52 km away.

By Rail

Closest stations are Collem, located 28 km away on the Hubli line, or Carambolim, situated 40 km away on the Konkan Railway.

By Air

Dabolim Airport is located around 70 kilometers away when traveling through Ponda and Usgao.

Visitor Tip

The bus service runs daily from Tiska to Bondla, with the exception of Thursdays. Elephant rides are typically available in the afternoons at around 4:00 PM.
