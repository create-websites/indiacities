Jabalpur: The Marble City

An impressive city nestled along the Narmada River, enveloped by rich history and breathtaking natural beauty.

Overview

Located in central Madhya Pradesh along the banks of the sacred Narmada River, Jabalpur is a significant city with a rich history. It has been a capital for various ruling dynasties such as the Kalchuri and Gond, and later served as the commission headquarters for the Narmada territory during British rule. Jabalpur is renowned for its breathtaking Marble Rocks and acts as a starting point for exploring wildlife havens like Kanha and Bandhavgarh National Parks.

Altitude: 212 meters
Rainfall: 152 cms

History

Anciently known as TripuriJabalpur was ruled by the Hayahaya dynasty and mentioned in the Mahabharata. It later became part of the Mauryan and Gupta Empires before being taken over by the Kalchuri and Gond dynasties. By the 16th century, it had evolved into the formidable kingdom of Gondwana.

The legendary Gond Queen, Rani DurgavatiThey perished in battle against Emperor Akbar's Mughal army on this very soil. After Maratha rule, the British assumed authority in 1817, designating Jabalpur as the administrative center for the Narmada regions.

Natural Spectacles

Marble Rocks (Bhedaghat)

The Narmada is flanked by towering white marble rocks, reaching a height of one hundred feet on each side. The peaceful beauty of the landscape is enhanced by the sunlight glistening on the dazzling white marble peaks.

Dhuandhar Falls

Referred to as the 'smoke cascade,' the Narmada rushes through the Marble Rocks in a mighty waterfall, its thunderous sound echoing for miles around.

Historic & Religious Landmarks

  • Madan Mahal Fort

    Constructed in 1116 by Gond ruler Raja Madan Shah on a rugged hill, this fortress towers over the town and provides a sweeping vista of the surroundings.

  • Chausat Yogini Temple

    This 10th-century temple is dedicated to Goddess Durga and features intricately carved stone figures from the Kalchuri period, offering a stunning view of the Narmada River.

  • Religious Sites

    Tilwaraghat is where Mahatma Gandhi's ashes were scattered, along with other significant sites like the Tripur Sundari Temple and the renowned Jain temple Pisanhari ki Madiya.

Travel Guide

By Air

Jabalpur operates its own domestic airport, with regular flights arriving from Delhi, Bhopal, and other major cities.

By Rail

The Jabalpur railhead is situated on the main Mumbai-Howrah line, offering overnight train services from Kolkata, Bangalore, Mumbai, and Delhi.

By Road

Jabalpur is well-connected to most major cities in Madhya Pradesh through regular bus services.
