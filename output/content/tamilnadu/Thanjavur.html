Thanjavur Tour

The Chola Capital and the Granary of South India.

A Center for Classical Arts

Thanjavur continues to be a prominent hub for classical arts, music, and the distinct Tanjore painting style, with its architectural marvels mostly attributed to the Chola dynasty.

Places of Interest

Brahadeeswarar (Big Temple)

An impressive example of Chola architecture, the temple rises to a height of 216 feet and is strategically built to ensure that its shadow never touches the ground.

Saraswathi Mahal Library

Established in approximately 1700 AD, this collection includes more than 44,000 palm leaf and paper manuscripts.

Royal Palace

An impressive structure with a seven-story tower reserved for the royal family's use.

Rajagopala Beerangi

The immense cannon found in India showcases the remarkable metallurgical expertise of that time.
