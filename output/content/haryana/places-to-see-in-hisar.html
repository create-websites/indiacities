Hisar: The Fortress of Feroz

An ancient crossroads of Harappan civilization and Tughlaq architectural grandeur.

Location and History

Formerly spelled as Hissar, this city in Haryana was founded by the ruler Firoze Shah TughlaqThe word's Turkish origin translates to 'fortress,' and it was first called Hissar-e-Firoza (the Fort of Feroz).

Connected with ancient Vedic tribes
Main cotton industry hub of India
Home to Hissar Agricultural Institute
162 km from Delhi

Architectural Landmarks

Palace of Feroze Shah

An impressive structure with a hidden chamber and mosque, sitting alongside the remains of the Harappan civilization, merging various periods of history.

Lat Ki Masjid

A notable L-shaped mosque built in the Suljuk style, featuring Hindu grid influences and renowned for its distinctive lithic pillar and square chamber.

Gujri Mahal

Constructed using stone, this palace now lies in disrepair, yet its elevated platform, once used for ancient social events, still stands intact.

Jahaz Kothi

An opulent palace blending Mughal influences, commissioned by an Irish ruler named Jahaz Sahib from the Mughal dynasty.

Ancient Heritage

Hisar is a significant site for the Harappan Civilization, with prominent ruins found at three specific mounds:

  • Banavali: Shows the ruins of an ancient township.
  • Kunal: Displays three distinct stages of civilization development.
  • Agroha: Unveiled the unearthing of coins from the 3rd Century B.C.

Sacred Sites

  • Jain Pillar

    Just beyond the fort lies a 2-foot pillar adorned with a diamond symbol, revered as extremely sacred by the Jain community.

  • Tomb of Pranpir Badshah

    The final resting place of the esteemed Sufi saint can be found within the grounds of the Government College of Hisar, a location that is truly one-of-a-kind.

Travel Quick Facts

Proximity

Educational Hub

Home to major institutions like the Hissar Agricultural Institute.
