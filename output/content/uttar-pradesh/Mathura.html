Mathura: The Holy Abode

Discover the hallowed origins of Lord Krishna and the essence of Brij tradition.

Krishnajanmabhoomi

Mathura is widely known as the birthplace of Lord Krishna, highly respected by Hindu pilgrims and a must-visit destination in Uttar Pradesh. With a history spanning 3,000 years, the city has been a center of culture and civilization, revered by followers of Buddhism, Jainism, and Brahmanism.

145 Km south-east of Delhi
Best visit: October to March

Major Religious Sites

Shri Krishna Janma Bhoomi

It is believed that the exact location of Lord Krishna's birth was in King Kansa's prison 5,000 years ago, as marked by a stone slab within the temple.

Dwarkadheesh Temple

Constructed in 1814 along the banks of the Yamuna River, this temple is the most renowned in the city, celebrated for its magnificent design and ornate festival adornments.

Vishram Ghat

A hallowed area on the Yamuna River where Lord Krishna is believed to have rested following his victory over the tyrannical King Kansa.

Krishna Balrama Mandir (ISKCON)

Founded in 1975, it stands as a pinnacle of Deity worship in Vrindavan, drawing followers from across the globe.

Festivals & Traditions

  • Holi: A lively festival held during the full moon in February or March, featuring colorful dye throwing and festive processions.

  • Janmashtami: Thousands gather to celebrate Krishna's birth at midnight with folklore, recitals, and an electrifying atmosphere.

Temples of Vrindavan

Jaipur Temple

Constructed in 1917 by the Maharaja of Jaipur, showcasing intricate craftsmanship and sturdy stone columns.
