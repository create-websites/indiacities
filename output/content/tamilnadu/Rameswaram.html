Rameswaram Tour

The Varanasi of the South and an Engineering Wonder.

Overview

Rameswaram, shaped like Vishnu's Conch, is where Lord Rama expressed gratitude to Shiva. The town is linked by the Indira Gandhi Bridge, a remarkable feat of engineering.

Sightseeing

Ramanathaswamy Temple

This Jyotirlinga is one of the twelve, boasting 22 wells filled with water known for its varied tastes and healing qualities.

DhanushKodi

Named for Lord Rama's bow, this part of the island was mostly destroyed in 1964 yet still holds significance as a spiritual location.

Erwadi

A well-known pilgrimage destination for Muslims, containing the tomb of Ibrahim Sahid Auliya.

Getting There

Rail: Directly connected to Chennai, Madurai, and Trichy.

Air: Madurai Airport (167km) is the nearest.
