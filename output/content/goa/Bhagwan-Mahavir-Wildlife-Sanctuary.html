Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary

A dense expanse of green forests offering refuge to Goa's unique wildlife.

Sanctuary Profile

Area: 240 square kilometers
Best Time: October to March
Location: Mollem, Goa (65 km from Panaji)
Major Attractions: Panther, Gaur, Dudhsagar Falls

History & Vegetation

While Goa is renowned for its stunning beaches, it also boasts remarkable biodiversity. Previously named Mollem Game Sanctuary, the Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary was renamed in order to protect its 240 sq. km of untouched wilderness.

The sanctuary consists of tropical evergreen and moist deciduous forests. Major tree species include Xylia, Lagerstroemia, and TerminaliaThe landscape features dense grass and ancient jungle settings.

Wildlife & Birdwatching

Animal Kingdom

See leopards, panthers, mouse deer, barking deer, wild boar, sambhar, Malabar giant squirrels, and the majestic Gaur (Indian Bison) in the wild. Keep an eye out for rare pangolins and

Aviary Paradise

Around 200 different bird species make their home in this area. Keep an eye out for the Indian hornbill, fairy bluebird, black woodpecker, wagtails, kingfishers, and emerald doves while observing from watch

Must-See Landmarks

Dudhsagar Falls

At 310m tall, the 'Ocean of Milk' is the tallest waterfall in Goa and the fifth tallest in India. It is adorned with a stunning South Central Railway aqueduct that runs through it. Visitors can reach it by hiking or driving a 4-wheel drive vehicle from Collem.

Tambdi Surla Temple

A stunning Shiva temple from the 12th century, constructed with basalt and adorned with detailed carvings of elephants and chains on its pillars, showcasing a garbhagriha and antarala.

Devil's Canyon

Downstream from Dudhsagar, a haunting canyon of water-carved crevices, sculpted from solid rock by twisting underwater currents.

Visitor Info

Hours

8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Everyday

Entry Fees

Adults: Rs. 20

Children: Rs. 5

Transport

By Air: Dabolim Airport (80 km)

By Rail: Colem Station (6 km)

By Road: State buses & taxis available.

Safety Note

During the monsoons (June-July), accessing Dudhsagar is both hazardous and restricted, while hiring a local guide is essential for Tambdi Falls.
