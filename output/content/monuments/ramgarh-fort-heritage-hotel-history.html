A Heritage Reimagined

The Fort Ramgarh seamlessly combines history with hospitality, as the Chandel descendants have turned their ancestral residence into a heritage hotel, maintaining its tradition alongside contemporary amenities.

Unique Selling Points

  • ●**Exclusively Veg:** The region's sole heritage property offering 100% vegetarian dining.
  • ●**Alcohol-Free:** The hotel upholds a no-alcohol policy, emphasizing a welcoming and family-oriented environment.
  • ●**Fresh Picks:** A variety of vegetables are organically grown on-site, providing guests with healthy and flavorful meals.

A Living Legacy

The Chandel family transformed their ancestral fort into a heritage hotel, allowing the legacy of Ramgarh to live on not just in its walls, but through the experiences of modern-day travelers.

The fort's towering wooden gate, honored in the Limca Book of Records as India's tallest, once stood as a symbol of defense and strength. Today, it warmly greets visitors with its historic grandeur.
