Explore the Manchester of India, the biggest city in Uttar Pradesh.

Manchester of India

Kanpur, a prominent industrial city in India, was established by the Hindu Chandel King of Sachendi. Located along the river Ganga, the city has a rich historical, religious, and commercial heritage. Serving as the largest city in Uttar Pradesh, Kanpur is a major center for education, art, and literature.

Major Leather & Textile Center
Home to reputed IIT Kanpur

Relevance of the Name

The city originally known as Kanhiyapur, after Lord Krishna, evolved into Kanhapur and then Kanpur. It is also linked to Karna, a well-known figure from the Mahabharat.

Iconic Places to Visit

Green Park Stadium

This illuminated stadium is a cricket lover's dream, with a seating capacity of 45,000 and is the sole multi-purpose venue in Uttar Pradesh for hosting Test and One Day matches.

Jajmau (Siddhapuri)

This historic city boasts the Siddhnath and Siddha Devi temples, as well as the tomb of sufi saint Makhdam Shah Ala-ul-Haq commissioned by Firoz Shah Tughlaq.

Massacre Ghat

Previously known as Satti Chaura Ghat, this location rose to prominence during the 1857 uprising and was later renamed Nana Rao Ghat in honor of Nana Sahib, the leader of the revolt, before ultimately becoming known as Massacre Ghat.

Allen Forest Zoo

Founded in 1971, this zoo and botanical garden is home to a diverse range of indigenous and exotic animals and plants.

Parks, Gardens & Worship

  • Phool Bagh: Ganesh Udyan, also called as, is the venue for yearly flower exhibits and public gatherings held at the K.E.M. Hall.

  • Moti Jheel: A well-liked picnic destination with lovely recreational areas and a playground for children.

  • Spiritual Landmarks: Explore the Shri Radhakrishna Temple (J.K. Temple), the Jain Glass Temple, and the ancient Bhitargaon Temple.

Traveler Insights

Shopping

Kanpur is famous worldwide for its top-notch leather and textile products.

Kamla Tower

Modeled after the London Tower and Big Ben, it functions as the headquarters for the JK Singhania Business family.

Education Hub

Kanpur is home to IIT Kanpur, Harcourt Butler Technological Institute (HBTI), and GSVM Medical
