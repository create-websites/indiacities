Ladakh Tour

Land of High Passes, Ancient Monasteries, and Eternal Snow.

The Land and People

Ladakh, located in the northernmost part of Jammu and Kashmir, is a stunning valley characterized by towering mountains and snow-covered roads. The inhabitants of this region are renowned for their humble nature and trace their heritage back to Indo-Aryan and Tibetan roots.

The region consists of two districts: Leh, the largest district spanning 45,110 Sq Km, and Kargil, which became globally recognized following Operation Vijay in 1999.

Geography & Climate

Boundaries: Bounded by the Himalayas, Karakoram, Ladakh, and Zanskar ranges.

Altitude: Approximately 9,000 feet above sea level.

Summer Max: 27°C

Winter Min: -20°C

Monasteries (Gompas)

Ladakh is renowned for its beautifully constructed Buddhist monasteries perched on remote hills, known for their aesthetic appeal. mane walls (votive stones with inscribed prayers) and Chortens (semi-religious shrines).

Major Monasteries:

Hemis, Thiksay, Alchi, Lamayuru, Spituk, Phyang, Rizong, Likir,

Palaces and Citadels

Leh Palace

Referred to as 'Lhachen Palkhar,' this nine-story building was constructed in 1600. It provides stunning views of Leh and is presently undergoing renovation by the Archaeological Survey of India.

Stok Palace

The residence of the royal family from the 19th century features a museum displaying 400-year-old thangkas, crown jewels, royal attire, and religious artifacts.

Shey Palace & Monastery

Constructed in the 1600s as a former summer getaway, this site now holds a grand copper-gilded figure of Buddha Shakyamuni.

Basgo Rabtan

At the top of the hill sits a castle adorned with striking statues of the Maitreya Buddha: one towering two stories high and crafted from copper, while another stands three stories tall and fashioned from clay.

Wildlife & Adventure

Unique Wildlife

Ladakh is the habitat of unique creatures such as gazelles, ibex, ammon (wild sheep), and shapo.

Trekking

Trekkers can explore the rugged mountainscape and discover a multitude of captivating routes to experience the raw beauty of nature up close.

Trip Info

Districts

Must See

Monasteries, Leh Palace, and the copper Buddha at Shey.

Experience

High-altitude trekking through the Himalayas and Karakoram.
