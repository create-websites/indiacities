Kanha National Park

Explore the heart of India's top tiger reserve and immerse yourself in the wild.

Park Overview

Situated in the Banjar and Halon Valleys within the Mandla and Balaghat districts, Kanha National Park Known as a top destination in Madhya Pradesh, this location is famous for its abundant Royal Bengal Tigers, leopards, sloth bears, and the rare Barasingha.

Area: 1,945 sq.km
Altitude: 600-900 meters
Best Time: April-June & Nov-Jan
Climate: 2°C to 43°C

History & Evolution

The name Kanha is derived either from Kanhar, the local term for clayey soil, or from KanvaA sacred individual resided in the woods. The park was established on June 1, 1953, stemming from the Banjar Valley and Hallon Valley sanctuaries.

Initially designated as a reserve forest in 1879, the boundaries of the forest were gradually extended to include the Mukki Valley in 1970 and the Hallon Valley in 1974.

Must-Visit Spots

Bamni Dadar

Commonly known as the Sunset PointThis spot provides a stunning vantage point to watch the sun set and is perfect for observing sambar, barking deer, and the four-horned antelope.

Kanha Museum

Situated within the park, the museum offers valuable information on the reserve's topography and varied biological features.

Biodiversity

Mammals

The park is home to around 22 different species of mammals, with notable sightings including:

  • Tiger
  • Gaur
  • Sloth Bear
  • Swamp Deer
  • Hanuman Langur
  • Blackbuck

Avifauna

Home to 175-200 documented bird species, this destination is a paradise for bird enthusiasts. Keep an eye out for the Purple Heron, Shikra, and White-eyed Buzzard.

Explore Nearby

Kawardha Palace

A magnificent structure made of Italian marble, constructed from 1936 to 1939 and surrounded by 11 acres of beautiful gardens.

Nagpur (Orange City)

This renowned city, situated 260 kilometers from here, is well-known for its citrus fruit and acts as a key transportation center.

Travel Guide

By Air

The closest airport to Nagpur is 266 km away and offers domestic flights from Mumbai.

By Rail

Jabalpur (169 km) serves as the most convenient railhead.

By Road

Easily accessible from Jabalpur (175 km), Raipur (219 km), and Nagpur (266 km).

Internal Distances

  • Kanha-Mukki: 32 km
  • Kanha-Katia: 4 km
