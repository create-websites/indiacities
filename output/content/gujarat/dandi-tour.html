Dandi: Landmark of Freedom

The Salt Satyagraha's historic shore, where Gandhiji embarked on his legendary 241-mile journey.

Introduction

Dandi is located along the coast, only 5 kilometers away from the center of SuratAlthough it is well-known for its salt production, the main reason for its fame is its historical significance.

In March 1930, Gandhiji performed the famous ‘Dandi March Salt Satyagraha’ Gandhiji and his 78 followers marched around 241 miles from Sabarmati Ashram to this coastline to challenge the British Government's salt laws, igniting a movement that ultimately led to India's freedom.

History & Heritage

At the time of the Dandi March, the village was without essential infrastructure such as roads, water, and electricity. Now, the Government has maintained the bungalow where Gandhiji resided, transforming it into a historical site. exhibition of Dandi March photographs.

The Prayer Monument

A monument is located beneath a towering banyan tree where Gandhiji conducted interfaith prayer gatherings. The inauguration was led by Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru in 1961.

Hajani Bibi ni Dargah

Dandi is a revered pilgrimage destination for the Dawoodi Bohra community.

Environment & Salt Production

Dandi sits on the Arabian Sea shore The village, located along the Purna River, would often be surrounded by high tides, resembling an island. This would trap water in the low-lying lands, allowing it to evaporate and naturally produce salt.

Yet, the recurring tides frequently inundated homes and eroded farmland. In order to safeguard the village, a Soil Dam The construction was intended to prevent water infiltration, leading to the halt of salt production.

The modern Dandi village has transformed into a fully-equipped community, complete with electricity, schools, hostels, and running tap water in each home.

Etymology & Demographics

What's in a Name?

  • Diva Dandi: Talking about a former lighthouse that used to be located on the coast.
  • Dando: A Gujarati term describing submerged land, mirroring Dandi's island-like layout.

Population

In 1930, the village's population was estimated to be just 460 people, but now it stands at around 1260 residents.
