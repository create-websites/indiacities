Panipat: The Turning Point of History

An antiquated city situated along the Grand Trunk Road, where three crucial battles determined the destiny of India.

Historical Significance

Panipat, historically known as PanduprashthaAccording to the Mahabharata, the Pandavas founded , a city situated on the Grand Trunk Road Located 90 km from Delhi and 135 km from Chandigarh, (Shershah Suri Marg) is a city known for being the site of three pivotal battles that shaped Indian history.

First Battle: Start of Mughal Rule
Second Battle: Mughal Power Restored
Third Battle: Rise of British Supremacy
Situated on the banks of River Yamuna

Major Attractions

Historical Monuments

  • Panipat Battle Museum: Dedicated to the historic conflicts.
  • Tomb of Ibrahim Lodhi: The memorial of the Lodi dynasty ruler.
  • Kabuli Bagh: A beautiful mosque and garden built by Babur.
  • Hemu’s Samadhi-sthal: Commemorating the Hindu king Hemu.

Scenic & Modern Sites

  • Kala Amb: A site of natural beauty known as the 'Black Mango'.
  • Devi Mandir: A significant local temple.
  • Mittal Mega Mall: A hub for modern shopping and entertainment.
  • Angel Prime Mall: Another premier retail destination in the city.

Industrial Powerhouse

Panipat is a massive industrial centreknown worldwide for its exceptional textile and carpet manufacturing. The company is a top provider of blankets for the Indian military and is celebrated for its long-standing tradition of excellence. 'Achar Pachranga' pickle.

Textiles: Largest producer of shoddy yarn and rags.
Bakery: The origin of the famous Kwalitys bakery.

Travel Info

Panipat Expressway

A six-lane elevated expressway spanning 10 kilometers designed to alleviate congestion on the Delhi-Amritsar road.

Neighboring Districts

Karnal, Jind, and Sonepat.

Nearby Excursion

Samalkha: A nearby town famous for its industrial foundry.
