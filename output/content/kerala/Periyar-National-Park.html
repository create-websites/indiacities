Periyar National Park

A sanctuary for tigers and elephants nestled in the lush Western Ghats.

Quick Facts

Location: Idukki District, Kerala
Area: 778 sq km
Altitude: 914m - 1830m
Best Time: October to June
Climate: Summer (21°C-31°C) | Winter (19°C-21°C)

History & Overview

Located in the Western Ghats, Periyar National Park and Tiger Reserve is a source of pride for Kerala, with a stunning lake at its center created by a dam constructed in 1895.

The reservoir winds through the wooded hills, offering a constant water source for wildlife. Although it is a Tiger Reserve, visitors mainly come to see Indian elephants frolicking and bathing along the lake's shore.

Wildlife & Sightseeing

Majestic Mammals

Indian Elephants, Gaur, Wild Pigs, Sambar, Barking Deer, and Mouse Deer can be spotted here. Occasional sightings of the Indian Wild Dog (Dhole) and around 40 Tigers also occur.

Primates & Rare Species

The rare Lion-tailed Macaque, Nilgiri Langur, Common Langur, and Bonnet Macaque all call this place home, along with the elusive Nilgiri Tahr.

Lake Boat Cruises

Experience the sanctuary in the best possible way with a morning cruise at 7:00 AM, where you can catch sight of elephant families and wild boar by the water's edge.

Explore the Surroundings

Kumily (4 km)

An expanding spice trade hub with top-notch hotels and resorts, serving as the primary entry point for tourists to Periyar.

Cardamom Hills

Conveniently reachable from Periyar and Kumily by jeep or taxi rides, this stunning scenery is ideal for group outings.

Nearest airports are Kochi (200 km) and Madurai (140 km).

By Rail

Travel Tip
