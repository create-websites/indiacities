Bhiwani: Small Kashi

A spiritual refuge and the heart of Indian boxing located in Haryana.

Historical Legacy

town founded by Rajput King Neem in honor of his wife, Bhani, Bhiwani was established on December 22, 1972. "City of Temples" and "Small Kashi" India is home to more than 300 temples and has remained a key hub of commerce since the Mughal era.

300+ Temples across the city
Prominent Mughal-era Commerce Center
Home to three Haryana Chief Ministers
Cradle of Freedom Fighters

Major Attractions

Spiritual Landmarks

  • Sri Hari Har Temple: A revered city landmark.
  • Devsar Dhaam: Home to the Maa Durga Temple.
  • Jain Temple: Located in Ranila Village.
  • Baba Jyotram Temple: Situated in Badala Village.

Boxing Powerhouse

Established by legendary boxer Late Capt. Hawa Singh, the Bhiwani Boxing Club India's boxing powerhouse, the primary hub for Indian boxing, has solidified its position as the top center for the sport.

Freedom Heritage

The city produced numerous freedom fighters, including Neki Sharma (honored with the title Haryana Kesri), who was instrumental in the 1922 Bardoli Satyagraha.

Education Hub

Home to the Technological Institute of Textile and Sciences sponsored by the Birla Group, the Haryana Shiksha Board, and various engineering and degree colleges.

Regional Geography

The district comprises 442 villages, with prominent towns including:

Charkhi
Loharu
Bewani Khera
Dadri
Tosham
