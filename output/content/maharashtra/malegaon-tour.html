Malegaon City Trip

A historical textile hub located where the Mousam and Girna rivers meet.

Location and History

Malegaon, also known as Taluka Loha, is located in the Nasik district of Maharashtra at the meeting point of the Mousam and Girna riversIt is the district's second largest city following Nasik and has become a notable sanctuary for North Indian Muslims since the 1857 revolt, continuing to serve as a cultural center for Muslim communities.

57 km from Nanded
Famous for Textile & Power Loom industries
Base for the Maratha film industry
Home to ~300 Mosques

Major Attractions

Malegaon Fort

Built by a Maratha ruler in the 18th century along the Mousam river, this structure stands out with a shrine atop and a temple below, showcasing the area's diverse past.

Urdu Library

An established institution with a 60-year history, boasting an extensive collection of works by celebrated Urdu authors spanning literature, history, health, and education.

Jamiatus Swalehaat

The premier Islamic educational institution for women in India, known worldwide for its esteemed reputation that draws students from across the globe.

Indian Muslim Heritage

Visit the Shahi Masjid, the oldest mosque in the city, or the 40-acre largest cemetery in India.

Industry and Media

Malegaon has gained national attention through the documentary Supermen of Malegaon, highlighting the town's vibrant film-loving culture.

Textile Hub: Visiting the bustling power loom workshops is a favorite pastime among tourists.
Film Studios: The film sets and studios of the Maratha film industry are popular attractions for tourists.
