Alwar: The Tiger Gateway

A historic Rajput state surrounded by the ancient Aravalli hills.

Historic Majesty

The ancient state of Mewat, Alwar, is adorned with a grand fort that has stood witness to numerous battles over its tumultuous past, and is renowned in the histories. Mahabharata Matsya, the kingdom that sheltered the Pandavas during their exile.

Northeastern Rajasthan, India
Best Season: September to February
Hindi, English, Rajasthani
STD Code: 0144

Forts & Palaces

The Alwar Fort

Constructed before the Mughal Empire, this fort stands at an impressive altitude of 595 meters above sea level and is renowned for never being besieged. The fort includes Salim Mahal, where Jahangir resided during his exile.

Vinay Vilas Mahal

A marvel from the 18th century that combines Rajput and Mughal architectural influences, featuring government offices on the ground floor and a well-maintained museum on the upper level.

Local Attractions

  • Government Museum: Features rare manuscripts in Persian, Arabic, and Sanskrit, including the Gulistan and the Waqiat-I-Babri.

  • Purjan Vihar (Company Garden): A beautiful garden, 'Shimla', was created in 1868 as a tranquil sanctuary.

  • Culinary Specialty: Don't miss the Palang Torh Alwar Milk Cake, a dessert rumored to be so scrumptious it may cause one to break their bed if they overeat.
