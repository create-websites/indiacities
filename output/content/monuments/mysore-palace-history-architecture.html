The Phoenix Palace

Mysore Palace's tale is one of ruin and rebirth, with each version symbolizing the Wadiyar dynasty's lasting strength. Explore the timeline to witness its transformation.

Indo-Saracenic Marvel

Architect Henry Irwin skillfully fused Hindu, Mughal, Rajput, and Gothic elements, crafting a distinct design that reflected Mysore's modern and diverse vision.

🏰

Granite & Marble

A three-level building of smooth grey granite, topped with vibrant pink marble domes, offers a bold visual contrast.

🏹

Expansive Arches

The majestic exterior showcases seven broad arches resting on ornate pillars, forming a harmonious design.

👑

Gilded Central Dome

The 44-meter tower features a gold-plated dome, embodying supreme royal power.

🐘

Gajalakshmi Sculpture

Atop the central arch rests a statue of the wealth goddess, bestowing celestial blessings on the realm.

Halls of Splendour

The palace's interior showcases a rich blend of global artistry, featuring Scottish stained glass, English tiles, and exquisite inlay work from Agra, reflecting a monarchy with worldwide ties.

The Spectacle of Dasara

The palace serves as the centerpiece of Mysore Dasara, a 400-year-old, 10-day festival that draws millions and lights up the city.

Jamboo Savari: The Grand Procession

The festival concludes with a grand elephant parade, featuring the lead elephant bearing the 750kg Golden Howdah that enshrines Goddess Chamundeshwari's idol, merging regal heritage with communal festivity.

A Global Destination

The Mysore Palace, a top attraction in India, is overseen by the Mysore Palace Board, balancing heritage conservation with the challenges of heavy tourism.

Visitor Footfall (2023-2025)

Even with a steep fee increase for foreign visitors, tourist numbers have rebounded strongly post-pandemic, highlighting the palace's enduring and rising allure.
