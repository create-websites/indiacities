The Siege of Aligarh, 1803

A crucial and fierce clash of the Second Anglo-Maratha War. The British aimed to dismantle Daulatrao Scindia's French-trained forces, with the fort as their stronghold. The battle showcased the fort's resilience, delivering heavy losses to the assailants.

The Belligerents

British East India Company

Led by the fierce General Lord Gerard Lake, the elite 76th Regiment of Foot spearheaded the attack.

Maratha Confederacy

The garrison, led by French mercenary Colonel Pedron and overseen by Pierre Perron, had troops trained and structured in European fashion.

A Fortress of Death

The French-crafted defenses were both clever and ruthless, featuring 14 trenches, spiked barriers (*chevaux-de-frise*), and even released tigers and lions.

The Bloody Price of Victory

The future Duke of Wellington deemed it 'Among the most remarkable achievements of the British in Northern India.'

The Living Fort

Today, the fort has evolved into a vibrant hub for science, nature, and culture, guided by Aligarh Muslim University's stewardship.

Botanical Garden

The AMU Department of Botany manages the grounds, showcasing diverse flora that transformed a once-military zone into a peaceful hub for learning and nature.

Ecological Refuge

The gardens offer a tranquil refuge for birds such as Indian Grey Hornbills, peacocks, and diverse wildlife, serving as a vital hub for urban ornithological studies.

Folklore & Identity

Locally dubbed 'Bonay Chor ka Kila' (Fort of the Dwarf Thief), it draws from a legendary bandit's tale, offering a vibrant contrast to its state-sanctioned history.

Visitor Guide

Visiting soon? Here's what to know before you arrive. Keep in mind, this isn’t just a landmark it’s a vibrant university campus.

Permission Required

IMPORTANT: Authorization from the AMU Botany Department is required to access the fort grounds. This is mandatory.

Entry & Timings

Admission is free. Regular hours run 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM, but may vary per university rules.

How to Reach

Situated on GT Road, ~5 km from Aligarh Junction. Easy access to local transport.

Best Time to Visit

The cooler months, November through March, bring ideal weather for enjoying the grounds.
