Patiala

A fusion of Rajput, Mughal, and Punjabi traditions created a majestic tapestry.

The Land of Baba Ala Singh

Patiala, known as the 'Pati' of Baba Ala Singh, is a lively city in Punjab that blends tradition and modernity, renowned worldwide for its unique cultural symbols:

Patiala Turban
Patiala Peg
Traditional Jutti
Paranda & Salwaar

Royal Palaces & Forts

Qila Mubarak

This complex, a fusion of late Mughal and Rajasthani architecture, sits on 10 acres within the city and includes the main palace (Qila Androon), guesthouse (Ran Baas), and grand Darbar Hall.

The nearby market is renowned for its exquisite jewelry, handcrafted textiles, and vibrant shoes.

Sheesh Mahal and Museum

Commissioned by Maharaja Narendra Singh, a passionate supporter of fine arts, the museum showcases exquisite paintings in the traditional Rajasthani style, illustrating stories from mythology, legends, and the Raga-Ragni series.

Moti Bagh Palace

The palace showcases stunning Rajasthan-style jharokas and chhatris, surrounded by lush gardens, terraces, and water channels.

Spiritual Heritage

Gurudwara Dukhniwaran Sahib

This elevated site was constructed on land gifted by the villagers of Lehal to honor the visit of Guru Teg Bahadur. According to local folklore, praying here brings relief from suffering (Dukhniwaran).

How to Reach

By Air

Chandigarh Airport (IXC): 54 km from the city center.

Sahnewal Airport (LUH): 73 km from Patiala.

By Rail

Patiala Station is located along the Ambala-Bathinda railway line, with the closest major railheads Ambala Cantonment (60 km) and Rajpura Junction.

By Road

Located just off National Highway 1. Approximately 250 km from Delhi.
