Greater Noida

A bustling commercial center and impressive industrial masterpiece within the National Capital Region.

Overview

Greater Noida, situated in the southeast of New Delhi, is an urban hub and commercial center. It is a sub-city falling under the National Capital Region (NCR) and is renowned as one of Asia's biggest industrial townships.

The city is a prominent center for international IT corporations and well-known companies. It has a sub-tropical climate common to the area and offers modern amenities like Gautam Buddha University, Jaypee Greens golf course, and Knowledge Park.

Premier Venues & Marts

India Expo Centre

This venue in India is known as the top choice for hosting events, boasting a 21,000 square meter space with six fully air-conditioned halls, top-notch amenities, and a maximum capacity of 5,000 guests.

Exposition Mart

India's inaugural modern mart dedicated to Cottage and Handicraft industries, inspired by the Shanghai Mart, aims to boost exports of handlooms, carpets, and jute.

Sports & Future Projects

Formula 1 Track

Greater Noida has established itself as a top racing destination with a world-class 5.5 km track designed by Hermann Tilke, covering 4,000 hectares and accommodating 200,000 spectators.

Night Safari

An upcoming zoological garden inspired by renowned safaris in China and Singapore, with a focus on promoting environmental consciousness and boosting tourism.

Taj International Airport

An international hub is being developed to support cargo and tourism, with direct access to New Delhi via the Taj Expressway for easy transportation.

Recreation

City Park

The park boasts a stunningly landscaped area filled with rare plants, elegant fountains, and expertly manicured gardens. One standout feature is the enchanting dolphin fish pond, sure to captivate visitors of all ages.
