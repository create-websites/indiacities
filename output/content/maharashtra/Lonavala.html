Lonavala City Trip

The Gem of the Sahyadri Range, tucked away in the lush hills of the Western Ghats.

Hill Station Overview

Lonavala, a top hill station in Maharashtra, is nestled among the serene Western Ghats. Just 106 km from Mumbai, it is a favored destination for those seeking a break from the city and also serves as a starting point for visiting historic Buddhist caves carved into rock.

State: Maharashtra
Altitude: 625 meters
Best Time: October to May
Marathi, Hindi, English

History

The region served as a crucial Buddhist hub since the 2nd century BC, despite being geographically cut off by the challenging Sahyadri landscape, it held great importance to the Maratha Empire.

  • Maratha Era: Ruled first by Chattrapati Shivaji and later by the Peshwas.
  • British Era: Conquered by the British following their victory over the Peshwa rulers.

Major Attractions

Ancient Rock-Cut Caves

The Karla and Bhaja caves, situated 11 km away from Lonavala, are believed to have been constructed in the 2nd century BC, showcasing some of the oldest Hinayana Buddhist temple art in India.

Scenic Water Bodies

Lonavala is encircled by several charming lakes such as Tugauli, Lonavala, and Bhushi, as well as cascading waterfalls.

Historic Forts

The area is speckled with historic Maratha forts like Rajmachi, Tunga, Lohagad, Visapur, Tikona, and Kore

Neighboring Hill Stations

Khandala, a quaint hill station, is situated a mere 5 kilometers away from Lonavala, providing more picturesque views.

Climate & Best Time

Summer: Reaches up to 36°C

Winter: Reaches up to 12°C

Rainfall: High annual rainfall of 1800 mm

Best Time: October to May for ideal weather

How to Reach

By Air

The nearest airport is at Pune, located 66 km away.

By Rail

Lonavala is a stop along the Central Railway's Mumbai-Pune line.

By Road

Situated on the Mumbai-Pune Highway, 104 kilometers away from Mumbai and 66 kilometers away from Pune.

Quick Facts

Area: 30 sq.km
STD Code: 02114
