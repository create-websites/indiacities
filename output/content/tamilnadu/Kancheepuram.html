Kancheepuram Tour

The City of a Thousand Temples and World-Famous Silk.

The Holy City of Kanchi

Kancheepuram is renowned as a sacred destination for Hindus, known for its exquisite hand-woven silk sarees and ancient culture that spans centuries.

Places of Interest

Kailasanathar Temple

Constructed in the iconic Pallava architectural style, renowned for its intricate carvings and distinctive pyramidal tower.

Ekambareswarar Temple

Includes a Raja Gopuram standing at 57 meters tall and a mango tree that has been around

Kanchi Kamakshi Temple

A lovely temple honoring the goddess Kamakshi, who embodies Parvati, Lakshmi, and Saraswati.

Handloom Industry

Discover the origins of authentic mulberry silk sarees, prized for their sheen and lasting quality.
