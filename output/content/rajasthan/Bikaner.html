Bikaner: The Royal Desert

A grand stop along the historic Silk Road nestled in the Thar Desert.

A Legacy of Red Sandstone

Founded in 1486 A.D. by Rao BikajiBikaner, a regal city surrounded by a massive fort wall spanning over 5 km, boasts distinctive red sandstone buildings and vast sand dunes, attracting both nature enthusiasts and history buffs.

Rajasthan, India
Best Season: October to March
Hindi, Urdu, Rajasthani
STD Code: 0145

Top Attractions

Camel Breeding Farm

Bikaner's unique facility is the sole of its kind in Asia, showcasing the city's historical importance as a key stop along the Silk Route for camel caravans.

Junagarh Fort

The fort area is renowned for its stunning architecture and vibrant murals, serving as a key landmark and bustling shopping center.

Vibrant Fairs & Festivals

Camel Festival

Occurs annually in January under the full moon, showcasing camel races, traditional dances, and the vibrant atmosphere characteristic of Rajasthan.

Karni Mata Fair

Occurs biannually in March/April and September/October at the desert citadel of Deshnok to pay tribute to the goddess Karni Mata.

Kite Festival

The Teej Festival, held in April/May, celebrates the city's founding day.

Kapil Muni Fair

An annual event at Kolayat Lake in November, featuring a sacred bath and a famous cattle fair.

  • Lightweight Nokha Quilts

Traveler's Guide

By Air

Nal Airport (17km) handles civilian flights Nov-Mar. Nearest International: Jaipur/Jodhpur.

By Rail

Easily accessible from Delhi (12 hours), Mumbai, and Kolkata. The station is located in the heart of the city.

What to Wear

In summer, opt for loose cottons and in winter, choose light woolens. Make sure to wear comfortable shoes for walking.

Climate

Summer: 28°C - 41.8°C

Winter: 5°C - 23.2°C
