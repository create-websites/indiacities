Haldwani Tour

Entryway to Kumaon - An idyllic spot tucked away in the Nainital region.

Location and History

Haldwani, often referred to as the 'gateway of Kumaon,' is the third most populous city in Uttarakhand. Situated in the Nainital district alongside its twin city Kathgodam, it was founded in the 19th century and has grown into a significant center for administration and commerce.

Places of Interest

Haldwani provides a range of attractions for both nature enthusiasts and spiritual seekers.

  • Gaula Barrage: An ideal spot for picnics by the scenic Gaula River, great for hiking and camping.
  • Hedakhan Ashram: A serene sanctuary known for its calming presence and picturesque river vistas.
  • Sat Tal: Situated 22 kilometers from here, this special blend of seven freshwater lakes is encircled by thick oak and pine woodlands.
  • Jim Corbett National Park: Located 55 km from Haldwani, India's oldest national park is home to approximately 448 species of flora and fauna.
