Dalhousie Tour

Discover the peaceful allure and historic grandeur of Himachal's top hill station.

Hill Station Profile

Area: 13 sq km
Altitude: 6678 feet above sea level
Best Time: Throughout the year
STD Code: (91) - 1899

History of Dalhousie

Lord Dalhousie, the former British Governor General, once considered Dalhousie his dream destination due to its unique secluded charm and laid-back lifestyle, setting it apart from similar counterparts like Shimla and Mussoorie.

The hill station provides a serene environment against a stunning backdrop of snow-capped mountains. Visitors can discover numerous colonial buildings and get away from the hustle and bustle of urban living in this peaceful Himachal Pradesh retreat.

Major Sightseeing

Subhash Baodi

A well-liked picnic area located at an elevation of 2085 meters, providing beautiful surroundings, mild weather, and stunning vistas of faraway snow-capped mountains.

Kalatope

Situated at an altitude of 2438 meters, this site includes a stunning palace, a forest guest house, and a wildlife sanctuary inhabited by ibex, deer, bears, and leopards.

Satdhara

Referred to as the 'Seven Streams,' these springs are thought to have healing qualities for treating chronic illnesses, and can be found en route to Panjpulla.

St. Andrew's Church

Constructed in 1903 in the Scottish architectural style, this church, also known as the Church of Scotland, is well-maintained and located near the bus stand.

Popular Getaway: Khajjiar

Situated 27 km from Dalhousie, Khajjiar Nestled in the Himalayan foothills, this charming village, once a British summer retreat, is renowned for its refreshing climate and expansive green pastures.

Guests have the option to participate in a variety of adventure activities or simply relax in the peaceful setting of this secluded destination.

How to Reach

By Air

Gaggal Airport near Kangra is the most convenient option for reaching Dalhousie, with taxis and buses readily available for transportation.

By Rail

Pathankot is the closest railway station, with connections to major cities such as Delhi through the Jammu Mail and Shalimar Express.

By Road

Both luxury and standard buses run from nearby cities such as Pathankot, Ambala, and Ludhiana.

Heritage Churches

Other significant colonial landmarks in the vicinity include St. Patrick's, St. Francis, and St. John's Churches.
