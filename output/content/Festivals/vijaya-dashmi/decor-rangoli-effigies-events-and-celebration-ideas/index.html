Decor & celebrations

Rangoli and home decoration, eco-friendly effigies, and ideas for community, office and apartment celebrations.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • Dussehra Decoration Ideas for Home Simple Dussehra home decoration means a marigold and mango-leaf toran at the door, a rangoli at the entrance, and a puja corner with a lamp and the books or tools you will honor.
  • Traditional Dussehra Rangoli Designs Traditional Dussehra rangoli motifs include a bow and arrow, Ravana's ten heads, a lotus or kalash, Durga's trident, Shami leaves, diyas and the words 'Shubh Dussehra' or 'Vijayadashami'.
  • Easy Vijayadashami Rangoli Designs for Beginners Easy Vijayadashami rangoli for beginners: a flower-petal circle, a dot-grid kolam, a bow-and-arrow outline filled with color, a diya with petals, or stencils.
  • Dussehra Door and Entrance Decoration Ideas For a Dussehra entrance, hang a marigold and mango-leaf toran, add a rangoli or kolam at the threshold, place two diyas or LED lamps on either side and add a small Shami or Apta leaf bunch.
  • Dussehra Flower Decoration Ideas Dussehra flower decoration usually means marigold garlands and torans, plus Aparajita (blue butterfly pea) for Durga.
  • Dussehra Puja Room Decoration Ideas To decorate a puja room for Dussehra, clean and redrape the altar in red or yellow cloth and frame it with a marigold garland.
  • Ravana Effigy Making Ideas for Community Events For a community Ravana effigy, build a frame of bamboo tied with jute, cover it with paper and cloth, and make the ten heads light and well braced.
  • Eco-Friendly Ravana Effigy Ideas Eco-friendly Ravana effigies use bamboo, newspaper, cloth, jute, natural colors and paper-pulp heads, with no plastic, thermocol or chemical paint and few or no firecrackers.
  • How to Organize a Community Dussehra Celebration To organize a community Dussehra, form a small committee six to eight weeks ahead, fix the venue and permits (fire, police, noise), and set a budget.
  • Dussehra Event Planning Checklist A Dussehra event checklist covers venue booking, permits (fire, police, municipality, noise), insurance, budget, program and performers, sound and lighting, the effigy and its safety perimeter, food vendors, first aid, crowd control, accessibility, signage, waste and post-event cleanup, with a timeline from eight weeks out.
  • How to Organize a Small Ramlila in a Community A small community Ramlila can be done in three to five short episodes or one 90-minute show.
  • Dussehra Stage Decoration Ideas Dussehra stage ideas: a painted backdrop of Ayodhya or Lanka, marigold and fabric drapes, a temple-arch frame, a bow-and-arrow centerpiece and warm wash lighting.
  • Dussehra Photo Booth and Backdrop Ideas Dussehra photo booth ideas: a marigold-curtain backdrop, a cut-out 'Ravana' with face holes, a Ramlila-crown prop table, a giant bow-and-arrow frame and a 'Shubh Vijayadashami' neon or LED sign.
  • Dussehra Party and Cultural Program Ideas A Dussehra party or cultural program can run 90 to 120 minutes: welcome and short puja, a kids' Ramayana skit, a dance segment (garba, folk or classical), a quiz and a symbolic 'victory' moment, then dinner.
  • Dussehra Games and Activities for Families Dussehra games for families include 'hit the Ravana' with soft balls, a Ramayana quiz, Ramayana dumb charades, a treasure hunt for Sita's jewels, ring toss on a ten-headed board and a bow-and-arrow target with suction darts.
  • Dussehra Celebration Ideas for Apartment Communities For apartment communities, a Dussehra evening in the clubhouse or podium works well: a kids' Ramlila, a rangoli contest, a potluck or food stalls, Ayudha Puja for residents' vehicles and a small eco-effigy or symbolic show.
  • Dussehra Office Celebration Ideas Office Dussehra ideas: a short Ayudha Puja for laptops and equipment, an inclusive team lunch, a Ramayana or India-festivals quiz, a desk-decoration or rangoli contest and an optional ethnic-wear day.
  • Eco-Friendly Dussehra Celebration Ideas An eco-friendly Dussehra means a smaller or non-burning effigy made of bamboo and paper, few or no firecrackers, and flower, rice-flour or natural-color rangoli.
  • How to Celebrate Dussehra Without Burning an Effigy You can celebrate Dussehra without burning an effigy: write ten bad habits on Ravana's paper heads and tear or recycle them, pop balloons on a cut-out, or stage a puppet Ramlila.
  • Dussehra Celebration Ideas for Families Living Abroad Families abroad can celebrate Dussehra with a home puja, an online or local temple Ramlila, a paper Ravana for children, and a Shami or Apta-style leaf exchange using any local leaf.
