Travel & fairs

Where to see Mysuru Dasara, Kullu Dussehra, Ramnagar Ramlila and Bastar, with trip planning, etiquette and photography tips.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • Best Places in India to Experience Dussehra The best places to experience Dussehra are Mysuru for the Jamboo Savari and palace lights, Kullu for its week-long gathering of deities and Varanasi for the Ramnagar Ramlila.
  • Dussehra in Mysuru: Complete Travel Guide Plan Mysuru Dasara around the last weekend: the palace lights up every evening, and the Jamboo Savari and Bannimantap torchlight parade fall on Vijayadashami.
  • Kullu Dussehra: Complete Travel and Festival Guide Kullu Dussehra starts on Vijayadashami and runs seven days at Dhalpur Maidan.
  • Ramnagar Ramlila in Varanasi: Visitor Guide The Ramnagar Ramlila runs about a month across Ramnagar, on the Ganga's east bank opposite Varanasi.
  • Dussehra in Delhi: Best Ramlila and Effigy-Burning Experiences Delhi's best-known Dussehra events are the Ramlilas at Ramlila Maidan and the Lav Kush Ramlila near the Red Fort, plus large effigy burnings in many neighborhoods.
  • Kota Dussehra Mela: Visitor Guide The Kota Dussehra Mela runs for several weeks around Dussehra on the city's Dussehra grounds, with trade fairs, rides, food, folk performances and evening concerts.
  • Dussehra in Ayodhya: Travel and Festival Guide For Dussehra in Ayodhya, plan two or three days for Ramlila performances, the Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi and the Saryu ghats at sunset.
  • Dussehra in Bastar: Tribal Traditions and Cultural Guide Bastar Dussehra in Chhattisgarh lasts 75 days, among India's longest festivals, and honors the goddess Danteshwari rather than Rama.
  • How to Plan a Dussehra Trip to North India A north India Dussehra trip can combine Delhi's Ramlilas, Varanasi's Ramnagar Ramlila and Ayodhya, or a mountain detour to Kullu, which begins on Vijayadashami itself.
  • How to Plan a Dussehra Trip to Karnataka For a Karnataka Dasara trip, spend three or four days in Mysuru for the palace lights, Dasara exhibition and Vijayadashami Jamboo Savari, with a half day at Srirangapatna.
  • How to Attend a Ramlila Respectfully: Visitor Etiquette At a Ramlila, arrive early, dress modestly, remove shoes where a stage area is sacred and sit where volunteers direct you.
  • Dussehra Fair Guide: What to Expect at a Mela A Dussehra mela usually has food stalls (jalebi, chaat, kulfi), rides and Ferris wheels, toy and craft stalls, cultural stages and, at the end, an effigy burning.
  • Dussehra Travel with Kids: Tips and Family Activities With kids at Dussehra, choose an earlier Ramlila night over the main effigy evening, and bring ear protection and an ID card with your number.
  • Dussehra Photography Guide: Ramlila, Processions and Effigies For Dussehra photography, shoot Ramlila in the hour before showtime when actors are in costume, use a fast lens (f/1.8 to f/2.8) at ISO 1600 to 3200 for stage light, and scout effigy angles in daylight.
  • How to Photograph Fireworks and Ravana Effigy Burning Safely To photograph Ravana Dahan safely, stay behind the barriers upwind.
  • Best Dussehra Cultural Experiences for International Visitors The best Dussehra experiences for international visitors are Mysuru's Jamboo Savari and palace lights, Kolkata's Durga Puja pandals and Bijoya, the Ramnagar Ramlila, Kullu's procession of deities, and a neighborhood Ramlila and mela in Delhi.
  • Dussehra Food Guide for Travelers Travelers at Dussehra should try fafda-jalebi in Gujarat, jalebi and chaat at north Indian melas, Mysore pak and bisi bele bath in Mysuru, Kolkata's Puja street food and bhog, and sundal at Tamil Golu homes.
  • What to Wear When Attending Dussehra Events in India For Dussehra events in India, wear breathable traditional clothes such as a kurta, salwar or saree in bright festive colors (red, yellow, orange), with comfortable closed shoes for crowds.
  • Dussehra Travel Packing List Pack for Dussehra travel: breathable festive clothes and a light layer, closed walking shoes, a scarf for temples, ear plugs, a mask for smoke and dust, a small power bank, and copies of ID.
  • Why Dussehra Is One of India’s Most Diverse Festival Travel Experiences Dussehra is one of India's most diverse festivals to travel for.
