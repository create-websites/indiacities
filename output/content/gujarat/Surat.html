Surat Tour

India's Silk City and Diamond Hub, where a blend of history and industry creates a vibrant tapestry.

City Snapshot

Area: 327 sq. km.
Altitude: 13 metres
Best Time: October to March
STD Code: (91) - 261

Historical Legacy

Founded by Hindu trader Mallik Gopi in 1496, Surat has been a significant port city with a rich history. By the late 17th and early 18th centuries, it had become one of India's wealthiest cities, renowned for its high-quality spices and silks that attracted merchants from around the globe.

Historical highlights include:

  • The establishment of the British East India Company's first warehouse in 1612.
  • Serving as the departure point for Mecca pilgrims during Mughal rule.
  • In the late 19th century, this city underwent a transformation, blossoming into a thriving industrial hub that became the nation's primary textile market and a center for petrochemicals and steel production.

Major Sightseeing

Dandi

The location of Mahatma Gandhi's renowned Salt Satyagraha, a significant symbol in India's fight for independence.

Bardoli

Sitting 34 kilometers away from Surat is the Swaraj Ashram, famous for hosting Gandhiji and Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel as they initiated the farmer's satyagraha in

Winchester Museum

Contains a unique assortment of historical and geographical writings about Surat, including some that are centuries old.

Bulsar (Vansada National Park)

An exciting experience for nature lovers, showcasing leopards, tigers, panthers, and wild boars in their native environment.

Attractions & Projects

Ukai Dam

Situated 94 km from Surat, this dam is one of the biggest in the country, rivaling the Bhakra Nangal in both height and water storage capacity.

Beaches & Resorts

Dumas seaside resort (16 km), palm-lined Hajira beach (28 km), and Tithal beach (108 km) provide ideal escapes.

Old Fort

Constructed in the 14th century by Muhammed Tughlaq, this fort stands as a prominent historical site in the city.

Culture & Festivals

Surat is a diverse city, often holding exchange programs like the blending of various cultures. Octave Festival, highlighting the customs, music, cuisine, and culture of eight states in north-eastern India.

The vibrant cultural life of the city is highlighted by its Navratri celebrations and traditional folk dances.

How to Reach

By Air

Nearest airport is Vadodara. Daily flights from Delhi connect Vadodara/Surat.

By Rail

Key stop on the Delhi-Ahmedabad-Mumbai line, with frequent services such as Golden Temple Mail and Gujarat Mail.

By Road

Excellent road network connects with Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Delhi.
