Pune: The Motor City

A key player in the Maratha Empire's history and a bustling hub of education and industry in India.

Overview

Pune, formerly known as Poona, Pune is the 8th largest metropolis in India and the second largest city in Maharashtra after Mumbai. Positioned at an elevation of 560 meters above sea level, it sits at the confluence of the Mula and Mutha rivers.

Education Hub: Features world-class facilities attracting students from India and abroad[cite: 590].
Motor City: Known for its widespread development of the automobile industry[cite: 598].
Tech Center: A rapidly growing center for Information Technology[cite: 589, 599].
Maratha History: Formerly a prominent hub of the Maratha Empire's power [cite: 588, 589].

History

Pune has a rich history, dating back to 937 AD when it was first recognized as a town. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, the founder of the Maratha Empire, grew up in Pune before his coronation in 1674, where he played a key role in the area's development.

Baji Rao became the Prime Minister of the Maratha Empire in 1720, establishing Pune as his headquarters. The city then served as the monsoon capital of the Bombay Presidency from 1837 until independence.

Industrial Powerhouse

Pune's status as the "Motor City of India" is driven by industry leaders[cite: 598]:

IT & Forging

Includes the prominent Hinjawadi IT Park (Rajiv Gandhi Park) and significant forging industries [cite: 589, 599].
