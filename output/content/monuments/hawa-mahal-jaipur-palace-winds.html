The Science of the Breeze

The palace's name is both poetic and practical, reflecting its purpose. Its design cleverly applies the Venturi effect from fluid dynamics to enable natural cooling.

💨

1. Air Enters

Warm air from outside flows through the wide openings at the rear of the palace.

→
↓

🌬️

2. Air is Constricted

The breeze flows through tight corridors and tiny, latticed *jharokhas*.

→
↓

❄️

3. Air Cools & Accelerates

When air is forced through narrow gaps, its speed rises while pressure falls, producing a refreshing, cool breeze indoors.

A Lasting Icon

Hawa Mahal stands as Jaipur's iconic landmark, showcasing Rajputana artistry and drawing visitors from across the globe.

Architectural Fusion

The palace beautifully merges Rajput and Mughal designs. Fluted columns, domed chhatris, and lotus patterns reflect Rajput tradition, while the ornate jali screens and elegant arches highlight Mughal artistry, forming a stunning fusion.
