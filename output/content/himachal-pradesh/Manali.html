Manali: Valley of the Gods

The Switzerland of India, where mythology meets breathtaking Himalayan landscapes.

Traveler's Snapshot

Altitude: 1,926 - 2,050 metres
Best Time: April to July & Sept to Nov
Summer: Max 25°C, Min 12°C
Winter: Max 14.5°C, Min 2°C
Languages: Pahari, Hindi, English
STD Code: 01902

Legend & History

Manali literally means the 'Home of Manu'In Hindu mythology, Manu is known as the lawgiver who survived a massive flood and sought refuge in order to repopulate the earth with humans.

Manali, previously referred to as the 'end of the habitable world,' is located at the entrance of the Kullu valley. It is a region filled with fruit-filled orchards, charming villages, and mountains covered in tranquil snow.

Major Attractions

Hadimba Temple

Constructed in the year 1533 A.D., this temple in the shape of a four-tiered pagoda honors Goddess Hadimba and is nestled amidst a thick forest of deodar trees.

Rohtang Pass (3979 m)

Gateway to the stunning regions of Lahaul, Spiti, and Leh, offering breathtaking mountain vistas and accessible from June through October.

Solang Valley

Situated 13 km away from Manali, this place provides breathtaking vistas of glaciers and snow-capped peaks. Known for its ski slopes and winter festivals.

Vashist Hot Springs

Known for its natural hot sulphur springs and separate bathing tanks, along with pyramidal stone temples honoring Vashist Muni and Lord Ram.

Adventure & Culture

Adventure Sports

Manali is a popular destination for skiing, hiking, mountaineering, paragliding, rafting, kayaking, and mountain biking. The Mountaineering Institute, located just 3 km from town, offers specialized training.

Tibetan Monasteries

Explore the vibrant newly-built monasteries in town and Aleo, where you can find genuine Tibetan handicrafts and carpets for sale.

Kothi & Rahala Falls

Kothi, a charming village on the way to Rohtang, is beloved by poets and filmmakers. Just 16 km away lies the captivating Rahala Water Fall, a popular spot for picnics.

Hidden Gems

  • Nehary Kund: Nehru used to drink from a natural spring of cold, clear water during his visits.
  • Arjun Gufa: Arjun is rumored to have meditated in a cave close to Prini village.
  • Jagatsukh: Renowned for its Shikhara-style temples honoring Lord Shiva and Sandhya Gayatri.
  • Club House: Situated along the Manalsu Nallah, providing indoor games and picnic areas.
