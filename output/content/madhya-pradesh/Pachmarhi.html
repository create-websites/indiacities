Pachmarhi

The 'Queen of Satpura' is a magical hill station known for its caves, waterfalls, and ancient tales.

Overview

Pachmarhi, also known as "Satpura Ki Rani" Known as the Queen of Satpura, this tranquil hill station is located in the Hoshangabad district of Madhya Pradesh. Nestled at an elevation of 1,100 metres in the Satpura Range, it provides a rejuvenating retreat just about 210 km away from Bhopal.

Altitude: 1,100 meters
Best Time: Throughout the year
Temp: 14.9°C to 25.7°C
Hindi, English

History & Colonial Heritage

Captain James Forsyth of the British Army introduced the area to the modern world in 1857, discovering the spot now recognized as. Priyadarshini Point (formerly Forsyth Point) and fell in love with its beauty.

Natural & Mythological Wonders

Pandava Caves

Rock-cut caves dating back to the 6th-7th century are thought to have been built in ancient times. According to legend, the Pandava brothers sought refuge in these caves during their exile.

Jata Shankar

A sacred cavern where Lord Shiva is believed to have found respite, adorned with naturally formed Shivalingas and the enigmatic 'Gupta Ganga' flow.

Dhoopgarh

Pachmarhi's highest point at 4429 feet offers unparalleled sunrise and sunset views, making it the top spot for breathtaking experiences.

Waterfall Wonders

Bee-Fall: A 150-foot spectacular fall also known as Jamuna Prapat.
Dutch Fall: Especially beautiful during the rainy season.

Spiritual Legacy

Bade Mahadev

A cave measuring 60 feet in length, believed to be the site where Lord Vishnu (in the form of Mohini) defeated Bhasmasur, is situated close to the cave dedicated to Goddess Parvati

Gupt Mahadev

The cave, measuring 40 feet, is located off a narrow path from Bade Mahadev and houses both Lord Ganesha and a Shivalinga.

Fairs and Festivals

Pachmarhi is renowned for its Shivratri Mela in February and March. Worshipers carry symbolic tridents and place them on the summit of Chauragarh hill to celebrate the marriage of Lord Shiva and Parvati.

How to Reach

By Air

Nearest airport is Bhopal (195 km), with flights connecting to Delhi, Mumbai, and Gwalior.

By Rail

The nearest railhead is Pipariya (47 km). Taxis are readily available from the station.

By Road

Pachmarhi is connected by regular bus services to Bhopal, Nagpur, Hoshangabad
