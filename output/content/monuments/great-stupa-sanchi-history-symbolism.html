The Written Stone

The Great Stupa's gateways (*toranas*) showcase brilliant narrative art. During this aniconic era, the Buddha was symbolized, not depicted in human form. Select an item to explore further.

Rediscovery and Restoration

Neglected for 600 years, Sanchi was rediscovered in the 19th century, sparking both destruction and efforts to preserve its legacy.

Rediscovery and Damage

In 1818, British officer General Henry Taylor discovered the ruins. Sadly, this triggered years of looting by treasure hunters and amateur archaeologists, leading to severe destruction, such as the west gateway's collapse and the loss of valuable relic caskets.

Scientific Conservation

Organized restoration efforts started in the late 1800s, peaking with Sir John Hubert Marshall's leadership (1912-1919). His team meticulously reconstructed the fallen structures, safeguarding the site for posterity and earning it UNESCO World Heritage status in 1989.
