Top Tourist Sites

Kaba Gandhi's No Delo (Gandhi Smriti)

The home of Mahatma Gandhi's ancestors, dedicated to his father Karamchand 'Kaba' Gandhi, now serves as a museum housing personal belongings and a striking photo collection chronicling the Mahatma's life, accessible through an elegant arched pathway.

Heritage & Recreation

Rashtriya Shala

Established in 1921 as part of Mahatma Gandhi's efforts, this institution was founded to offer national education during the Non-Cooperation Movement.

Jubilee Garden

The Connaught Hall is one of the colonial monuments located in the central recreational park of the city.

Libraries of Rajkot

The Lang Library and G.T. Sheth Library are major literary hubs in the city, contributing to its rich literary tradition.

Must Visit

An architectural marvel located near the city.

Jagat Mandir

A stunning temple dedicated to Shri Ramakrishna Paramhansa.

Picnic Spots
