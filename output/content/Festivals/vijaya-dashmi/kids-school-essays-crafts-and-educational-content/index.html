Kids & school

Essays, speeches, quizzes, worksheets and crafts that help children learn what Dussehra means.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • 10 Lines on Dussehra for Kids Here are 10 simple lines on Dussehra for kids in classes 1 to 5: what the festival is, why it is celebrated, and how families mark it.
  • Short Essay on Dussehra for School Students A short essay on Dussehra for school students, about 250 words, covering the date, the stories of Rama and Durga, how it is celebrated across India and its message of good over evil.
  • Dussehra Essay in 200 and 500 Words Two model Dussehra essays for students, about 200 and 500 words, with an introduction, the stories behind the festival, regional celebrations and a conclusion.
  • Dussehra Essay in Hindi for Students A Dussehra essay in Hindi (दशहरा पर निबंध) for school students: about 200 words in simple Devanagari covering the festival's meaning, stories, celebrations and message.
  • Dussehra Speech for School Assembly A two-minute Dussehra speech for a school assembly with a greeting, the stories of Rama and Durga, how India celebrates and a closing message about overcoming our own 'Ravanas'.
  • Short Dussehra Speech in Hindi A short Dussehra speech in Hindi (दशहरा पर भाषण) for school assemblies, about one minute long, in simple Devanagari.
  • Dussehra Paragraph for Class 3, 4 and 5 Students A Dussehra paragraph for class 3, 4 and 5 students, about 100 words in short, clear sentences on what the festival is, why it is celebrated and how.
  • Dussehra Project Ideas for School Students Dussehra school project ideas: a regional-celebrations map, a Ramayana comic strip, a Navratri-to-Dussehra timeline, an eco-effigy model, a family interview and a survey on noise and air pollution from fireworks.
  • Dussehra Poster Ideas for School Competitions Dussehra poster ideas for school competitions: 'burn the inner Ravana' with ten heads labeled as bad habits, Durga's victory, a map of India's Dussehra traditions, an eco-friendly Dussehra message and Ramlila scenes.
  • Dussehra Drawing Ideas for Kids Easy Dussehra drawing ideas for kids: a ten-headed Ravana effigy, Rama with his bow, Hanuman flying with the mountain, Durga on her lion, a Ramlila stage, a fair with a Ferris wheel, marigold garlands and a festive rangoli.
  • Dussehra Coloring Pages and Printable Activities Dussehra coloring and printable ideas for children: outline drawings of Rama, Ravana and Durga, a word search, a matching worksheet, a maze from Ayodhya to Lanka, a bow-and-arrow cut-out and a 'my good habits' chart.
  • Easy Dussehra Craft Ideas for Children Easy Dussehra crafts for children include a paper-plate Ravana mask, a toilet-roll effigy, a cardboard bow and arrow, a marigold toran from crepe paper, a mini Golu step display and a clay diya, all with safe materials and no fire.
  • How to Make a Ravana Effigy Craft for School Projects To make a school Ravana effigy, build a body from a plastic bottle or cardboard tube, attach ten paper heads in a row, and dress it with colored paper, foil and a crown.
  • Dussehra Quiz for Kids: Questions and Answers A 15-question Dussehra quiz for kids with answers, on Rama, Ravana, Durga, Navratri, Ramlila and regional traditions, ready for class or a family game.
  • 50 Dussehra General Knowledge Questions for Students Fifty Dussehra general-knowledge questions with one-line answers, grouped into dates and names, the Ramayana, Durga and Navratri, regional traditions and customs, for quizzes and exam revision.
  • Ramayana Story for Kids: Simple Dussehra Version A simple Ramayana story for kids, told in seven short parts from Rama's exile to his victory over Ravana and the return to Ayodhya.
  • How to Explain Dussehra to Children To explain Dussehra to children, start with one line ('it is the day good wins over bad'), then tell one story (Rama and Ravana or Durga and Mahishasura).
  • Dussehra Activities for Families and Children at Home Family Dussehra activities at home include a puppet Ramlila, a 'ten bad habits' paper Ravana, a Shami or Apta leaf exchange, a rangoli, cooking kheer together, a storytelling night and a family resolution jar.
  • Dussehra Vocabulary: Important Words and Their Meanings Twenty important Dussehra words with simple meanings, including Vijayadashami, Ramlila, Ravana Dahan, Navratri, Shami, Apta, Ayudha Puja, Vidyarambham, Bijoya and visarjan, for students and new learners.
  • Dussehra Worksheet for Kids: Rama, Ravana, Durga and Traditions A printable-style Dussehra worksheet with fill-in-the-blanks, true or false, matching and a short-answer question on Rama, Ravana, Durga and festival traditions, with an answer key.
