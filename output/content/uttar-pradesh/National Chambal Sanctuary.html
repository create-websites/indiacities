National Chambal Sanctuary

A Haven for Wildlife and Nature in the Center of India.

Explore the Sanctuary

The National Chambal Sanctuary in Uttar Pradesh is a must-visit for wildlife enthusiasts, providing a chance to discover the rich natural heritage and varied wildlife of Northern India.

Being a top wildlife destination, the sanctuary is connected to a larger system of protected areas, offering valuable insights and experiences for individuals passionate about conserving Indian wildlife.

Nature and Biodiversity

The sanctuary is committed to maintaining the ecological harmony of the area. Guests can appreciate peaceful views and experience the untamed wilderness while being educated on the sanctuary's conservation initiatives.

Wildlife Protection

Committed to protecting endangered species and their habitats throughout the Uttar Pradesh region.

Eco-Tourism

A place created for nature enthusiasts in search of tranquility and a deep bond with the wilderness.
