Vijayawada: The Place of Victory

A lively coastal town filled with commerce, historical repentance, and mythical victories.

At a Glance

Altitude: 125 Metres
Best Time: Sept to Feb (Dasara)
Languages: Urdu, English
Summer: 28°C - 47°C
Winter: 15°C - 30°C
Also Known As: Bezawada

History of Vijayawada

Vijayawada literally translates to "The Place of Victory"Legend has it that Arjuna, the prince of the Pandavas, prayed on top of Indrakeeladri Hill and received the blessings of Lord Shiva.

During the British rule, the city thrived with the completion of the Krishna Barrage, nestled between the Indrakiladri hills and the Budameru River. Today, it stands as a cosmopolitan hub, once frequented by the renowned Chinese traveler. Hieun T’sang.

Major Attractions

Kanaka Durga Temple

The ruling goddess of the city resides atop Indrakeeladri Hill. This self-manifested deity represents strength and wealth.

Prakasam Barrage

A modern regulator spanning 1223.5m across the Krishna River creates a unique 'Venetian' look for the city with its canals.

Undavalli Caves

These high hills, situated 8 km from the city, showcase impressive cut-in architectural and sculptural models with river views.

Bhavani Island

A charming island situated upriver on the Krishna River, perfect for a delightful picnic getaway for those who love holidays.

Excursions & Surroundings

Amaravati (64 km)

Known for the Lord Amareswara temple and internationally acclaimed Buddhist artwork and archaeological findings.

Kuchipudi (60 km)

Siddhendra Yogi, the creator of the renowned Kuchipudi dance style, was born in this location, which houses a complete dance academy.

Manginapudi Beach

Near Machilipatnam lies a natural bay with calm waters and a charming park complete with fountains.
