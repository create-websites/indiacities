Faridabad: The Industrial Hub

Founded in 1607, this historic township seamlessly fuses economic strength with rich cultural traditions.

Historical Legacy

Founded in 1607 A.D. Originally designed by Shaikh Farid, the treasurer for Emperor Jahangir, Faridabad was intended to safeguard the Delhi-Mathura Highway. The city features a distinctive semi-circular layout, with its expansive industrial areas located on the periphery.

  • Founder: Shaikh Farid (Baba Farid)
  • Region: National Capital Region (NCR)
  • Economy: Major Industrial Center
  • Boundaries: Delhi (North), UP (East), Gurgaon (West)

Major Attractions

Suraj Kund Tourist Complex

A picturesque picnic spot established by Rajput King Surajpal Tomar, includes a historic lake with rock-cut steps and the 'Rajhans' Siddha Kund, a natural pool with reputed healing powers.

Surajkund Crafts Mela

Each year in February, this renowned fair unites talented artisans from all corners of India, showcasing a lively tribute to traditional handicrafts, handlooms, and folk culture.

Nahar Singh Stadium

The stadium is a prime location for hosting One Day International (ODI) cricket matches, accommodating up to 25,000 spectators. Additionally, the venue boasts a modern sports complex tailored for swimming and football.

Baba Farid's Tomb

The city is named after a Sufi saint who locals believe founded it to protect the region, making it a popular pilgrimage center dedicated to him.

Industry & Shopping

Industrial Prowess

Faridabad excels in the production of Heena and the manufacturing of tractors, motorcycles (Escorts), switch gears, refrigerators, and tyres.

Modern Retail

The city has a thriving mall scene with top-notch shopping destinations like Crown Plaza, SRS Mall, and Crown Interiorz.

Quick Facts

Annual Crafts Fair

Every February at Suraj Kund.

Lakes

Badhkal Lake (Aravalli Range) and the picturesque Peacock Lake.

Leisure

Aravali Golf Club and Siddhadata Temple complex.
