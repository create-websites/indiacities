Nainital Tour

City of Lakes - Situated in the heart of the Kumaon region within the Central Himalayas.

The Naini Legend

Nainital is renowned for its captivating Naini Lake, a picturesque body of water nestled among hills. Named after Goddess Naini, this charming town is a paradise for photographers and offers a range of activities including boating, gentle hikes, and golfing.

Snow View

Accessible by cable car, it provides a stunning view of the snow-capped Himalayas.

Naina Peak

At an elevation of 8,568 ft, the peak offers a panoramic view of Nainital from above

Naina Devi Temple

A holy site for Hindus, said to be where the eyes of Sati landed.

High Altitude Zoo

A stunning high-altitude garden that is inhabited by tigers, bears, and deer.

Travel Logistics

Rail: Kathgodam (34 km away) is the nearest station.
Road: Well-connected buses from Delhi (approx. 8 hours).
