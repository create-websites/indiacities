Hubli: The Flower Creeper

North Karnataka's industrial powerhouse and major trading center for cotton.

Commercial Hub of North Karnataka

The name Hubli Originating from a Kannada term signifying 'Flower vine,' the city was previously referred to as Raya Hubli or Elaya Puravada Halli, and has transformed into a bustling commercial center renowned for its handloom textile factories and industrial production.

As the headquarters for the South Western Railway ZoneIt is home to one of the oldest railway wagon workshops and serves as a premier trading hub for groundnuts and cotton, featuring one of the largest cotton markets in India.

Must-Visit Attractions

Chandramouleshwara Temple

Constructed in the Chalukya era, this historic temple showcases a stunning statue of Lord Shiva as Chandramouleshwara.

Unkal Lake

A well-liked location for picnics with beautiful gardens, boating options, and play areas for kids, all with a stunning sunset view.

Siddharoodha Math

A renowned religious institution and hub for Advaita philosophy, inspired by the teachings of the esteemed Swami Siddharoodha.

The Glass House

Opened by the ex-Governor of Karnataka, this building bears a striking resemblance to the renowned glass house at Lalbagh in Bangalore.

Spiritual Diversity

Sayed Fateh Shah Vali Dargah

A place of communal harmony for Hindus and Muslims to worship, with educational institutions for girls on the premises.

Asar

Originally constructed as a hall of justice, this location currently houses two sacred hairs from the Prophet's beard and serves as the focal point for the annual Id Milad un Nabi festivities (Please note: Women are prohibited from entering).

Bhavanishankar Temple

This temple, another stunning Chalukya creation, showcases the ten incarnations of God encircling the central image of Sri Narayana.

Nature & Excursions

  • Nripatunga Betta: A beloved spot for picnics situated atop Unkal Hill, providing stunning city vistas.
  • Sathodi & Mathodi Falls: Nature-gifted waterfalls that are ideal for trekking and photography.
  • Sykes Point: A stunning vantage point to take in the sunset and lush landscape of the area.
  • Ulavia: A sacred place hosting the Shri Chennabasaveshwar temple, acting as a starting point to explore Jog Falls and Om Beach.

Quick Travel Guide

Economy

Key industries include steel furniture, food products, rubber, electrical equipment, and leather tanning.

Local Flavor

Don't leave without trying the world-famous Hubli Pedas (sweets).

Connectivity

Hubli serves as a significant railway hub in the South Western zone, boasting exceptional rail and road connections.

Key Info

Language: Kannada
Significance: Major Cotton Trading Centre
