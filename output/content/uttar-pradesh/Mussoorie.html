Mussoorie: Queen of Hills

A charming escape at 2003 meters in the Garhwal hills.

Historical Legacy

Captain Young of the British army discovered Mussoorie in 1820, falling in love with its beauty and making it his residence. Located just 34 kilometers from Dehradun, Mussoorie is now one of the most popular hill stations in India.

The name comes from the plentiful 'Mussoorie' plants that once grew here, now a hub for accessing key Hindu pilgrimage destinations such as Gangotri, Yamunotri, Haridwar, and Rishikesh.

Major Sightseeing Highlights

Gun Hill

The second highest summit in Mussoorie can be reached via a 400m ropeway, providing stunning views of Himalayan peaks such as Bunderpunch and Gangotri.

Kempty Fall

Situated 15 kilometers down Yamunotri Road, this is the largest waterfall in the region and a favored destination for rejuvenating dips.

Municipal Garden

A picturesque picnic area with a lovely garden and a man-made lake for boating.

Mussoorie Lake

A new spot located 6 kilometers from town on the Dehradun road, providing pedal boats and scenic village views.

Historic Sites & Temples

  • Sir George Everest House: Mount Everest was named in honor of the first Surveyor General of India, who called it his home.
  • Jwalaji Temple: Perched on Benog Hill at an elevation of 2104m, surrounded by dense forests, is a temple with a statue of Mata Durga
  • Nag Devta Temple: Situated 6 kilometers away from the town, providing delightful views of the Doon Valley.
  • Clouds End: Constructed in 1838, a heritage bungalow has been transformed into a luxurious resort, offering stunning views of the snow-covered Himalayas.

Plan Your Trip

Area: 65 sq km

Altitude: 2005.5 m

Best Time: April-June and September-October

Climate: Summers max 31.2°C; Winters min 1.1°C

How to Reach

By Air

Jolly Grant Airport (near Dehradun) is 24 km away.

By Rail

Dehradun, connected to Delhi, Mumbai, and Lucknow, serves as the closest railhead.

By Road

Buses run directly from nearby cities to popular stops such as the Library or Masonic.
