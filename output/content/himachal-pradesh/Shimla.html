Shimla: Queen of Hill Stations

Once the summer retreat of the British Raj, located at an elevation of 2205 meters above sea level.

Quick Facts

Area: 5.31 Sq Km
Altitude: 2205 Meters
Best Time: May-July & Sept-Nov
STD Code: 0177

Colonial History

During the British era, Shimla was designated as the summer capital of India, providing a refreshing retreat for British civil servants, merchants, and soldiers seeking relief from the intense heat of the plains.

The city is enveloped by stunning snow-capped mountains and lush green meadows, creating a picturesque backdrop. With its rich history, the landscape features many colonial buildings that add to the unique architectural beauty, harmonizing perfectly with the majestic Himalayan surroundings.

Major Attractions

Viceregal Lodge

Constructed in 1888 in the Elizabethan style, this grand structure located on Observatory Hills served as the British Viceroys' summer home before becoming the current home of the Indian Institute of Advanced Studies.

Christ Church

Situated on the Ridge, this church is the second oldest in North India and renowned for its breathtaking stained glass windows depicting faith, hope, and humility.

The Mall & The Ridge

The primary shopping district and popular gathering spot. Notable meeting spots include Scandal Point and The Ridge, providing stunning mountain vistas.

State Museum & Library

Situated 2.5 km away from Scandal Point, this museum contains a variety of ancient Indian artifacts such as sculptures, paintings, coins, and manuscripts.

Spiritual Landmarks

Jakhu Temple

A well-known temple devoted to Lord Hanuman, providing a majestic perspective of the nearby mountain ranges.

Tara Devi & Sankat Mochan

Tara Devi pays tribute to a form of Goddess Parvati, while Sankat Mochan is a serene temple devoted to the monkey deity, Hanuman.

Experiences & Entertainment

Festivals & Carnivals

  • Summer Festival: Organized in June with cultural programs.
  • Ice-Skating Carnival: Held in Dec-Jan at the Roller Skating hall.
  • Winter Carnival: A celebration of local culture and sports.

Theatrical Heritage

The Gaiety Theatre A replica of a historic British theater stands on the Mall and has showcased iconic performances by actors such as Shashi Kapoor.

How to Reach

Toy Train (Kalka-Shimla)

A UNESCO Heritage narrow-gauge railway journey that winds through stunning forests and passes through 103 tunnels.

By Air

Jubbarhatti Airport, located 23 km from the city, offers daily flights to Delhi and Chandigarh.
