Gurgaon: Commercial Hub

From a tranquil neighborhood to the most rapidly developing hub for business and IT in India.

Location and History

Once a quiet suburb of Delhi, Gurgaon has evolved into the rapidly growing city of India and a significant IT hubLegend has it that the Kauravas and Pandavas bestowed the city to their Guru as a token of respect. Now, it thrives as a bustling hub for both business and living, situated on the outskirts of Delhi.

Fastest Growing IT & Business Field
Mythological ties to the Mahabharata
Unofficial Mall Capital of India
Located on the outskirts of New Delhi

Key Tourist Spots

Sultanpur Bird Sanctuary

Located 15km from the city, this sanctuary founded by Dr. Salim Ali Approximately 100 bird species call this place home. The watch towers and museum are ideal spots for spotting migratory birds, especially during the winter months.

Damdama Lake

A pretty and eye-catching spot for nature lovers. The Water Banks The resort offers opulent amenities and activities such as hot air ballooning and boating.

Sheetala Devi Temple

A popular Hindu temple known as a 'Shakti Peeth'It draws in a significant number of followers year-round in search of spiritual blessings.

Bhondsi Forest Reserve

A stunning location for those who appreciate plant and animal life, showcasing a magnificent white statue of Lord Shiva standing atop a mountain.

Lifestyle and Entertainment

Gurgaon is the top choice for those seeking a taste of contemporary urban India, famous for its Hi-Tech MallsWith its variety of malls, movie theaters, and retail stores, it satisfies the shopping desires of all enthusiasts.

Shopping: Unofficial Mall Capital with global brand outlets.
Youth Hub: Booming with discos, pizza corners, and vibrant nightlife.

Nearby Excursions

Nuh

A historic town with iconic leaning towers and the tomb of Sheikh Musa.

Sohna

Known for its red sandstone masonry tank and a blend of Muslim and Rajput architectural styles.

Bhondsi Village

Stunning sunset vistas, perfect for nature lovers in search of tranquil woodland landscapes.
