Amethi: A Political Legacy

Discover the ancient capital of Uttar Pradesh and delve into its rich political heritage.

Amethi Overview

Located in the south-west part of Sultanpur district, Amethi officially falls under Chhatrapati Shahuji Maharaj Nagar districtThis area is widely acknowledged as the historic stronghold of the Nehru-Gandhi political family.

Rural Population: 83,299
Literacy Rate: 39.5%

Political Background

The Amethi Parliamentary Constituency became globally renowned for its strong ties to the family of Shri Jawahar Lal Nehru. The constituency has been represented by:

  • Shri Sanjay Gandhi
  • Shri Rajiv Gandhi (Former Prime Minister)
  • Smt. Sonia Gandhi (President of Indian National Congress)

Places of Interest

Raipur-Amethi

Explore the Hanumangarhi temple and a historic mosque, both built approximately a century ago.

Tomb of Malik Muhammad Jaisi

3 kilometers to the north of Ram Nagar lies the burial site of the renowned saint-poet.

Industrial Landscape

Amethi is home to the Avionics Division of Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), producing aircraft for the Indian Airforce, and also includes a unit of Indo Gulf Fertilizers.

How to Reach

By Rail

Direct connectivity to Delhi, Lucknow, Kanpur, Varanasi, and Kolkata.

By Road

UPSRTC buses provide seamless national and local connectivity.

By Air

Amausi Airport in Lucknow (141 km) is the nearest major hub.

Health & Education

  • Sanjay Gandhi Hospital: Well-known facility in Munshiganj.
  • Educational Hub: Home to IIIT and Rajarshi Rananjay PG College.
