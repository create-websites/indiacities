Mysore: City of Palaces

A place with a royal history, flourishing gardens, and a deep cultural heritage.

Overview

Mysore, whose name is derived from the demon king Mahishasura, is considered one of the top tourist spots in Karnataka. Formerly the state capital, it has transformed into a contemporary city while still maintaining its abundant historical and cultural heritage.

Known globally as the "City of Palaces," Mysore boasts a plethora of royal edifices built by past monarchs, as well as stunning waterfalls, historic temples, top-notch museums, and exquisite gardens.

Must-Visit Attractions

KRS Dam (Kannambadi)

Constructed by Sir M. Vishveswariah and funded by Maharaja Sri Krishnaraja Wodeyar, this engineering feat was the world's inaugural dam to include automatic sluice gates, creating one of India's most expansive lakes.

Brindavan Gardens

Created by Diwan Sir Mirza Ismail, these gardens are renowned for their captivating musical fountains. As the sun sets, numerous illuminated fountains move in time to enchanting music, producing a enchanting ambiance.

Chamundi Hills

Standing at an elevation of 1065m, the hilltop is home to the Chamundeshwari Temple, accessible by climbing 1000 granite steps and passing by a colossal Nandi bull and a massive statue of Mahishasura.

Mysore Zoo

Established with royal support, one of India's oldest zoos has thrived over the years. It is now a leader in captive breeding and boasts state-of-the-art facilities for animal care.

Heritage & Scenic Spots

St. Philomena’s Church

The grand building boasts towering spires reaching 175 feet high and houses a 3rd-century relic of St. Philomena in a catacomb beneath its main altar.

Lakes of Mysore

The city boasts three beautiful lakes: Karanji Lake (home to India's largest walkthrough aviary), Kukkaranahalli Lake, and Lingabudi Lake. These are prime spots for bird watching and picnics.

Travel Guide

Best Time to Visit

From September through February, it is the perfect time to discover the city's gardens and hills.

Photography Tips

Make sure to catch the evening fountain show at Brindavan Gardens and take in the sweeping city vista from Sykes Point.

Did You Know?

The KRS Dam supplies essential water to farmlands in Mysore and Mandya districts while also producing electricity for Shivanasamudra.
