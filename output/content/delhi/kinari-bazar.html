Kinari Bazar

Delhi's vibrant destination for wedding splendor and traditional embellishments.

Shopping Hub

When in Delhi, popular shopping spots to check out are Connaught Place, Chandni Chowk, Karol Bagh, Lajpat Nagar, South Extension, and Rani Bagh. If you're hunting for unique traditional finds, Kinari Bazar is a must-visit.

Festival Season

During major festivals such as Dussehra, Diwali, and Karwa Chauth, the bazaar transforms into a center for traditional and festival-themed goods that are crucial for Indian festivities.

Pro Tip: Bargain!

Haggling is a common practice at Kinari Bazar, so feel free to barter for the best prices on your cultural treasures.
