Moradabad

The Renowned "Brass City" of Uttar Pradesh

City Profile

Founded in 1600 AD by Prince Murad, son of the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan, Moradabad was initially named ChaupalaSituated along the Ramganga River, this city has developed into a significant industrial hub, located around 167 km from Delhi.

Key Stats (2001)

  • Population: 641,240
  • Literacy Rate: 74% (Higher than national average)

Languages

Hindi, Urdu, English, and Punjabi

The Brass Industry

The Indian Brassware Industry has its origins in the 1860s when Mohammed Yar Khan migrated from Afghanistan. Nowadays, Moradabad has become a leading hub for brass goods, home to around 5,000 industrial units and 600 export units.

Global Reach

Exports to the UK, USA, Germany, Canada, and the Middle East yield an annual revenue of more than ₹2,200 crore.

Beyond brass, the city is a significant exporter of Mentha, adding over ₹400 crore annually to the global market.

Travel & Logistics

By Road

Strong connectivity to Delhi, Bareilly, Agra, Ghaziabad, and Lucknow.

By Rail

Well-linked to major Indian cities through a robust railway network.

Nearest Airports

  • Jolly Grant (Dehradun) - 162 km
  • IGI Airport (Delhi) - 167 km
