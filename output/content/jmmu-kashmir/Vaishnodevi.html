Shri Mata Vaishno Devi

The ancient million-year-old sacred site where faith meets the majestic peaks of Trikoot.

The Divine Legend

The holy shrine of Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Known as one of the most revered and frequented pilgrimage destinations in India. 'Moonh Maangi Muradein Poori Karne Wali Mata'It is thought that no one leaves her presence without something in hand.

The sacred cave is located at an elevation of 5,200 feet within the tri-peaked mountain. TrikootIt is revered as the spot where Mata united her physical form with the astral form of the three Supreme Energies, appearing as three natural rock formations instead of statues. the Pindies.

The Yatra (Pilgrimage)

The pilgrimage involves a 12 km trek from KatraNumerous tea and snack stalls line the narrow mountain path to cater to devotees, while Mata's court is open 24/7 year-round.

Overseen by the Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board since its establishment in 1986, the site draws thousands of worshippers from both India and overseas.

Offerings & Donations

Traditional Offerings

Red Chunri, Saries, Cholas, ornaments (Silver/Gold), Dry Fruits, and Flowers. Note: No candies allowed, but Coconuts are welcome in the Cave.

Bhaint Shops

The Shrine Board runs Bhaint shops in Katra offering Prashad at reasonable prices, eliminating the need to bring them from the starting point.

You can make donations using cash, cheques, or online payments and receive tax benefits under section 80-G of the Income Tax Act.

Essential Tips for Yatris

Yatra Slip: Get a complimentary Yatra slip from the registration counter close to the Katra bus stand. You will not be allowed to pass the Banganga check post without it.

Transport: Make sure that porters, ponies, and palanquins possess a valid registration card and adhere to pre-approved rate cards.

Accommodation: Accommodations in Katra, Adkuwari, Sanjichhat, or Bhawan can be reserved at the Niharika Complex conveniently located near the bus stand.

Queue Management: Stop by the Yatra slip check post to get assigned a group number for the Darshan line.

Final Darshan: It is traditional for Darshans at the Bhairon temple to be performed exclusively. after visiting the Holy Cave.

Trip Planner

Base Camp

Katra, Jammu and Kashmir.

Trek Distance

Approximately 12 km from Katra to the Bhawan.

Preparation

Bring only essential items. Make sure to shower and put on fresh clothes before going for Darshan.
