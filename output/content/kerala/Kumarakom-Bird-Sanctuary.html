Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary

A verdant oasis nestled along the shores of Vembanad Lake, where local treasures and international visitors mingle.

Quick Facts

Location: Kottayam District, Kerala
Area: 14 acres
Best Time: June & August (Local), Nov to Feb (Migratory)
Climate: Tropical (10°C - 26°C)

Overview

Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary, also known as the Vembanad Bird Sanctuary, located in the Kottayam district of Kerala, is a popular spot for bird enthusiasts. Nestled beside the picturesque Vembanad Lake, this destination offers a perfect environment for various bird species, both local and migratory.

Surprisingly, this delightful region was once a rubber plantation called Baker's EstateAn Englishman later converted it into a bird sanctuary, providing a pristine haven for bird enthusiasts.

Enriched Wildlife

Indigenous Species

  • Brahminy Kite & Egret
  • Heron & Waterfowl
  • Cormorant & Darter
  • Cuckoo, Owl & Moorhen

Migratory Guests

  • Siberian Crane
  • Flycatcher & Teal
  • Parrot, Lark & more

A large number of travelers come from distances as vast as the Himalayan range.

How to Reach

By Air

The nearest airports are Kochi (76 km) and Thiruvananthapuram (160 km). International hubs include Chennai and Bangalore.

By Rail

Kottayam is the primary railhead, well-connected to major Kerala destinations.

By Road

Located just 12 km from Kottayam. Accessible via bus, taxi, or auto-rickshaw.
