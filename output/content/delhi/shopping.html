Connaught Place (CP)

CP, located in the heart of Delhi, is a vibrant blend of historical charm and contemporary business. Notable features include Palika Bazaar and Janpath In the city streets, you can find local vendors, while the surrounding area boasts high-end dining and designer stores.

Chandni Chowk

Established by Shah Jahan in 1650, this historic bazaar in Old Delhi continues to be a legendary hub despite the changing shopping trends with the emergence of modern malls. traditional shoppers, specifically for jewelry and marriage essentials. Don't miss Kinari Bazar for specialized event items.

Lajpat Nagar & South Extension

The nearby residential neighborhoods are known for their bustling markets, with Lajpat Nagar being the most famous for its vibrant atmosphere. Central Market, a favorite for local fashion and ethnic wear.

Exploring the NCR

Satellite cities in the National Capital Region (NCR) provide a range of upscale shopping opportunities.

Gurgaon (Gurugram)

Known for its luxurious shopping centers, this city is conveniently situated on the route from Delhi to Jaipur.

Noida & Faridabad

Lively urban centers boasting top-notch shopping opportunities, both cities are popular stops for travelers journeying from Delhi to Agra.
