Citadels of Power and Spirit

Explore India's majestic forts through an interactive journey. Uncover the tales, tactics, and architectural brilliance that define these timeless icons of strength, legacy, and creativity.

India's forts are more than relics of the past; they are vibrant chronicles etched in stone, strategy, and legend. This app invites you to explore these majestic structures, not as a linear narrative but as a network of interconnected stories. Discover the unique architectural visions of mighty dynasties, compare their approaches, uncover the myths that breathe life into them, and trace their historical evolution.
