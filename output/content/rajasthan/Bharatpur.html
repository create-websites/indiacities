Bikaner & Bharatpur

Majestic desert cities that served as important stops along the historic Silk Road.

Desert Majesty

In the center of the Thar Desert, Bikaner was established in 1486 A.D. by Rao Bikaji. This regal city is enclosed by a 5 km long fortification wall, historically serving as a crucial stop on the Silk Route for camel caravans.

Rajasthan, India
Best Season: October to March
Hindi, Urdu, Rajasthani
STD Code: 0145

Climate & Environment

Bikaner boasts a classic desert climate of blistering days and cool nights, set against a mesmerizing backdrop of rocky soil and sand dunes that's sure to enchant nature enthusiasts.

  • Summer (April - June): 28°C to 41.8°C
  • Winter (Nov - Feb): 5°C to 23.2°C

Vivacious Fairs & Festivals

Camel Festival

Each January, under the full moon, a festival is held showcasing camel races, camel dances, and lively rides in honor of the 'Ship of the Desert.'

Gangaur Fair

In March/April, Goddess Gauri is honored with celebrations. Women partake in rituals and processions to seek marital happiness.

Kite (Teej) Festival

To honor the establishment of Bikaner, a festival is held in April/May.

Karni Mata Fair

The desert citadel of Deshnok hosts the Navratra festival twice a year, in March/April and Sept/Oct.

Shopping & Wares

Bikaner is a center for traditional crafts and artistic talents, with notable locations including Kote Gate and Junagarh Fort.

  • Camel Hide Goods (Belts, Bags, Jootis)
  • Camel & Sheep Wool Carpets
  • Kundan Jewellery & Uncut Stones
  • Nokha Quilts & Sanganeri Prints
