Bhilwara: The Land of Courage

A renowned trading hub in Mewar with a rich history of bravery and perseverance.

Explore Bhilwara

Bhilwara, founded between 300 and 400 years ago, faced numerous destructions due to ruler conflicts before thriving in the 18th Century. Today, it is considered a prominent city. Mewar Acchal Trading centre and the "Land of Courage and Sacrifice."

Key Attractions

Mejha Dam

The biggest dam in the city also serves as a main water source, offering a rest house, pool, and playground for children. It is a popular spot for picnics during the rainy season.

Harni Mahadev

A sacred Shivling rests beneath a massive boulder by a waterfall, while the nearby Chamunda Mata temple provides a breathtaking vista of the city below.

Menal

The Mahanaleshwar Temple, constructed by the Chauhan dynasty, is nestled within a breathtaking valley where a majestic 150-foot waterfall cascades into a V-shaped gorge.

Joganiya Mata

An 8th-century temple located deep within a thick forest, this site was once used for animal sacrifices before being transformed by Jain Sadhvi Yaskanwarji. Today, it boasts modern amenities for tourists to enjoy.

Heritage Landmarks

  • Triveni Confluence

    The confluence of the Menali, Bards, and Badach rivers serves as a sacred location where devout pilgrims come together to partake in ceremonial baths during significant times such as Kartik and Magh.

  • Mangalgarh Fort

    A fortress naturally protected at an elevation of 1850 feet above sea level, with multiple temples dedicated to gods and goddesses spanning 1 kilometer in length.

  • Shahpura Art & Freedom

    Known for its 700-year-old traditional paintings and acclaimed artists, this place was not only a hub for the Ramsnehi Sampraday but also the residence of the patriotic Barhat family.

  • Bijolian Movement

    Significant in history for the Kissan movement, backed by Gandhiji, where farmers triumphed in their fight for rights against landlords.
