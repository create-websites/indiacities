Vadodara

Gujarat's cultural hub combines grand palaces with top-notch education.

City Profile

Area: 108.22 Sq. Km.
Altitude: 35.5 meters
Best Time: October - February
Climate: 10°C - 44°C

Historical Evolution

Vadodara has a long history, starting in 812 AD as a small settlement and experiencing changes in rulers from Hindu kings to the Delhi Sultanate in 1297, then the Gujarat Sultans and the Mughals over the centuries.

The Maratha rulers He took the throne in 1735 by defeating the Gujarat Sultans and reigned until India gained independence in 1947. Maharaja Sayaji Rao III is celebrated as an innovator who implemented civic changes and founded significant judicial and educational organizations.

Following independence, Vadodara became a prominent industrial center, specializing in the glass and pharmaceutical industries, all while upholding its esteemed academic standing.

Architectural & Cultural Wonders

Laxmi Vilas Palace

Constructed by Maharaja Sayaji Rao III in the Indo-Saracenic architectural style, this palace is one of the grandest in the city. The floors are made of beautiful white marble, and the interiors are filled with stunning artifacts and relics.

EME (Dakshinamurthy) Temple

Known for its distinct geodesic design, this sacred sanctuary for Lord Shiva embodies the beliefs of five different religions - Hinduism, Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, and Jainism. Surrounded by vibrant foliage and guarded by five majestic banyan trees, it stands as a symbol of unity and diversity.

Baroda Museum & Art Gallery

Within this establishment, you will find an impressive array of sculptures, Tibetan artwork, and Mughal miniatures. Standout displays feature an ancient Egyptian mummy, the skeletal remains of a blue whale, and Akota bronzes dating back to the 5th century.

Parks & Palaces

Sayaji Bagh (Kamati Baug)

An expansive facility showcasing an art gallery, zoo, museums, and a toy train. The meticulously tended lawns and gardens are greatly appreciated.

Makarpura Palace

This historic building, designed in a sophisticated Italian style, is now serving as a training school for the Indian Air Force.

Other key sites include the Kirti Mandir (royal mausoleum of the Gaekwads), the Lehripura Gate (built in 1558), and the Khanderao Market.

How to Reach

By Air

Vadodara Airport is located 8 km away from the train station, with daily flights connecting to Mumbai and Delhi through Indian Airlines and Jet Airways.

By Rail

Vadodara Railway Station is a key stop for all superfast and express trains on the Mumbai-Delhi route.

By Road

Located 118 km away from Ahmedabad and 420 km from Mumbai, state buses and taxis are easily accessible for transportation.

Local Languages

The main languages spoken in the city are Gujarati, Hindi, and English.
