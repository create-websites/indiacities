Trivandrum: Capital of Kerala

Built over seven hills, discover the tourist-friendly "City of Anantha."

Quick Facts

Area: 2,192 sq km
Altitude: Sea level
Best Time: October to April
Rainfall: 170 cms (May-Nov)
Languages: Malayalam, Tamil, English
STD Code: 0471

History & Overview

Trivandrum is the anglicised form of Thiruvananthapuramcapital city of Kerala, is famously referred to as the city built over seven hills. 'City of Anantha', referring to the sacred serpent in Hindu mythology.

The city is a key entry point to southern India, boasting its own international airport. It has deep historical and socio-economic ties to the Middle East, Sri Lanka, and the Maldives.

Major Attractions

Padmanabhaswamy Temple

Constructed in the Dravidian architectural style, the walls are adorned with exquisite murals depicting Lord Vishnu reclining on the annular snake, Anantha.

Kovalam Beach

A popular destination for tourists, boasting shimmering sands and gentle palm trees. Attractions include Lighthouse Beach, thrilling outdoor activities, and many yoga facilities.

Kuthiramalika Palace Museum

Established by Maharajah Swathi Thirunal Balarama Varma, this museum showcases a unique assortment of wood carvings, miniature paintings, and antiques.

Sri Chitra Art Gallery

Situated close to the Napier Museum, this place showcases artwork by Raja Ravi Varma and pieces from the Rajput, Tanjore, and Mughal dynasties.

Popular Excursions

Kanyakumari (87 Km)

Where the Arabian Sea, Indian Ocean, and Bay of Bengal converge lies the southernmost tip of India, renowned for its breathtaking sunsets and religious importance.

Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary

A popular spot for migratory birds such as the Siberian stork, boat cruises on Vembanad Lake offer the perfect opportunity to observe resident herons and egrets.

Thiruvallam (10 Km)

A tranquil section of water ideal for peaceful houseboat and canoe excursions.

Travel Guide

By Air

The international airport offers connections to Dubai, Colombo, and Kuwait, with daily domestic flights to Delhi, Mumbai, and Chennai.

By Rail

Thampanoor is a major railway junction where the Kerala Express runs daily to New Delhi.

By Road

Thampanoor's central bus stand serves as a hub for KSRTC buses traveling to Kochi, Kollam

Distance to Major Cities

  • Kochi: 220 Km
  • Madurai: 264 Km
  • Coimbatore: 413 Km
  • Bangalore: 753 Km
