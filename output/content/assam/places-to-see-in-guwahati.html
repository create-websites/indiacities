Guwahati: Heaven on Earth

A magical realm of endless azure peaks, stunning waterways, and revered temples.

The City of Eastern Light

Guwahati, the Assamese words from which the capital of Assam, receives its name ‘Guwa’ (areca nut) and ‘Haat’ (market). Historically known as ‘Pragjyotishpur’, the city is situated on the eastern foothills of the Himalayan Mountains.

Situated on the banks of the mighty Brahmaputra RiverIt is the primary industrial, commercial, and educational center of North East India, known for its tea plantations, tranquil rivers, and picturesque blue hills.

Spiritual Landmarks

Kamakhya Temple

Located on Nilachal Hill, the 10th-century shrine constructed by King Naranarayan is a renowned tantric site dedicated to Shakti worship, where the tradition of animal sacrifice is still practiced.

Umananda (Shiva) Temple

Situated on an island in the heart of the Brahmaputra River, accessible by motor boats and public ferries departing from Umananda Ghat.

Navagraha Temple

This ancient hilltop shrine, known as the 'Temple of Nine Planets,' has long been a hub for the study of astrology and astronomy.

Sri Balaji Temple

A stunning two-acre temple in Betkuchi showcases the architectural splendor of the South in the heart of the North East.

Culture & Heritage

Srimanta Sankardeva Kalakshetra

A cultural hub honoring the Vaishnava saint and cultural icon Srimanta Sankardeva, celebrating the rich heritage of the Assamese community through various art forms.

Vashistha Ashram

Founded by Sage Vashistha, this ashram is nestled in lush green surroundings, with the three flowing streams: Lalita, Kanta, and Sandhya.

Bhubaneshwari Temple

Perched atop the Kamakhya Temple, offering a breathtaking panoramic vista of Guwahati.
