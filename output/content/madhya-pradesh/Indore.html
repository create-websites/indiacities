Indore: Mini Mumbai

The economic hub of Madhya Pradesh, fusing Holkar dynasty legacy with contemporary industrial development.

Overview

Indore, the biggest city in Madhya Pradesh, is located on the banks of the River Khan and Saraswati. Known as the 'Mini Mumbai' of India, the city gets its name from the Indreshwar temple, the former seat of the Holkar rulers. It is now a bustling center for industries like automobile, textile, and IT, and boasts renowned educational institutions like IIM Indore.

Altitude: 553 meters
Rainfall: 152 cms
Best Time: September to March
Hindi, Marathi, English

History

Founded in 1715 as a bustling trade hub in the Narmada River valley, the city was named after the construction of the Indreshwar temple in 1741. Over time, it evolved into the capital of the Holkars and later served as the headquarters of the British Central India Agency.

Indore was the summer capital of Madhya Bharat from 1948 to 1956, and later became a part of present-day Madhya Pradesh.

Major Attractions

Rajwada

The Holkar's seven-storied palace, a historic landmark in the heart of the city, showcases the grandeur of Holkar architecture from around 200 years ago.

Kanch Mandir

A magnificent glass structure constructed in the early 20th century, showcasing an interior completely adorned with glass and mirrors that reflect the images of Lord Mahavir endlessly.

Lal Baag Palace

This ancestral palace, reflecting the grandeur and lifestyle of the Holkars, once boasted one of India's finest rose gardens and played host to numerous royal receptions.

Kamala Nehru Park

Frequently referred to as 'Chidiya Ghar', this location is a combination of a park and a zoo. It is well-liked for offering elephant and camel rides by the Khan River during the evenings.

Cultural Vibrancy

Rangapanchami

Five days after Holi, the local municipal corporations continue the tradition of sprinkling colored water on the main streets, a practice that has been celebrated with unique zest since the time of the Holkar reign.

Anant Chaudas

This festival, seen in September, showcases grand carnivals with floats (Jhanki) and the submergence of huge Ganesha idols.

Travel Guide

By Air

The Indore Airport is situated approximately 9 kilometers to the west of the city center, offering regular flights to various major cities across India.

By Rail

Indore serves as a crucial station in MP, offering direct connections for all major express trains.

By Road
