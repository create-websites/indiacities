Gandhinagar

The capital city of India is known for its lush greenery and is a sanctuary of tranquility, creativity, and spirituality

History & Location

Gandhinagar, the capital city of Gujarat in western India, is renowned for being the greenest capital in the country, situated on the banks of River Sabarmati.

Located just 30 kilometers away from Ahmedabad, this destination has become a popular tourist spot. Legend has it that it was commissioned by Sultan Ahmed Shah, the same ruler who founded the city of Ahmedabad.

Major Attractions

Akshardham Temple

Gandhinagar's Akshardham temple, a favorite destination, is a stunning masterpiece adorned with intricate carvings on pink stones. Encircled by vibrant gardens and flowing fountains, the temple is a sight to behold.

Nighttime laser displays are held at the temple and draw in numerous visitors.

Adalaj Ni Vav

Situated approximately 18 kilometers south of Gandhinagar, the step-well was constructed by Rani Rudrabai during the 15th century and features intricate floral carvings.

Nature & Parks

Indroda & Deer Park

Indroda Park (Deer Park) offers a tranquil retreat from urban living, boasting a Dinosaurs Park with diverse statues, a python enclosure, an aquarium, and a snake park.

Recreation & Gardens

Sarita Garden, located along the River Sabarmati, is a popular spot for picnics, while Punit Van in Sector 19 is a unique botanical garden that links plant varieties with zodiac signs.

Culture & Heritage

To explore the rich local heritage, be sure to check out the craftsmen village in Pethapur. Additionally, don't miss these other notable landmarks in the city:

  • VitthalBhai Patel Bhavan: The domed Capital Complex.
  • Sector 28 Children's Park: Featuring a toy train and rides.
  • Gujarat Law University: A prominent academic site.
  • Nearby: The golden beaches of Daman and Diu.
