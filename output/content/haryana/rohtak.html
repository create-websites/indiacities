Rohtak: The Educational Hub

An essential satellite city of New Delhi, contributing significantly to Haryana's academic and industrial development.

Historical Legacy

Rohtak, whose name is derived from Rohtashgarh Raja Rohtash, a major town in Haryana, is situated 70 km west of New Delhi and 210 km south of Chandigarh. It is a key satellite city in the National Capital Region.

Total Area: 1745 sq. km
Population: 940,128 (2001 Census)
Premier Academic Destination
Major Industrial Hub

Academic Excellence

Top Universities

  • Indian Institute of Management (IIM): A top-ranked Indian institute.
  • Maharishi Dayanand University: A cornerstone of higher education.
  • Engineering Institutes: Vaish College and Matu Ram Institute.
  • Sanskriti College of Education: Premier teacher training center.

Notable Schools

  • Model School: Renowned for academic standards.
  • D.A.V. Centenary Public School: A leading public school.
  • The Sanskriti School: Modern holistic education.
  • Shiksha Bharti Sr. Sec. School: Quality secondary education.

Industrial Powerhouse

Agricultural Economy

Approximately 51.89% A significant portion of the workforce in the region is involved in agriculture, where modern machinery such as versatile threshers, seed tools, and mechanical weeding wheels are used to enhance productivity.

Sports Infrastructure

Rohtak boasts a sprawling sports facility that includes a Cricket Pavilion, Soccer Stadium, Hockey Ground, and dedicated halls for Boxing and Wrestling.
