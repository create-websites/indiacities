Thane: The City of Lakes

Known in history as 'Sristhanaka' and India's first railway's original endpoint.

Regional Overview

Thane district is located in the northwestern region of Maharashtra and is commonly referred to as the Abode of Lord GaneshaThis town is surrounded by Nashik and Ahmadnagar to the east, Raigad to the south, Mumbai to the north, and Pune to the southeast. It is a thriving industrial hub experiencing rapid growth in trade and construction.

Terminus of India's first railway
Former Portuguese trading center (until 1739)
Home to approximately 30 lakes
Divided by the scenic Thane Creek

Waterways & Recreation

The identity of Thane is shaped by its numerous water bodies, providing ample opportunities for boating and riding water scooters.

Upvan Lake

Nestled among the foothills of Yeour hills, and encircled by the majestic Neelkanth, this spot is a favorite among both young people and families in search of peaceful natural beauty.

Talopali

A popular spot for people of all ages to gather, where they can partake in activities like rowing, riding water scooters, and indulging in local roadside eateries offering delicious Bhel and ice cream.

Major Rivers

  • Vaitarna: Flows across Shahapur, Wade, and Palghar.
  • Ulhas: Begins at Borghat and flows into the Arabian Sea at Vasai Creek.

Places of Interest

Yeour Hills

Also referred to as Mama Bhanja Hills, this sanctuary is a paradise for wildlife enthusiasts and animals alike. It is home to a diverse range of species. Swami Math, a perfect retreat for peace seekers.

Tikujiniwadi

A top-notch picnic destination nestled in the wilderness, complete with a restaurant on-site and a plethora of outdoor activities to enjoy surrounded by natural beauty.

Hill Stations

The district features naturally beautiful, pollution-free hill stations like Jawahar and Suryamal.

Nearby Escapes

Conveniently located sights to visit are Essel World, Water Kingdom, Matheran, Tansa Wildlife Sanctuary, and Jawhar Palace.

Travel Guide

Climate

The temperature ranges from 32°C to 34°C in hot and humid weather, with mountainous regions receiving higher rainfall than the coast.

Other Notable Lakes

  • Masunda Lake
  • Kachrali Lake
  • Makhmali Lake
  • Siddheshwar Lake
  • Raila Devi Lake

Distance Hub

Close proximity to Mumbai, providing a gateway to North Maharashtra.
