Jaipur: The Pink City

The grand capital of the Maharajahs, India's inaugural planned city.

Historical Legacy

Founded in 1727 first planned city of India by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is recognized globally. Pink City, it is an essential component of the popular Golden Triangle tourist route that also includes Delhi and Agra.

Area: 200.4 sq. km
Best Season: Oct to mid-March
Hindi, Rajasthani, English
STD Code: 0141

Major Sightseeing

Amber Fort

Well-known for the stunning Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace), this 16th-century masterpiece showcases Hindu and Mughal architecture.

Hawa Mahal

The 'Palace of Winds' built in 1799 boasts 953 screened windows, allowing royal ladies to observe street festivals.

Jantar Mantar

Constructed as the largest stone observatory in the world, this structure was designed to accurately measure local time, altitude, and eclipses.

Jal Mahal

A crimson sandstone palace sits at the center of Man Sagar Lake, with four levels still hidden beneath the water's surface.

Vibrant Festivals

Elephant Festival

Elephants participate in polo, races, and processions groomed in gold.

Kite Festival

Observed on January 14th, when the sky is filled with thousands of kites competing fiercely.

Travel Logistics

By Air

The distance from Jaipur Airport (Sanganer) is 13 km, making it the sole international airport in Rajasthan.

By Rail

The Bani Park station serves as a connection point to important cities, with the 'Palace on Wheels' making a stop here as well.

Climate

Summer: 45°C Max | Winter: 22°C Max to 8°C Min (evenings can drop to 4°C).
