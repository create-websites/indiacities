Imphal: The Homestead

A historic city nestled in a verdant valley, acting as the heart of Manipur's culture and politics.

Overview

Imphal, the capital city of Manipur, is among the oldest towns in the Indian subcontinent. yumpham The center of cultural, commercial, and political activities, (known as homestead), is nestled in a wooded valley encircled by the Koubrou and Namaijing ranges, presenting a picturesque landscape.

Best Time: October to February
Temp: 0.0°C to 32°C
Languages: Manipuri, Hindi, English
Area: 29.57 sq. kms

Top Attractions

Keibul Lamjao National Park

Located 53 kilometers from Imphal, the world's only floating National Park is the last remaining natural habitat of the marsh-dwelling 'Sangai' deer.

Ima Bazaar (Mothers Market)

This distinctive market, also called Nupi Keithel, is operated solely by women who vend traditional hand-woven shawls, fish, and honey flavored with orange.

Loktak Lake & Sendra Island

Sendra Island, offering a bird's-eye view of the park, is the largest freshwater lake in eastern India and functions like an inland sea.

Shree Govindajee Temple

Manipur's top Vaishnavite temple boasts golden domes and a spacious congregation hall, located next to the former royal palace.

Historical & Heritage Sites

Andro Cultural Complex

An ancient village located 27 kilometers from Imphal is home to northeastern pottery and a 'Doll-House' showcasing 29 recognized tribes.

War Cemeteries

Administered by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission, honoring the memory of British and Indian soldiers who perished during World War II.

Moirang & INA Museum

A historic location where the Indian National Army flag was raised for the first time in 1944, housing a museum dedicated to the fight for independence.

Sekta Archaeological Site

The ASI uncovered a living museum showcasing ancient secondary burial practices.

Travel Guide

By Air

Indian Airlines offers daily flights connecting Imphal and Delhi through Guwahati.

By Rail

Travelers must travel by bus or air to cover the remaining distance after reaching the nearest railheads in Guwahati or Dimapur.

Special Days

April 23: Khongjom Day is a state holiday that honors the Anglo-Manipuri war of 1891.
