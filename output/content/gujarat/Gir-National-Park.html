Gir National Park

The final sanctuary of the Asiatic Lion within the heart of Gujarat's arid deciduous woodlands.

Sanctuary Profile

Area: 1412 Sq. Km
Altitude: 40m to 50m
Best Time: Mid October to Mid June
Temperature: 12°C to 40°C

Pro Tip: November and February are ideal for lion sightings.

Conservation History

The history of Gir is defined by the preservation of Asiatic lions, with its population decreasing to only 15 individuals in the early 20th century as a result of trophy hunting.

Concerned about the decrease, British viceroys, including Lord Curzon, urged the Nawab of Junagadh to take action. In response, the Nawab proclaimed the forest and its lions as protected and enforced a strict hunting ban.

Currently, Gir is the biggest compact area of dry deciduous forest in western India, home to the largest number of carnivores and the biggest population of marsh crocodiles in the nation.

Beyond the Wild

Jeep Safari

The best time to observe big cats is during the early morning and late evening, when driving through the forest in a jeep offers a close-up view of lions in their natural habitat.

Junagadh Heritage

Discover the hard-to-reach Uparkot Fort, the Maqbara tombs of the Nawabs, and the ancient rock inscriptions of Emperor Ashoka.

Nearby Points of Interest

  • Beaches: Explore the sunny coastal resort of Chorwad or the pristine beach of Ahmadpur Mandvi, known as one of India's best.

  • Temples: Somnath Temple is among the twelve most sacred Shiva shrines in India, with the Damodar sacred tank and Girnar temples being must-visit sites as well.

  • Tribal Culture: Interact with indigenous tribes living within the park, sharing their habitat with wildlife and preserving distinct ways of life.

Visitor Guidelines

» Take official guides for every trip.

» Do not tease or irritate animals.

» Smoking is strictly prohibited.

» No flash photography or intrusive gear.

» Camping and picnicking are not allowed.

» Wear simple clothes that blend with nature.

How to Reach

By Air

Closest airport is Keshod, just 90 km away, with daily flights available from Mumbai.

By Rail

Nearest connection: Meter gauge line from Ahmedabad (395 km).

By Road

Located 400 km away from Ahmedabad, state buses operate from Junagadh and Veraval during the months of November to June.
