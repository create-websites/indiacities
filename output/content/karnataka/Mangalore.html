Mangalore: Coastal Gateway

An energetic coastal town with ancient temples, historic churches, and sun-drenched beaches.

City Overview

Mangalore, situated on the coast, is a crucial center for commerce and trade. Dakshina Kannada Located in Tulu Nadu, Karnataka, the district was established as a hub in August 1997 and is one of the five taluks in D.K.

The city provides a special mix for visitors, with numerous ancient temples and churches for the spiritual, and picturesque beaches for those in search of both relaxation and excitement.

Spiritual & Architectural Wonders

Kadri Manjunath Temple

Constructed in the 14th century atop Kadri hills, this temple is devoted to Manjunatheshwara (a form of Shiva) and holds the most ancient idol of Lord Manjunathaswamy in South Indian temples. Every year during the Hindu month of Karthika, the temple is illuminated with numerous earthen lamps to celebrate Deepotsava.

St. Aloysius College Chapel

Perched on a hill, this stunning gem mirrors Rome's Sistine Chapel with its breathtaking interior showcasing a collection of intricate paintings by the renowned artist Bro. Moscheni.

Shri Sharavu Mahaganapathi Temple

A sacred site for pilgrims located in the city center, this place was first devoted to a Shivalinga but now also contains representations of 'Sri Dhashabhuja Mahaganapathy' and 'Siddilaxmi,' hosting various cultural events.

St. Aloysius Church

Constructed during the 18th century, this lovely church showcases breathtaking paintings by the Italian artist Antony Moshaini adorning its walls.

Landmarks & Parks

Sultan Battery

Tipu Sultan constructed a black stone fortress in Boloor to safeguard the Gurpur River from enemy warships. Nowadays, this abandoned stronghold, complete with surrounding cannons and 'Tipu's Well', attracts many tourists.

Kadri Park

An ideal family garden with a toy train and a zoo filled with snakes and other exciting animals to entertain children.

Light House Hill-Garden

Provides stunning vistas of the ocean, sunset, and passing sailboats from an impeccably landscaped hillside garden.

Adventure & Coastal Life

Adventure seekers and beach lovers flock to Mangalore for its prime destination offerings.

Pilikula Nisarga Dham

A sprawling recreational park offering a water theme park ('Manasa'), golf course, science center, arboretum, and boating amenities.
