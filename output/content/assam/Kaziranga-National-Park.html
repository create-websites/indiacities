Kaziranga National Park

A beautiful refuge and the majestic dwelling of the Great One-Horned Rhino.

History & Conservation

Kaziranga National Park, located in Assam in the northeast of India, is renowned for its breathtaking beauty and diverse wildlife. It is home to the one-horned rhino (Rhinoceros unicornis) and lies on the southern banks of the Brahmaputra River.

Established as a game reserve in 1908, it was upgraded to a sanctuary in 1940 to combat rampant poaching. The Indian Government officially designated it as a protected site in 1974.

The rhino and the elephant, the park's original residents, now flourish in a tranquil setting. With its expansive open areas and the formidable Brahmaputra and Mikir hills, the park offers a true jungle experience.

Major Wildlife Attractions

The Big Five & More

In addition to the one-horned Rhino, the park is home to significant numbers of Indian Elephants, Indian Bison, Swamp Deer (Barasingha), Sloth Bears, and Tigers. Visitors may also spot Leopard Cats, Hoolock Gibbons, and Wild Buffalo.

Birding Paradise

A country known for its raptors, including the Pallas's Fishing Eagle and Himalayan Griffon, attracts large flocks of migratory birds like Greylag Geese and Ruddy Shelduck to its lakes during winter.

Elephant Safari

The expansive countryside provides a delightful wildlife viewing experience. Guests have the option to embark on an elephant-back ride in the early morning hours with experienced Mahouts.

This opportunity allows visitors to observe wild animals up close, such as elephant herds and rhinos calmly passing by.

Sightseeing & Local Culture

  • Tea & Plantations: Explore the renowned tea estates of Assam or wander through the verdant coffee and rubber plantations of Karbi Anglong.

  • Karbi Villages: Explore Karbi villages to connect with the locals and experience their distinctive lifestyle.

How to Reach

By Air

The closest airport to the park is Guwahati, located 217 km away, with Jorhat as another option at a distance of 97 km.

By Rail

Furkating, the closest railhead to the National Park, is located 75 km away.

By Road

Kohora on NH-37 is where the main gate is located, where both ASTC and private buses make stops on their route to Guwahati, Tezpur, and Upper Assam.
