Gwalior - Tour to Historical City

A region in Madhya Pradesh filled with tales of folklore and impressive architectural beauty.

City History

Gwalior's history dates back to a legend from the 8th century AD, where a chieftain named Suraj Sen was healed by a hermit-saint named Gwalipa, leading him to found the city in honor of the saint.

Places of Interest

Gwalior Fort

The city's most prominent monument, towering over the landscape on a steep sandstone hill, has observed historic events, conflicts, and was praised by Emperor Babur as 'the jewel of the fortresses in Hind.'

Gujari Mahal

Nestled in the fort, this ancient wonder has been transformed into an Archaeological Museum showcasing precious artifacts from as early as the 1st century A.D.

Gopachal Parvat

Situated on the hills of Gwalior Fort, this site showcases exceptional statues of Jain Tirthankars commissioned by Tomar Kings, including the largest single-stone statue of Bhagwan Parshwanath sitting on a lotus.

Jai Vilas Palace and Museum

The Scindia family currently resides in a home with Italianate architecture. More than 25 rooms have been transformed into the Jivaji Rao Scindia Museum, displaying their regal lifestyle.

How to Reach

By Rail

Well-linked with Delhi, Agra, Mumbai, and Chennai rail networks.

By Road

Linked to Agra, Mathura, Jaipur, Delhi, Lucknow, Bhopal, Indore, Jhansi

By Air

The distance between Gwalior airport and the city is 8 km, providing connections to Mumbai, Bhopal, Indore, and Delhi.
