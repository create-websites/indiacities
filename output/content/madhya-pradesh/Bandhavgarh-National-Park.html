Bandhavgarh National Park

Land of the Tigers and Ancient Legends

Quick Facts

Area: 450 sq.km
Altitude: 800m above sea level
Best Time: February to June
Language: Bagheli, Hindi
Temp Range: 2°C to 42°C
STD Code: 07653

Overview

Bandhavgarh National Park, located in Madhya Pradesh, is situated 197 km northeast of Jabalpur and is part of the Vindhyan mountain ranges. It is famous worldwide for its wildlife. highest density of tiger population There are around 46 to 52 tigers spotted in this country.

The park is surrounded by forested mountains and cliffs, with wetlands that support thriving populations of Kingfishers and Egrets, and vultures nesting in the towering cliffs above.

Mythology & History

The park's name is derived from an ancient fort situated in its central region, dating back to the mythological era. RamayanaAccording to legend, the architects of the bridge to Lanka constructed this fort for Lord Rama, who then presented it to his brother Lakshmana.

Rewa was established in 1234 as a state in ancient times. The fort was acquired by the Baghela family as part of a dowry and was later used as a private game reserve for the Maharajas of Rewa.

Wildlife & Avifauna

Mammals

  • Carnivores: Tiger, Leopard, Sloth Bear, Gray Mongoose
  • Primates: Common Langur, Rhesus Macaque
  • Herbivores: Spotted Deer, Sambar, Gaur, Nilgai

Birds

With over 250 species, it is a bird watcher's paradise.

  • Grey Malabar Hornbill
  • Paradise Flycatcher
  • Steppe Eagle
  • Blossom Headed Parakeet

Places to See within the Park

Shesh Shaiya: 10th-century statue of Lord Vishnu reclining on a serpent.
Chakradhara: A sprawling meadow rich in flora and fauna.
Bari Gufa: The biggest man-made cave dating back to the 10th century.
Climber's Point: Spectacular views of woody climbers amidst giant Sal trees.
Sehra: The biggest meadow, offering great fort views.
Mahaman Pond: Ideal water hole to watch herbivores and carnivores.
