Nagarhole Wildlife Sanctuary

Explore the raw beauty of nature at the Kabini River's edge.

Quick Facts

Area: 644 sq Km
Best Time: September to May
Languages: English, Hindi, Kannada
Climate: Summer (Max 33°C), Winter (Min 14°C)

History & Landscape

Once the exclusive hunting grounds of the rulers of Mysore, Nagarhole became a protected area in the 1950s. Today, it provides a captivating glimpse into the beauty of nature, showcasing dense forests, sprawling grasslands, and abundant water sources.

The River Kabini The river acts as the park's southern border, dividing it from Bandipur National Park. It is a bustling gathering spot for animals seeking water and sunlight.

Wildlife & Avifauna

Mammals

  • Major Attraction: Giant Asian Elephant
  • Predators: Tiger, Panther, Wild Dog
  • Others: Bison, Sloth Bear, Spotted Deer, Sambhar, Bonnet Macaque

Birds

Over 250 rare species of birds reside here:

  • Crested Serpent
  • Woodpecker
  • Golden Back Parakeet
  • Crested Hawk
  • Alexandrine
  • Bulbul

Experience the Wilderness

The Forest Department offers mini bus safarisPrivate jeeps are allowed but must be accompanied by an official guide, making them the best way to spot wildlife.

Elephant joy rides are another major attraction for tourists visiting the sanctuary.

Travel Guide

By Air

The closest airport to Bangalore, located 235 kilometers away, offers daily flights to major cities.

By Rail

Mysore, located 95 km away, is the closest railhead with frequent train services from Delhi and other major cities.

By Road

Nagarhole is connected to Mysore and Bangalore by state buses and taxis. The bus fare from Mysore is around Rs. 50.
