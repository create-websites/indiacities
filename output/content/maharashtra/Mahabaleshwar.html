Mahabaleshwar Hill Station

The majestic Western Ghats cradle the 'Queen of Hill Stations'.

Overview

Mahabaleshwar, known as the 'Queen of all the Hill Stations'Known for its tranquil lakes, lush hills, and breathtaking viewpoints, is a sought-after destination. Its name originates from a Lord Mahadev temple and three Sanskrit words. Maha (great), Bal (power), and Ishwar (God).

Altitude: 1,372 meters
Rainfall: 7640 mm
Temp: 13°C to 29°C
Marathi, Hindi, English

History

Mahabaleshwar's origins can be traced back to 1215, when King Singhan of Deogiri erected a modest temple and water tank at the river Krishna's source. Over time, the area saw successive rule by Brahmin dynasties, the Moguls, and Muslim Kings from Bijapur and Ahmednagar.

In the 17th century, Chatrapati Shivaji Maharaj The region was taken over by the Peshwas in their fight against foreign domination. They continued to govern until 1819, when it was integrated into the Raja of Satara's territory.

Famous Viewpoints & Attractions

Arthur's Seat

This point, standing at an elevation of 1470 meters, provides a distinct geographical perspective of the division between Kokan and Deccan. The spot is known as Mallet Point in honor of Arthur Mallet, the pioneer who constructed the first residence at this location.

Wilson Point

Also known as Sunrise PointAt 4710 ft above sea level, Mahabaleshwar's highest point boasts three observation towers strategically placed for optimal views.

Venna Lake

A popular spot that covers 25 acres, known for its boating, fishing, horseback riding, and renowned for delicious strawberries.

Kate's Point

Referred to as the 'echo point', this spot provides stunning vistas of the Krishna river meandering through lush fields.

Pratapgadh Fort

Constructed in 1656 AD under the orders of Shivaji Maharaja by Moropant Trimbak Pingale, this fort stands at an elevation of 900 meters. Inside, visitors can find a statue of Shivaji, along with temples dedicated to Mahadev and Hanuman. Additionally, the fort provides panoramic views of Raigad and Makarandgad forts.

Other notable spots include Bombay Point (Sunset Point), Lodwick Point (Sydney Point), and the Lingamala, Chinaman, and Dhobi Waterfalls.
