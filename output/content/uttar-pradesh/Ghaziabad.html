Ghaziabad: Gateway of UP

The historical intersection of ancient mythology and modern industry.

Discover Ghaziabad

Adjacent to Delhi, Ghaziabad is a thriving industrial hub and a growing educational center in the NCR. The city boasts rich mythological and historical significance, with Garhmukteshwar being a place of salvation and villages like Pooth having ties to the Mahabharat era.

Key Places of Interest

Hapur

Hapur, the district's largest tehsil, serves as a significant market for grain, jaggery, and papar. The historic 1670 Jama Masjid, constructed during Aurangzeb's rule, is located here.

Modinagar

Established by Gujar Mal Modi, this manufacturing hub creates necessities such as sugar, fabrics, and coatings. It showcases a 19th-century shrine constructed by Rani Bala Bai Sindhia.

Bahadurgarh

This town, renamed by Nawab Bahadur Khan, is renowned for its Khurja pottery and handloom cloth.

Dasna

Just 10 km away from Ghaziabad lies a place whose name is tied to a tale of a man getting bit by a snake (Dasna) during the construction of a fort.

Historical & Legend Hubs

  • Ajrara: Located on the banks of the Kali Nadi, this place is named after Yogi Ajaipal who constructed a thakurd
  • Dhaulana: Famous for its memorial commemorating the 1857 freedom struggle and the temple dedicated to Sati Malindi Devi.
  • Loni: Associated with the demon 'Lanvanasur' or the legendary King Lonnkaran.

At a Glance

Industrial Hub

Major producer of sugar, soap, varnishes, and flour.

Key Monument

Jama Masjid in Hapur (Built 1670).

NCR Proximity

Directly shares a boundary with the national capital, Delhi.
