Guntur City Trip

Discover the wealth of natural wonders, ancient caves carved into rock, and historic fortresses.

Introduction

Guntur is a region rich in nature's gifts, including hills, valleys, rivers, and beaches, and was a prominent hub for the growth of Buddhism in ancient times.

Guntur's cosmopolitan culture, rich history, modern attractions, scenic spots, and mouthwatering cuisine have transformed it into a sought-after tourist spot.

Major Attractions

Nagarjuna Sagar Dam

Situated 150 kilometers away from Guntur, this dam stands as the world's largest masonry dam, towering at a height of 124 meters. The awe-inspiring size and stunning scenery make it a sight to behold.

Ethipothala Falls

Emanating from the Chandravanka River, a tributary of the Krishna, these waterfalls flow 70 feet into a stunning lagoon surrounded by picturesque landscapes.

Undavalli Caves

Historic four-story rock-cut temples and caves line the shores of the river Krishna, showcasing a harmonious blend of Hindu and Buddhist influences in their architecture.

Mangalagiri

Known for the Lakshmi Narasimha temple, where devotees believe the deity only accepts half the amount of 'Panaka' (sweet drink) offered to him.

Historic & Religious Sites

Kondaveedu Fort

Constructed in the 10th century A.D. under the Reddy Kings, this site boasts 21 stupas and provides stunning natural vistas perfect for trekking.

Macherla & Karampudi

Macherla is famous for the Chennakesava Swamy temple constructed by the Hiheya Kings. In Karampudi, there is also a historic Chennakesava Swamy temple that houses weapons from the Palnadu war.

Unique Temples

Visit Chebrole to see the rare Chaturmukha Brahma Devalayam, or Sitanagaram for the Someswara Swamy temple and its vast sandy beach.

Nature & Recreation

Uppalapadu Bird Sanctuary

The village water tanks are home to 40 species of migratory birds from Siberia, such as Pelicans and Ibises, providing refuge for spot-billed pelicans.

NTR Manasa Sarovaram

A stunning recreational facility spanning 55 acres, featuring lush flowerbeds, manicured lawns, serene ponds, and gentle hills.
