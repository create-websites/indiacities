Wishes & greetings

Wishes in Hindi, English and five regional languages, plus greetings and captions for family, colleagues and social posts.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • 50 Happy Dussehra Wishes for Family and Friends Here are ready-to-send Dussehra wishes for family and friends, grouped by who you are writing to, from warm family blessings to short lines for friends.
  • Dussehra Wishes in Hindi Here are Dussehra wishes in Hindi (Devanagari) with English meanings: 'Dussehra ki hardik shubhkamnayein' and lines about Shri Ram, Maa Durga and the victory of truth.
  • Dussehra Wishes in English Simple, warm Dussehra wishes in English for family, friends and colleagues, centered on courage, new beginnings and the victory of good over evil.
  • Vijayadashami Wishes in Bengali Bengali Bijoya wishes such as 'Shubho Bijoya' and 'asche bochor abar hobe' (it will happen again next year), written in Bengali script with English meanings.
  • Dussehra Wishes in Marathi Marathi Dasara wishes in Devanagari with English meanings, including the classic Apta-leaf line 'sona ghya, sonyasarkha raha' (take gold, stay like gold).
  • Dussehra Wishes in Kannada Kannada Dasara and Vijayadashami wishes in Kannada script with English meanings, including blessings of Goddess Chamundeshwari, Mysuru's presiding deity, and Ayudha Puja greetings.
  • Dussehra Wishes in Telugu Telugu Dasara wishes in Telugu script with English meanings, including 'Dasara shubhakankshalu' and blessings of Durgamma, plus lines that suit Bathukamma and Vijayadashami greetings.
  • Dussehra Wishes in Tamil Tamil Vijayadashami, Saraswati Puja and Ayudha Puja wishes in Tamil script with English meanings, suitable for family, friends and Golu visitors.
  • Dussehra Wishes for Parents and Elders Respectful Dussehra wishes for parents, grandparents and elders that ask for their blessings and thank them for their guidance.
  • Dussehra Wishes for Husband, Wife and Family Affectionate Dussehra wishes for a husband, wife and close family, about partnership, home, courage and the year ahead together.
  • Dussehra Wishes for Friends and Colleagues Friendly Dussehra wishes for friends and colleagues, from cheerful one-liners to warm notes.
  • Professional Dussehra Greetings for Clients and Employees Professional Dussehra greetings for clients, partners and employees that are warm, brief and inclusive.
  • Short Dussehra Wishes for WhatsApp and SMS Short Dussehra wishes for WhatsApp and SMS, most under 15 words, ready to copy and send in bulk without sounding like a forward.
  • Dussehra WhatsApp Status Ideas One-line Dussehra WhatsApp status ideas: festive, reflective and playful lines that read well over a photo of a Ramlila, effigy or family puja.
  • Dussehra Instagram Captions Short Dussehra Instagram captions for Ramlila photos, effigy-burning reels, outfits, family pujas and festive food, with a few hashtag suggestions.
  • Dussehra Facebook Captions and Social Posts Longer Dussehra Facebook posts and captions for personal profiles, community groups and pages, with room for a family photo, an event invitation or a reflection.
  • Dussehra Quotes About Good Over Evil and New Beginnings Original Dussehra lines about good over evil, courage and new beginnings, written for this page rather than attributed to famous people.
  • Vijayadashami Greeting Card Messages Vijayadashami greeting card messages that fit inside a card: a heading line, a two-line blessing and a sign-off, for family, teachers, clients and friends.
  • Dussehra Image Caption and Poster Text Ideas Short headline and poster text for Dussehra images, banners, school notice boards and event flyers, plus tips on keeping text readable over a photograph.
