Agra Tour

Experience the Emblem of Love and the Majesty of the Mughal Dynasty.

The Iconic Taj Mahal

Agra, in Northern India, is a must-see location known for the iconic Taj Mahal, a significant monument built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in the 16th Century as a symbol of eternal love for his wife Mumtaz.

Built with white marbles, gold, and diamonds.
20,000 workers labored for over 10 years.
Fabulous views in both daylight and moonlight.
Considered one of the 7 Wonders of the World.

Situated close to the stunning **Yamuna River**, the monument is encircled by vibrant gardens, offering a peaceful setting for its exquisite design.

The stunning sight of the Taj Mahal as seen from the banks of the Yamuna River.

Agra Fort (Lal-Quila)

In addition to the Taj Mahal, tourists are drawn to the Agra Fort, also known as the Lal-Quila of Agra. This impressive red sandstone fortress was constructed by the Mughal rulers and is home to historic buildings like:

  • Amar Singh Gate
  • Jahangir Mahal
  • Diwan-a-aam

The Golden Triangle: Agra, Delhi & Jaipur

Agra is a key point in the popular tourist route known as the Golden Triangle, which also includes Delhi and Jaipur.

Discovering Jaipur (The Pink City)

Explore the magnificent **Hawa Mahal**, **Jal Mahal**, and the grand **Amer Fort** in Jaipur, also known as the Pink City. These historical landmarks showcase the rich heritage of Rajasthan.

"Taste the Tradition"

You can't visit Agra without trying the famous local sweet, 'Petha', made from pumpkin. Agra's Petha is renowned as the best in India.

Travel Logistics

By Rail

Catch daily direct trains such as the Taj Express and Rajdhani Express to reach Agra from Delhi in just 3 hours.

By Road

Travel from here to there in 4-5 hours using the traditional GT Road or opt for the quicker route via the new Delhi-Noida-Agra Highway.

Quick Tip

It is possible to visit Agra and return to Delhi in a single day.
