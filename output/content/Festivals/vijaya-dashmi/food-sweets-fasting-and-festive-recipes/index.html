Food & sweets

Festival dishes and mithai, regional specialities, recipes, prasad ideas, and how food changes from the Navratri fast to the Dussehra feast.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • Traditional Dussehra Foods Across India Dussehra foods change by region: jalebi and puri-sabzi in the north, fafda-jalebi in Gujarat, puran poli and shrikhand-puri in Maharashtra.
  • Dussehra Sweets: Popular Mithai and Festive Desserts Popular Dussehra sweets include jalebi, besan and boondi laddoo, kheer, shrikhand, puran poli, rasgulla, sandesh, coconut laddoo (narkel naru), Mysore pak and payasam.
  • Jalebi and Dussehra: Why This Sweet Is Popular During the Festival Jalebi is a Dussehra favorite because it is festive, cheap and made fresh at every mela and sweet shop.
  • Fafda Jalebi for Dussehra: Gujarati Tradition and Recipe Guide In Gujarat, Dussehra morning means fafda-jalebi: crisp gram-flour strips with hot, syrupy jalebi, papaya sambharo and green chillies.
  • Puran Poli for Dussehra: Maharashtrian Festive Recipe Puran poli is a soft flatbread stuffed with sweetened chana dal and jaggery, flavored with cardamom and nutmeg, and served with ghee or katachi amti.
  • Shrikhand and Puri for Dussehra: Traditional Meal Ideas Shrikhand-puri is a classic Dasara meal in Maharashtra and Gujarat: chilled, saffron-scented strained yogurt with hot puris, usually with batata bhaji, a koshimbir salad and pickle.
  • Kheer Recipes for Dussehra and Vijayadashami Kheer, rice slowly simmered in milk with sugar and cardamom, is one of the easiest Dussehra sweets and a common offering for the evening puja.
  • Sundal Recipes for Navratri and Vijayadashami Sundal is a quick stir-fry of boiled legumes (chickpeas, black chana, green gram or peanuts) with a mustard and curry-leaf tempering and fresh coconut.
  • Payasam Recipes for Vijayadashami Payasam is the south Indian milk pudding served on Vijayadashami and at most festive meals.
  • Festive Rice Dishes for Dussehra Festive rice dishes for Dussehra include pulihora (tamarind rice) in Andhra and Telangana, chitranna (lemon rice) and bisi bele bath in Karnataka, and puliyodarai and sakkarai pongal in Tamil Nadu.
  • Vegetarian Dussehra Dinner Menu Ideas A balanced vegetarian Dussehra dinner has puri or roti, one dry and one gravy sabzi, dal or kadhi, rice or pulao, raita, a farsan or chaat and one or two sweets such as kheer or jalebi.
  • Dussehra Prasad Ideas for Home Puja Good prasad ideas for a Dussehra home puja are kheer, suji halwa, boondi or besan laddoo, panchamrit, fruit, sundal, sweet pongal and coconut barfi.
  • Navratri Fasting to Dussehra Feast: How the Food Traditions Change During the Navratri fast many families avoid grains, onion and garlic and eat sabudana, kuttu, singhara, samak rice, potatoes, fruit and milk.
  • Dussehra Recipes for a Large Family Gathering For a large Dussehra gathering, choose dishes that scale and hold well: chole or rajma, a dry potato sabzi, jeera rice or pulao, puri or kulcha, boondi raita, one farsan and kheer or gulab jamun.
  • Easy Dussehra Sweets You Can Make at Home Easy Dussehra sweets to make at home include suji halwa (20 minutes), kheer, besan laddoo, coconut laddoo, semiya payasam, shrikhand from hung curd and quick microTopics or no-cook barfis.
  • Dussehra Snack Ideas for Guests Good Dussehra snacks for guests include dhokla, samosa or mini kachori, paneer tikka, sundal, chivda, masala peanuts, fafda, chaat cups and fruit chaat.
  • Dussehra Potluck Menu Ideas For a Dussehra potluck, assign categories rather than dishes: two mains such as chole and paneer, one rice, breads, two sides, a raita or salad, two sweets and drinks.
  • Regional Dussehra Foods from North, South, East and West India North India eats jalebi, puri-chana, kachori and kheer for Dussehra; the west has fafda-jalebi, puran poli and shrikhand-puri.
  • Healthy Dussehra Snack and Sweet Ideas Healthier Dussehra options include sundal, roasted makhana, baked or air-fried samosas, fruit chaat, dates and nut laddoo without added sugar, ragi or oats kheer sweetened with jaggery, and yogurt-based shrikhand with less sugar.
  • 20 Traditional and Modern Dussehra Recipes for a Festive Meal Here are 20 dishes for a Dussehra meal, from jalebi, fafda, puran poli and shrikhand to sundal, payasam and pulihora, plus modern twists such as baked samosa and jalebi rabri cups.
