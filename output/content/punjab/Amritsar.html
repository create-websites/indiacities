Amritsar

The soulful core of Punjab and a portal to the courageous spirit of Sikh heritage.

City Snapshot

Area: 7069 sq.km
Altitude: 3500m
Best Time: October to March
Rainfall: 2954 mm
Languages: Punjabi and English
STD Code: 0183

Overview & History

Amritsar holds great significance for the Sikh religion, renowned as the birthplace of the Golden TempleThe city gets its name from the holy pool located in the temple grounds.

Established in 1574 by Guru Ram Das Sahib, the city started as a home and a sarovar constructed in 1577. The sarovar was later refurbished in 1586 by Guru Arjun Singh, resulting in the city being named Amritsar.

Major Sightseeing

Golden Temple (Sri Harmandir Sahib)

The Golden Temple, a sacred site for Sikhs, showcases architectural excellence blending Hindu and Muslim influences.

  • Layed by Guru Ram Das in the 14th century.
  • Features four doorways and gorgeously decorated arches.
  • It stands in the center of a tank, inviting individuals from every faith.

Jallianwala Bagh

A memorial site close to the Golden Temple serves as a reminder of the tragic events that took place on 13 April 1919 when General Dyer ordered the firing upon a peaceful congregation during Baisakhi celebrations.

Wagah Border

Every evening at sunset, soldiers from both sides of the international border between India and Pakistan participate in the 'Beating the Retreat' ceremony, a synchronized drill that includes lowering their respective national flags.

Other Points of Interest

Durgiana Mandir

Constructed in the 1500s and inspired by the Golden Temple, this sacred site honors Goddess Durga and also pays homage to Lakshmi and Narayana through prayers.

Rambagh Garden

A modern garden showcasing the former residence of Maharaja Ranjit Singh, now transformed into a museum.

Tarn Taran

Situated 25km from here, it contains a sacred tank with reputed healing abilities and a Gurudwara constructed by Guru Arjan Dev in 1768.

Ram Tirath Temple

Located on the road between Amritsar and Chogawan, this is thought to be where Valmiki wrote the Ramayana.

Travel Information

By Air

Rajasansi Airport is well-connected with domestic flights throughout India and has recently been upgraded to an international status.

By Rail

There are direct train connections from Delhi to Amritsar, with a travel time of around 8 to 10 hours.

Major Festivals

Baisakhi and Guru Nanak Jayanti hold great importance in the city's festivities.
