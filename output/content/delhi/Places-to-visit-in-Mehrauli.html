Discover Mehrauli

Enter one of Delhi's seven ancient cities and experience its timeless history.

Historical Overview

Mehrauli, also known as Mihirawali, a district in the southern part of Delhi, is named after its founder. King Mihir Bhoja Part of the Gurjara-Pratihara Dynasty, it is among the seven historic cities that form Delhi.

Surrounded by Hauz Khas, Vasant Kunj, and Tughlakabad, Mehrauli is situated near Gurgaon and showcases British-style architecture designed by renowned architect AGK Menon.

The Qutub Complex

The most important attraction in Mehrauli is the Qutub MinarConstructed by Qutub-ud-din Aibak, the Qutub complex stands as a timeless architectural masterpiece and a UNESCO World Heritage site frequented by tourists throughout the year.

Qutub Festival

Delhi Tourism organizes a popular festival showcasing India's cultural art forms, held annually for three days in November-December.

The Iron Pillar

Situated at the Quwwat-ul-Islam mosque, this ancient iron marvel has stood the test of time, remaining free from rust despite being exposed to sun, air, and rain. Weighing in at six tons, the pillar is adorned with Brahmi inscriptions that hint at its possible origins. Chandragupta II of the Gupta Dynasty.

Originally installed on Vishnupada HillAnangpal Tomar later relocated it to its present spot close to the Qutub Minar.

Religious Monuments

Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiar Kaki Mosque

A 13th-century mosque that hosts the annual Phoolwalon-ki-sair Festival, a three-day festival honoring florists who symbolize unity and camaraderie.

Other Notable Sites

  • Jamali Kamali Mosque: Constructed in 1536 for the Sufi saint Shaikh Jamali Kamboh Dihlawi
  • Moti Masjid: A tiny mosque constructed by Bahadur Shah I for personal worship.
  • Yogmaya Temple: A well-known, ancient temple situated close to the Qutub Minar complex.

Historic Tombs & Mysteries

The Bhulbhulaiyan

Constructed in 1566 by Emperor Akbar, Adham Khan’s Tomb is known as Bhulbhulaiyan due to its maze-like passages where one could get lost.

Mehrauli Archeological Park

Home to the 13th-century Indo-Islamic tomb of Slave dynasty ruler Balban and the nearby tomb of his son, Khan Shahid.

Visiting Mehrauli

Location

Located in the southern district of Delhi, easily reachable from popular neighborhoods such as Hauz Khas and Vasant Kunj.

Festivals

  • Qutub Festival: Nov-Dec
  • Phoolwalon-ki-sair: Annual flower seller's festival

Don't Miss

  • Qutub Minar (UNESCO Site)
  • The Maze (Bhulbhulaiyan)
  • The Mystery Iron Pillar
