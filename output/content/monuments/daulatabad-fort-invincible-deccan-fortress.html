Monuments Within the Fortress

Beyond its fortifications, Daulatabad showcases diverse structures from various periods, each narrating its history.

Chand Minar

Tower of Victory

Constructed in 1445 by Ala-ud-din Bahmani, this 63-meter-high minaret, modeled after the Qutb Minar, marks the Bahmani Sultanate's victory over the fort. Adorned with Persian blue tiles, it stands as a striking piece of Indo-Islamic design.

Chini Mahal

The Chinese Palace

Once adorned with blue and white tiles, this palace became a high-security prison, housing Abul Hasan Tana Shah, Golconda's last king.

Bharat Mata Mandir

A Layered History

Once a Jain temple, this site was turned into a mosque by Alauddin Khalji and, post-independence, was rededicated to Bharat Mata, reflecting the fort's layered religious past.
