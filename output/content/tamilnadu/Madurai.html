The City of Temples

According to legend, Madurai, the ancient city and former capital of the Pandya kings, was blessed by Lord Shiva with divine nectar (Madhu), earning it the name Madhurapuri. This sacred city is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities.

Thirumalai Nayak Palace

Constructed in 1636, renowned for its Indo-Saracenic architecture and imposing black stone columns.

Alagar Koil

This temple is located on Solaimalai hill and is devoted to Lord Alagar (Vishnu).

Goripalayam Dargah

A well-known Islamic site located along the Vaigai river, featuring a unique green dome.
