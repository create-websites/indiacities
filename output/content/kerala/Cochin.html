Cochin: Queen of the Arabian Sea

A contemporary city center blending colonial charm with modern life.

Quick Facts

Area: 309.22 km²
Altitude: 8 meters
Best Time: November to March
Rainfall: 254 cm (June-Oct)
Languages: Malayalam, English
STD Code: 0484

Overview & History

Cochin (Kochi) is a highly developed city in Kerala, known as the commercial hub and one of the most modernized cities in the state. It is comprised of numerous islands and narrow peninsulas. Ernakulam serving as the mainland hub of commercial activity.

The city provides a striking dichotomy: on one side, a tranquil scene of colonial buildings, peaceful beaches, and calm backwaters; on the other, a vibrant modern setting filled with cutting-edge shopping centers.

Heritage & Architecture

Hill Palace Museum

Constructed in 1865, the sprawling complex of 49 traditional-style buildings set on 52 acres of terraced land was once the official residence of the Kochi Royal Family and now stands as the biggest archaeological museum in Kerala.

Bolgatty Palace

Constructed in 1744 by a Dutch merchant on Bolgatty Island, this historic Dutch palace is among the oldest outside of Holland. Currently operating as a heritage hotel, it was formerly owned by the British Raj until 1947.

Mattancherry (Dutch) Palace

Constructed in 1557 by the Portuguese and later refurbished by the Dutch, this site is renowned for its detailed carvings and murals showcasing stories from Hindu epics.

Jewish Synagogue

Built in 1568, this synagogue stands as one of the world's most ancient. Notably, it features exquisite hand-painted floor tiles imported from China.

Iconic Landmarks

Chinese Fishing Nets

Traders from Kublai Khan's court brought in these unique nets that grace the seafront, using an indigenous system believed to be exclusive to this region outside of China.

St. Francis Church

Constructed by the Portuguese in 1503, this historic building once served as the final resting place of explorer Vasco Da Gama until his remains were repatriated to Portugal.

Backwater Cruises

Cochin is a captivating backwater spot where houseboat rides provide stunning vistas of glistening beaches, swaying palm trees, and traditional coir production.

Nature & Recreation

Waterfalls

Visit Athirampally, where water plunges 80 feet, and the nearby Vazhachal waterfalls on the edge of the Sholayar forest range.

Veegaland (Wonderla)

An acclaimed theme park nestled among lush hills, offering water rides, thrilling attractions, and spooky haunted houses suitable for all ages.

Travel Guide

By Air

Located 25 km from the city, Cochin International Airport (Nedumbassery) serves as a gateway connecting major cities in India and the Middle East.

By Rail

Three stations, Ernakulam Junction, Ernakulam Town, and Willingdon Island, with the Junction serving as the hub

Shopping

Visit M.G. Road for unique camel bone carvings, metal artifacts, and pine mats. For a wide selection of antiques and jewelry, check out the GCDA complex.

Don't Miss

  • Santa Cruz Basilica (Fort Kochi)
  • Jew Town Mattancherry
  • Backwater Houseboat Cruise
