Pench National Park

The border of Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra boasts a breathtaking landscape teeming with a variety of wildlife.

Quick Facts

Area: 299 sq.km
Altitude: 200-1000m above sea level
Best Time: March to June
Annual Rainfall: 1300 mm
Temperature: 19.2 to 31.5°C
Language: Hindi, English

Overview

Situated on the border of Madhya Pradesh and MaharashtraThe name of Pench National Park comes from its reputation as one of India's easiest tiger reserves to visit. Pench RiverThe sanctuary is divided into almost equal eastern and western halves by a river that flows from north to south.

Established as a sanctuary in 1977, the region was later designated as a National Park in 1983 and officially recognized as a Tiger Reserve in 1992. Known for its rich biodiversity and distinct southern tropical dry deciduous teak forests, it is a celebrated conservation area.

Wildlife & Predators

Inhabitants

The park is famous for large herds of:

  • Gaur (Indian Bison)
  • Cheetal & Sambar
  • Nilgai & Wild Pig
  • Sloth Bear & Chinkara

The Predators

  • Royal Bengal Tiger
  • Leopard
  • Wild Dog (Dhole)
  • Wolf & Hyena

Avian Diversity

With over 285 species Pench is a haven for birdwatchers, with a diverse array of resident and migratory birds to observe

Hornbill
Indian Pitta
Osprey
White-eyed Buzzard

During the winter season, the park's reservoir is visited by numerous migratory birds such as Brahmini Ducks, Barheaded Geese, and Pochards.

How to Reach

By Air

Sonegaon Airport at Nagpur is the nearest (93 km).

By Rail

Seoni Railway Station is the closest railhead (30 km).

By Road

Seoni is conveniently located near major hubs in Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra, making it the closest bus stand.

Flora

Deciduous teak and mixed forests with scarce medicinal herbs.
