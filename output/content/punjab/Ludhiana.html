Ludhiana

A powerhouse of industry in Punjab, with a rich tapestry of history, culture, and natural beauty.

Industrial Heritage

Situated on the southern bank of the River Sutlej, Ludhiana is a significant industrial hub in Punjab, blending elements of both modernity and tradition with its mix of historical landmarks and revered religious sites.

Ludhiana provides a variety of experiences for travelers from all over the world, from the famous Punjab Agricultural University to its beautiful gardens and grand forts.

Major Attractions

Nehru Rose Garden

This garden, which covers 30 acres, houses more than 17,000 plants and 1,600 different types of roses. Each year, it holds a Rose Festival that draws visitors from near and far.

Tiger Zoo

Embark on a thrilling tiger safari to witness majestic tigers roaming freely in lush, protected jungles. This experience is sure to captivate families and nature enthusiasts alike.

Hardy's World

The third largest indoor snow city in India, this amusement park features themed parties, exciting rides, water sports, and authentic snowfall experiences.

Lodhi Fort

A historic fort, built by Sikander Lodhi 500 years ago, stands near the Sutlej River in the plains.

Culture & Museums

PAU Museum

The Punjab Agricultural University is home to a renowned museum showcasing the rural life and culture of Punjabis, as well as the annual Kisan Mela.

Maharaja Ranjit Singh War Museum

A tribute to brave soldiers worldwide, showcasing exhibits on the Kargil War, historical artifacts, uniforms, and an Audio-Visual Theatre with captivating light and sound presentations.

Sacred Shrines

Gurdwara Charan Kanwal Sahib

Situated in Machhiwara, this spot commemorates where Guru Gobind Singh halted during his guerrilla warfare against the Mughal forces.

Gurudwara Alamgir

Marks the location where Nabi Khan and Ghani Khan protected Guru Gobind Singh. Also, holds a significant festival in December.

Bilwanwali Masjid

A mosque from the Mughal period under Emperor Jahangir's rule, celebrated for its detailed brick carvings and ornate double-storied gates.

Pir-I-Dastir Shrine

A shrine fort situated in the north-west attracts Hindu and Muslim pilgrims, showcasing a rare bond of unity.

Travel Snapshot

Economy

Major industrial hub of Punjab, leading in textiles and manufacturing.

Sports

Guru Nanak Stadium boasts top-quality tracks and is the venue for National Football League games.

Major Events

Rose Festival, Kisan Mela, and the December fair at Gurudwara Alamgir are annual
