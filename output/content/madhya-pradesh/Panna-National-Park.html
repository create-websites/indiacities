Panna National Park

Land of the Wild Cats and India's Diamond Heart.

Essential Information

Area: 543 sq. km
Altitude: 212-538 meters
Best Time: January to May
Rainfall: 1100 mm
Temp: 13.5°C to 31.4°C
Language: Hindi, English

Overview

Located in the central Indian state of Madhya Pradesh, Panna National Park is only 57 km away from the renowned temples of. Khajuraho. The Panna region is unique for its diamond industry as a top-tier Tiger Reserve recognized globally for its diverse population of wild cats, antelope, and deer.

Travelers seeking a mix of cultural heritage and India's top wildlife sightings should not miss the opportunity to visit the park, located close to Khajuraho.

History & Legend

In the past, this area was used as the exclusive hunting grounds for the rulers of Panna, Chhatarpur, and BijawarAccording to local legends, the Pandavas from the Mahabharata are said to have spent some time in seclusion in these very forests.

  • 1981: Formally declared as a National Park.
  • 1994: Designated as the 22nd Project Tiger Reserve in India.
  • Current Status: This reserve is the fifth in Madhya Pradesh's list of Tiger Reserves.

Biodiversity

Wild Mammals

The Tiger, Leopard, Sloth Bear, Wild Dog, Wolf, Hyena, and Caracal reside in the park, while the grasslands are often populated by Blue Bull (Nilgai), Chinkara, and Spotted Deer.

Birdlife

Home to more than 200 species, including winter migrants, the park features standout sightings like the King Vulture, Paradise Flycatcher, and the Peacock - a mesmerizing sight during monsoon dances.

Flora & Landscape

The forest landscape is defined by vast plateaus and deep gorges, featuring the Upper Talgaon Plateau, Hinouta Plateau, and Ken Valley, with dry deciduous forests prevailing in the vegetation.

Travel Guide

By Air

The nearest airport is Khajuraho, located just 37 km from the park.

By Rail

The nearest railhead is Satna, at a distance of 90 km.

By Road

The Panna bus terminal has excellent connections to all key cities in Madhya Pradesh.

Park Highlights

  • Ken River Gorges
  • Migratory winter birds
  • Diamond City heritage
