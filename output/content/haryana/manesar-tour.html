Manesar: The New Gurgaon

A fast-growing industrial town nestled in the scenic Aravalli ranges.

History and Location

Manesar is a rapidly developing industrial town located in the Gurgaon district of Haryana. Situated in the lower Aravalli ranges Situated on a small rise, this once quiet town of 1,000 homes has evolved into a thriving industrial center with a workforce of over 10,000 individuals.

Major Industrial & Business Hub
Located in Aravalli Ranges

Places to Visit

Manesar Village

Located at the edge of town, the village exudes the charm of a traditional setting. Rajasthani HaveliThe hotel offers elegant accommodations and traditional Rajasthani dishes, ideal for vacation enthusiasts.

Modern Living

The town stands out for its self-contained housing developments that operate as small cities, complete with fitness facilities, pools, wellness centers, and retail spaces.

Pataudi Palace

A significant historical attraction near Manesar that showcases the heritage of the Nawabs of Pataudi.

Heritage Architecture

Explore Farukh Nagar for its red stone Mughal mosque or visit the Tomb of Sheikh Musa in Nuh, which blends Muslim and Rajput architecture.

Nearby Nature & Excursions

  • Sultanpur Bird Sanctuary: Just a short distance away, this area is a habitat for many rare and migratory waterfowl.

  • Damdama Lake: A well-liked destination close to Manesar that provides boat rides, hot air ballooning, and camel rides.

  • Sohna Sulphur Springs: Renowned for its therapeutic hot springs, situated near the town.
