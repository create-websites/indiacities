Ayudha Puja & rituals

Ayudha Puja, Vidyarambham, Shami and Apta leaves, Simollanghan, home puja and why the day suits new beginnings.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • Ayudha Puja: Meaning, History and How It Is Celebrated Ayudha Puja ('worship of instruments') is held on Navami, the ninth day of Navratri, mainly in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
  • Why Are Tools, Vehicles and Machines Worshipped During Ayudha Puja? Tools, vehicles and machines are worshipped during Ayudha Puja because they earn the family's living, and honoring them is an act of gratitude for work and safety.
  • Ayudha Puja for Cars, Bikes and Vehicles: Simple Celebration Guide For vehicle Ayudha Puja, wash the car or bike, mark it with turmeric, sandal paste and kumkum, and tie a marigold garland or mango leaves.
  • Ayudha Puja for Offices, Shops and Businesses For Ayudha Puja at work, clean the premises the day before and decorate the entrance with banana stems, mango leaves and marigolds.
  • Ayudha Puja for Computers, Machines and Modern Workplaces In modern workplaces Ayudha Puja honors laptops, servers, machines and lab equipment.
  • Saraswati Puja and Vijayadashami: Connection with Learning In south India, Saraswati Puja falls on Navami: books, instruments and pens are placed before the goddess of learning and left unused for the day.
  • Vidyarambham: Why Children Begin Learning on Vijayadashami Vidyarambham ('beginning of learning') is the Vijayadashami ritual in which a child, usually two to five years old, writes letters for the first time.
  • How Vidyarambham Is Performed in Kerala In Kerala, the child sits on an elder's or teacher's lap and writes 'Om Hari Sri Ganapataye Namah' in a plate of raw rice.
  • Vijayadashami for Students: Why the Day Is Auspicious for Education Students treat Vijayadashami as the day to restart study after Saraswati Puja, begin a new course, or start music or dance lessons.
  • Why People Start New Businesses on Vijayadashami Many people start businesses on Vijayadashami because it is considered auspicious all day, so no separate muhurat is needed.
  • Why Vijayadashami Is Considered Auspicious for Buying Vehicles and Property Vijayadashami is popular for buying vehicles, homes and gold because tradition treats the whole day as auspicious and it marks victory and prosperity.
  • Shami Puja on Dussehra: Meaning and Rituals Shami Puja is done on Vijayadashami afternoon at a Shami tree or with its leaves: devotees offer water, rice, flowers and a lamp, circle the tree, and take leaves home as a blessing.
  • Apta Leaves on Dussehra: Why They Are Exchanged as Gold In Maharashtra, Apta (Bauhinia racemosa) leaves are exchanged on Dasara as 'sona' (gold) with the words 'sona ghya, sonyasarkha raha': take gold and stay like gold.
  • Simollanghan on Dussehra: Meaning and Maharashtrian Tradition Simollanghan ('crossing the boundary') is the Maharashtrian Dasara custom of going to the edge of the village or town, worshipping the Shami or Apta tree and returning with leaves.
  • Dussehra and New Beginnings: Business, Education and Personal Goals Dussehra suits new beginnings because it marks victory and is considered auspicious all day.
  • How to Perform a Simple Vijayadashami Puja at Home For a simple Vijayadashami puja: clean the puja space and place an image of Durga, Rama or your family deity.
  • Dussehra Puja Samagri List: What You Need A basic Dussehra puja samagri list is a lamp with ghee or oil, cotton wicks, incense, camphor, kumkum, turmeric, sandal paste, akshat, flowers or marigold garlands, fruit, sweets, betel leaves and nuts, coconut and a kalash with water.
  • What to Offer During Dussehra Puja Common Dussehra puja offerings are flowers, especially marigolds and Aparajita (blue butterfly pea), akshat, kumkum, fruit, sweets such as laddoo or kheer, betel leaves, coconut and a lamp.
  • Dussehra Muhurat Traditions: What Makes Vijayadashami Auspicious? Vijayadashami is traditionally one of the 'sade teen muhurat', the three and a half days when the whole day is auspicious.
  • Vijayadashami Rituals for Families: A Practical Guide Families can plan Vijayadashami in three parts: a morning puja with books and tools, then taking them back and starting something new.
