Dehradun Tourism

The capital of Uttarakhand is nestled in the Doon Valley, located between the Yamuna and Ganges rivers.

Overview & History

Dehra Doon, also known as Dehradun, is the capital city of Uttarakhand. Its name is derived from 'Dehra' meaning camp and 'Doon' meaning river valley. Legend has it that the famous teacher Dronacharya resided here, earning the city the nickname 'Drona-nagri,' as mentioned in the Mahabharata.

Important Places to Visit

  • Clock Tower: Also referred to as Ghanta-ghar, this historical building features six functioning timepieces.
  • Military Institutes: Location of the Indian Military Academy (IMA) and Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC).
  • Moti Bazar and Paltan Bazar: Famous local markets for shopping and traditional bakery products.
  • Scientific Hub: Host to the ONGC headquarters and Forest Survey of India.

Climate & Logistics

Experience a refreshing climate thanks to the presence of the Sivalik Hills, with convenient rail and air connectivity through the nearby Jolly Grant Airport (25 km away).
