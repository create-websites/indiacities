Amarnath Yatra

Travel to the sacred shrine of Lord Shiva at an elevation of 14,000 feet in the Lidder Valley.

Divine Mythology

Amarnath, a renowned Hindu shrine in the Lidder Valley, is dedicated to Lord Shiva. Legend has it that this cave is where Shiva shared the secrets of creation and immortality with Goddess Parvati.

Two doves eavesdropped on the conversation and were granted immortality, choosing the cave as their permanent residence. Now, thousands of pilgrims flock annually to see the ice-stalagmite Shivalinga, which changes size with the phases of the moon.

The Pilgrimage (Yatra)

Schedule & Timing

The annual yatra organized by the Jammu & Kashmir Government takes place in July and August, corresponding to the Hindu month of Shravan. Darshan is allowed for a month, generally from Ashard Purnimashi to Shravan Purnimashi.

Registration Information

Registration typically opens a month before the Yatra, with the option to register at multiple centers throughout India depending on your location.

Routes & Logistics

Trekking Routes

The classic 42 km (26 mi) rugged trail begins in Pahalgam, taking 4-5 days to complete on foot. A shorter option from Baltal is also available. Ponies can be hired for kids and seniors.

Food & Water

Government depots located at Chandanwari, Sheshnag, and Panchtami offer necessary items at reasonable rates. As tea stalls are present along the route, it is recommended for pilgrims to bring biscuits and canned food.

Medical Assistance

Medical stations have been set up along the route to provide assistance to devotees during their challenging journey.

Yatra Checklist

Registration

Submit your application at least one month in advance through the specified Government counters.

Altitude Awareness

The shrine sits at an elevation of 14,000 feet. Make sure you are in good physical condition for trekking at high altitudes.

Essential Items

Pack high-energy snacks such as toffees and biscuits and bring warm clothing.
