Alleppey: The Watery Paradise

A maze of waterways, estuaries, and shorelines in the center of Kerala, India's own paradise.

Quick Facts

Area: 70.48 sq. km
Altitude: Sea level
Climate: Summer (22.5-35°C) | Winter (22-32°C)
Rainfall: 254 cm
Languages: Malayalam, English
STD Code: 0477

History & Heritage

Modern Alleppey was shaped significantly by Raja Kesava DasThe Diwan of Travancore, also known as the 'Creator of contemporary Alappuzha,' revolutionized the town into a leading port by building roads, canals, and warehouses.

Later developments under Velu Thampi Dalava brought the island of Pathiramanal The district is known for its coconut cultivation and increased rice farming, as well as being home to the first post office and telegraph office in the former Travancore state.

Major Attractions

Alappuzha Beach

A well-liked picnic destination with a 137-year-old pier and historic lighthouse. Nearby, Vijaya Beach Park provides boating and a playground for children.

Kuttanad: The Rice Bowl

Known for being the sole location globally where agriculture is conducted under sea level, this breathtaking 75 km expanse showcases vibrant emerald green rice paddies.

Pathiramanal Island

Only accessible by boat, this island in Vembanad Lake is a sanctuary for rare migratory birds and a must-see for bird enthusiasts.

Krishnapuram Palace

A protected monument exhibiting Kerala architecture. It houses the Gajendra Moksham, the largest single band of mural painting in Kerala.

Culture & Traditions

Alappuzha's culture is inseparable from its legendary boat races and vibrant festivals. OnamThe harvest festival is celebrated with great splendor, showcasing the warmth of the region.

Ambalapuzha Temple: Famous for its Kerala architecture and the traditional payasam (sweet pudding).
Arthunkal: Famous for its St. Andrews Church and the yearly celebration of St. Sebastian in January.

Travel Guide

By Air

The closest airport to Alleppey is Cochin International Airport, where visitors usually opt for a taxi to Cochin followed by a bus or train to reach their destination.

By Rail

Easily accessible from key cities such as Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai, New Delhi, and Kolkata through the Alappuzha station.
