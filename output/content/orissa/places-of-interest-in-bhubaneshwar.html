Bhubaneshwar

The "City of Temples" and the soul of Kalinga architecture.

Background

Bhubaneshwar, the biggest city and capital of Odisha, has a history dating back over 2000 years, being mentioned in historical accounts following the Kalinga war at Dhauli in the 3rd century BC.

In 1946, German architect Otto Konigsberger designed it, making it one of India's first planned cities in modern times. It serves as the ‘Swarna Tribhuja’ (Golden Triangle) Together with Puri and Konark, it is considered one of the most popular destinations in East India.

The city is renowned for its distinctive Kalinga architecture, with over 600 ancient temples celebrated for their elaborate sculptures.

Major Attractions

Lingaraj Temple

Constructed in the 11th century, this colossal 147-foot building showcases the distinctive Swayambhu Linga, a sacred fusion symbolizing both Lord Shiva and Lord Vishnu.

Mukteswara Temple

Dating back to the 10th century, this site is famous for its stunning stone arch and intricate sculptures that showcase Buddhist cultural influences.

Raja Rani Temple

Referred to as the 'love temple,' this 11th-century gem is adorned with intricate carvings of women and couples, set amidst stunningly manicured gardens.

Parasurameswara Temple

A group of 20 miniature temples from the 7th century, honoring Lord Shiva and adorned with intricate carvings of animals, birds, and flowers.

How to Reach

By Air

Biju Patnaik Airport is located just 5 kilometers away from the city, providing convenient connections to both Delhi and Mumbai.

By Rail

Bhubaneshwar Railway Station, located 6 km from the city center, has good connections to Delhi, Hyderabad, and Bangalore.

By Road

The primary bus terminal is situated 6 kilometers away from the city, providing frequent services to all major cities in the region.
