Udaipur: City of Lakes

A place of romance characterized by its sparkling lakes and majestic palaces.

Venice of the East

Udaipur is celebrated as the City of Lakes, showcasing a charming fusion of grandiose structures and tranquil waters. With its rich history and lively celebrations, it stands as a prominent cultural jewel in Rajasthan.

Rajasthan, India
Best Season: September to March
Hindi, English, Rajasthani
STD Code: 0294

Major Sightseeing

Royal Palaces

  • City Palace: A massive complex overlooking the lake.
  • Monsoon Palace: Offers stunning hill-top views.
  • Bagoor Ki Hawali: Known for its historic charm.

Famous Lakes

  • Lake Pichola: The crown jewel of the city.
  • Lake Fathesagar: A popular spot for local outings.

Spiritual & Nature

  • Shri Eklingji Prabhu Temple: A revered spiritual site.
  • Udaipur Zoo: A leisure spot for families.

Events & Culture

  • Mewar Festival: A grand cultural celebration.
  • Arts and Crafts Fair: Held annually in Shilpagram.

Temperature & Timing

The best time to visit Udaipur is from September to March.

In summers (April-June), the temperature can rise above 40ºC, making winters the most favorable time for sightseeing.

Travel Guide

By Air

Located 21 km from the city center, Dabok Airport offers flights to Mumbai, Delhi, Jaipur, and Jod

By Rail

Connected to Delhi, Jaipur, and Ajmer. The luxury Palace On Wheels also stops here.

By Road

Excellent connectivity from Ahmedabad, Mount Abu, Mumbai, and key cities in Rajasthan.

Distances

  • Jaipur: 400 Km
  • New Delhi: 650 Km
