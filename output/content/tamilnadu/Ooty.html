Ooty Tour

The Queen of Hill Stations in the Nilgiris.

Overview & History

John Sullivan developed Ooty as a summer getaway for British officials, and today it is renowned for its tea estates that produce India's beloved beverage, adding to the charm of the resort. It provides a peaceful and captivating escape from urban life.

Top Sightseeing

Botanical Garden

Encompassing 50 acres, this location showcases stunning plant life and hosts a yearly summer flower exhibition in May.

Ooty Lake

Constructed in 1824, an artificial lake provides opportunities for boating, fishing, and a nearby toy train excursion.

Doddabetta Peak

The Telescope House at Tamil Nadu's highest point, standing at 2,623m, provides breathtaking panoramic views.

Centenary Rose Garden

Covers 10 acres with more than 1,000 varieties of roses.
