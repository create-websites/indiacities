Questions & answers

Short, direct answers: why Ravana burns, why he has ten heads, what to wear, what to offer, and how Dussehra relates to Diwali and Navratri.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • Why Is Ravana Burned on Dussehra? Ravana is burned on Dussehra to act out Rama's victory over him in the Ramayana, turning the epic's climax into a public symbol of good overcoming pride and wrongdoing.
  • Why Does Ravana Have Ten Heads? Ravana has ten heads in the Ramayana; one version says they came from Brahma's boon after he offered his heads in penance.
  • Why Are Kumbhakarna and Meghnad Effigies Burned with Ravana? Kumbhakarna and Meghnad are burned with Ravana because they were Lanka's two other great warriors, killed by Rama and Lakshmana in the same war.
  • What Time Is Ravana Dahan Usually Performed? Ravana Dahan is usually performed after sunset on Dussehra, typically between about 6 and 8 pm, after the Ramlila's final episode.
  • Can Dussehra Be Celebrated Without Ravana Dahan? Yes, Dussehra can be celebrated without Ravana Dahan; most of India already does.
  • What Should You Offer During Dussehra Puja? During Dussehra puja, offer flowers (especially marigold and Aparajita), akshat, kumkum and sandal paste, a lamp and incense, fruit, a sweet such as kheer or laddoo, and betel leaves with a coconut.
  • What Should You Wear on Dussehra? On Dussehra, wear new or clean festive clothes, traditionally in red, orange or yellow: a kurta-pyjama, saree, lehenga or salwar suit, or regional dress such as a Bengali red-bordered saree.
  • Which Color Is Considered Auspicious on Vijayadashami? There is no single fixed color, but red, orange, yellow and gold are widely worn on Vijayadashami for victory, energy and prosperity.
  • Can You Start a New Business on Dussehra? Yes.
  • Why Do People Buy Vehicles on Vijayadashami? People buy vehicles on Vijayadashami because the whole day is considered auspicious, Ayudha Puja has just honored vehicles and tools, and the day symbolizes victory.
  • Why Are Books and Instruments Worshipped on Vijayadashami? Books and musical instruments are worshipped because Navratri's last days honor Saraswati, goddess of learning and the arts.
  • Why Are Apta Leaves Exchanged on Dussehra? Apta leaves are exchanged on Dasara in Maharashtra as symbolic gold, given with the words 'sona ghya, sonyasarkha raha' (take gold, stay like gold).
  • What Is Simollanghan and Why Is It Done? Simollanghan means 'crossing the boundary': on Dasara people go to the edge of their village or town, worship the Shami or Apta tree and return with leaves.
  • Why Is Vidyarambham Performed on Vijayadashami? Vidyarambham is performed on Vijayadashami because the day completes Saraswati worship and is considered auspicious for any beginning.
  • Is Dussehra the Same Day as Vijayadashami? Yes.
  • Dussehra vs Diwali: Which Comes First and How Are They Connected? Dussehra comes first, and Diwali follows about 20 days later on the new moon of Kartik.
  • Dussehra vs Navratri: Dates, Rituals and Meaning Navratri runs nine nights from Ashvin Shukla Pratipada, and Dussehra is the tenth day immediately after.
  • Dussehra at Home vs Community Celebration: Ideas and Planning A home Dussehra is quieter and simpler: puja, books and tools, blessings, a meal and perhaps a paper Ravana.
