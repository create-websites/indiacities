Bhiwadi: The Industrial Hub

Rajasthan plays a crucial role in driving the economy and is a vital component of the National Capital Region (NCR).

Strategic Gateway

Bhiwadi is an economically vital industrial town in the Alwar district. Its proximity to Delhi places it within the National Capital Region (NCR), attracting global industrialists and playing a key role in driving Rajasthan's economic development.

A Global Industrial Center

Bhiwadi, known as the third largest industrial hub in India, accommodates a wide range of units from steel and electronics to pharmaceuticals and textiles, representing all major industries. Bhiwadi Manufacturing Association (BMA).

Key MNCs Present

  • Honda Siel & Hero Honda
  • Pepsi & United Breweries
  • Gillette & Ray Ban
  • Saint Gobain & Kajaria Ceramics

Economic Stats

The government has signed MOU investments worth over ₹30,000 crores to boost industrial capacity and infrastructure.

Attractions & Surroundings

Although primarily a commercial area, the town is home to several historic landmarks in close proximity.

  • Vijay Mandir Palace

    Built by Maharaja Jai Singh in 1918, this impressive structure boasts a beautiful garden and a serene lake.

  • Lakes of Alwar

    The Siliserh and Jaisamand Lakes, located nearby, provide stunning getaways created by the past rulers of Alwar.

Location & Climate

Connectivity

  • Gurgaon: 40 km
  • Delhi: 75 km
  • Alwar: 90 km
  • Jaipur: 200 km

Climate

Summer: Up to 48°C (May-June)

Winter: Low of 2°C (Dec-Jan)

Why Invest?

Attractive to investors due to low property rates, minimal pollution, and modern townships.
