History & Overview

Chennai, previously known as Madras, began as a small fishing village and has been under the rule of various empires including the Pallava, Chola, Pandya, and Vijaynagar. In 1639, Francis Day obtained land to construct Fort St. George, which later became the heart of the colonial city.

Presently, Chennai ranks as the fourth largest metropolitan city in India and serves as a significant hub for commerce, industry, and culture, particularly renowned for its temple architecture.
