Jim Corbett National Park

India's First Tiger Reserve - A Paradise for Wildlife Lovers.

A Legacy of Conservation

Located in the Nainital and Pauri Garhwal districts, Jim Corbett National Park holds the distinction of being India's oldest national park. It was chosen in 1973 as the site for the launch of... Project TigerThe park includes hilly landscapes in the Shivalik and Outer Himalaya areas.

Flora and Fauna

The park is home to 600 bird species and a diverse range of trees such as Sal, Khair, and Sissoo, as well as rare animals like the Royal Bengal Tiger, Asian Elephant, and three types of otter. Guests can experience the jungle on Elephant, Jeep, or Canter Safaris.

  • ● Asian Elephant
  • ● Sambar & Barking Deer
  • ● King Cobra & Gharial
