Abroad & comparisons

Dussehra in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia, the UAE and Singapore, and how it compares with Diwali, Navratri, Durga Puja and Onam.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • How Dussehra Is Celebrated in the United States In the United States, Dussehra is celebrated mostly at Hindu temples and community centers on the nearest weekend.
  • Dussehra in New Jersey: Temples, Ramlila and Community Events New Jersey has one of the largest Indian-American communities, so Dussehra brings temple pujas, Navratri garba, Durga Pujas and community Ramlilas across Middlesex County and beyond, with Edison and Oak Tree Road as hubs.
  • Dussehra in California: Community Celebrations and Ramlila In California, Dussehra events cluster in the Bay Area (Fremont, Sunnyvale, San Jose), greater Los Angeles and Sacramento.
  • Dussehra in Texas: Dallas, Houston and Austin Celebrations In Texas, Dallas-Fort Worth (Irving, Frisco, Plano), Houston (Sugar Land and the southwest) and Austin hold the largest Dussehra events.
  • Dussehra in Seattle and the Pacific Northwest In the Seattle area, Dussehra is marked at Eastside temples and community halls in Bothell, Redmond and Bellevue, through Navratri garba nights and Bengali Durga Pujas.
  • How Dussehra Is Celebrated in Canada In Canada, Dussehra is celebrated most visibly in the Greater Toronto Area, Metro Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary and Edmonton.
  • Dussehra in Toronto and the GTA In Toronto and the GTA, Dussehra centers on temples and community grounds in Brampton, Mississauga, Scarborough and Richmond Hill.
  • Dussehra in Vancouver and Surrey In Metro Vancouver, Surrey, Burnaby and Richmond host most Dussehra events: temple pujas, Navratri garba, Durga Pujas and community Ramlila programs, often in indoor halls on the weekend nearest Vijayadashami.
  • How Dussehra Is Celebrated in the United Kingdom In the UK, Dussehra is marked at temples and community centers in London, Leicester, Birmingham, Manchester and beyond.
  • Dussehra in London: Ramlila and Community Celebrations In London, Dussehra brings temple pujas, Navratri garba in Wembley, Harrow, Southall and East Ham, Durga Pujas run by Bengali associations, and some community Ramlila performances.
  • How Dussehra Is Celebrated in Australia In Australia, Dussehra falls in spring and is celebrated at temples and community grounds in Sydney (Parramatta and the west), Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth and Adelaide.
  • Dussehra in Dubai and the UAE In Dubai and the UAE, Dussehra is celebrated at the Hindu Temple in Jebel Ali, the BAPS Mandir in Abu Dhabi and community halls, through Navratri garba and pujas.
  • Dussehra in Singapore: Indian Community Celebrations In Singapore, the season is marked mainly as Navarathri at temples such as Sri Srinivasa Perumal and Sri Thendayuthapani, with nightly cultural performances and Golu-style displays.
  • Dussehra vs Diwali: Meaning, Stories and Traditions Compared Dussehra marks Rama's victory over Ravana and Durga's over Mahishasura.
  • Dussehra vs Navratri: What Is the Difference? Navratri is the nine nights of worshipping the Goddess, starting on Ashvin Shukla Pratipada.
  • Dussehra vs Durga Puja: Regional Traditions Compared Durga Puja is the five-day worship of Durga from Shashthi to Dashami, central in Bengal, Assam, Odisha, Tripura and Bihar.
  • Vijayadashami vs Dasara vs Dussehra: Names and Regional Usage Vijayadashami ('the victorious tenth') is the Sanskrit name used across India.
  • Dussehra vs Onam: Two Different Indian Cultural Traditions Onam is Kerala's harvest festival in the month of Chingam (August to September).
  • How Indian Families Celebrate Dussehra Abroad Indian families abroad usually celebrate Dussehra on the nearest weekend: a home puja, a temple or community program, a Ramlila or children's skit, and a festive meal with friends.
  • How Dussehra Helps Diaspora Families Teach Children About Indian Traditions Dussehra is an easy festival for teaching children abroad because its story is vivid and its lesson is clear.
