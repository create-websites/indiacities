Secunderabad

The ancient twin city of Hyderabad, with roots in Nizam tradition and British military history.

Introduction to Secunderabad

Named after Sikander JahSecunderabad, the third Nizam of the Asaf Jahi dynasty, is located approximately 10 km east of its 'twin city,' Hyderabad, with a road connecting the two cities on the bund. Hussain Sagar Lake.

In the past, the British had a significant military presence in this city, which now serves as a center for historic landmarks and cultural heritage.

Palaces & Official Residences

Rashtrapati Nilayam

Regarded as South India's counterpart to the Rashtrapati Bhavan in New Delhi, this location functions as the official office for the President of India when they visit the region annually.

Falaknuma Palace

Constructed in an Italian architectural style by Sir Vikar Ul Umra, this magnificent palace is renowned worldwide for its stunning beauty. The grand halls are adorned with exquisite precious stones, adding to its allure.

Purani Haveli

Built in the 18th century European style by Mir Mahboob Ali, this structure is renowned for containing the world's longest wooden wardrobe.

Salarjung Museum

Constructed by Salarjung III, this museum contains ancient artworks and personal items belonging to notable emperors such as Aurangzeb, Tipu Sultan, and Shah Jahan.

Nature & Science

Nehru Zoological Park

Encompassing more than 300 acres, this park is among Asia's largest and offers a lion safari and a distinctive nocturnal zoo where visitors can see creatures active only at night.

B.M. Birla Planetarium & Science Museum

A must-see for fans of astronomy, this place is equipped with cutting-edge technology and provides a comprehensive overview of the universe and impressive scientific displays.

Lumbini Park

A contemporary addition to the urban landscape, renowned for its captivating musical fountain display featuring water 'dancing' in sync with curated music.

Spiritual & Educational Landmarks

  • Mecca Masjid: An elegant hall, upheld by stunning arches and showcasing octagonal columns crafted from solid granite.
  • Trinity Church: Historically important to British regiments, now cared for by the local church community.
  • Osmania University: Famous for its extensive passageways adorned with Ellora paintings, gateways with Damascus patterns, and peaceful gardens.
  • Golconda Fort: A Kakatiya marvel from the 13th century that evolved into a globally recognized hub for diamond commerce.

Travel Snapshot

Connectivity: Linked to Hyderabad via the Hussain Sagar Tank Bund road.

Did you know? Secunderabad has gained a reputation as a hub for "nocturnal zoo" activities, thanks to the unique exhibits found at the Nehru Zoological Park.

History Tip: The city is named after Sikander Jah, the 3rd Nizam of the Asaf Jahi dynasty.
