Chandigarh: The Fort of Chandi

India's inaugural planned city, celebrated for its urban layout and architectural excellence.

Historical Planning

Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru commissioned a new capital for Punjab following the 1947 partition, as Lahore had become part of Pakistan. The city was designed by the French architect. Le CorbusierChandigarh was established in 1961 and now serves as the capital of both Punjab and Haryana as a union territory.

First Planned City of India
Highest Per Capita Income in India
Cleanest City in India (Urban Dev. Ministry)
Name Origin: Fort of Chandi

Must-Visit Destinations

Rock Garden & Sukhna Lake

Famous city landmarks include The Rock Garden, featuring one-of-a-kind sculptures crafted from industrial and household waste, and Sukhna Lake, providing a serene escape at the base of the Shivalik Mountains.

Rose Garden

Part of the Leisure Valley, this is the largest garden in AsiaIt is home to numerous rose species and plays a key role in the expansive 'Leisure Valley' park network within the city.

Capitol Complex

Designed by Le Corbusier, the administrative hub includes the Open Hand Monument, Legislative Assembly, and High Court.

A City of Gardens

Famous for its lush vegetation, the city boasts the Botanical Garden, Bougainvillea Garden, Garden of Fragrance (Aromatic Plants), and the Garden of Rare Plants.

Cosmopolitan Culture

The culture of Chandigarh is a lively fusion of influences from neighboring states such as Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Bihar.

  • Diverse North Indian cuisine.
  • Modern dressing with traditional influences (Salwar suits, Trousers, and Jeans).
  • Home to the ancient Chandi temple.

How to Reach

By Air

Chandigarh's domestic airport provides connectivity to nearly all major cities across India.

By Rail

Great transport links from Delhi with Shatabdi, Jan Shatabdi, and Himalayan Queen trains.

By Road

Taxis and buses are easily accessible for the 250 km journey from Delhi using NH 21 and NH 22.

Shopping Hub

Sector 17 is the city's primary shopping and commercial center.
