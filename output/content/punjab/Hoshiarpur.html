Hoshiarpur

A historical entrance where the flatlands of Punjab intersect with the Shivalik foothills.

Regional Profile

Hoshiarpur borders Kangra and Una districts of Himachal Pradesh to the northeast, benefiting from its hilly terrain and forest cover to experience a more temperate climate than surrounding regions in Punjab.

The climate in this region is especially favorable for growing citrus fruits, making them the main agricultural crop in the area.

Ancient Roots & Archaeology

The history of Hoshiarpur stretches back to the Indus Valley CivilizationSignificant stone artifacts have been uncovered through excavations at Stone Age sites such as Atbarapur, Rehmanpur, and Takhni in the Shivalik foothills.

Epic Connections

Local legends associate several sites with the PandavasDasua is noted in the Mahabharata as the residence of Raja Virata, where the Pandavas resided during their period of exile.

Dholbaha Remains

The archaeological findings at Dholbaha temples offer important clues about the district's ancient history and cultural legacy.

Major Attractions

Harike Wetland (Hari-ke-Pattan)

Harike, one of the biggest freshwater wetlands in Northern India, is around 60 km away from Hoshiarpur. The Gurdwara on the lake's edge provides breathtaking views of the area.

Hoshiarpur Fun Park

Ideal for family entertainment, the Fun Park features a mix of exciting rides, an outdoor theater, beautiful gardens, and a variety of dining options.

Nearby Heritage Stops

Exploring Hoshiarpur allows for convenient exploration of Punjab's most famous landmarks:

Wagah Border

Witness the vibrant Beating Retreat Ceremony at the border between India and Pakistan.

Jallianwala Bagh

A fitting tribute to the victims of the 1919 massacre.
