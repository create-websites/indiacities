Ludhiana

India's Lancaster: A center of industry, history, and spiritual legacy.

City Profile

Established in 1480 and named after the Lodhi dynastyLudhiana, a densely populated city in Punjab, is renowned globally for its textile and light engineering industries.

The River Sutlej The river acts as a natural border between Ludhiana and Jalandhar and is a prominent center for education and agriculture, home to the well-known Punjab Agricultural University, famous for its annual Kisaan Mela.

Historical Monuments

Fort of Lodhi

Constructed around 500 years ago by Sikander Lodhi, this historical monument stands proudly on the shores of the River Sutlej.

Phillaur Fort

Originally designed by Dewan Mohkam Chand, this historic fortress now functions as a crucial training hub for law enforcement personnel.

Religious Significance

Gurudwara Shrimanji Sahib Alamgir

Located 10km from the city, this is where Guru Gobind Singh Transforming the peaceful Sikh clan into a warrior community known as The Khalsa, an annual fair is hosted here every December.

Pir-I-Dastgir Shrine

A renowned shrine of Peer Abdul Kadar Gilani in north-west Ludhiana acts as a distinctive gathering place for Hindu and Muslim worshippers.

Other notable sites include Gurudwara Charan Kamal, Gurudwara Nanaksar Jagraon, the Bilwanwali Masjid, as well as the ancient graves of Hussain Khan and Bahadur Khan.

Relaxation & Wildlife

Nehru Rose Garden

Covering 30 acres, this garden showcases 17,000 plants and 1,600 types of roses, as well as stunning fountains. The venue is home to a well-known Rose Festival that takes place each year.

Tiger Safari (Tiger Zoo)

This safari, situated 6 km outside of the city on GT Road, provides an opportunity to witness a variety of magnificent tigers in their natural habitat.

Modern Highlights

Harry's World

A popular destination for people of all ages, with the third largest indoor snow world in the nation, offering ice-skating and artificial snow.

Museums

Discover the abundant local history at the Rural Museum and honor the brave at the city's War Museum.

At a Glance

Industry

World-famous for textiles and light engineering.

Key Events

Kisaan Mela, Rose Festival, and the December Fair at Alamgir.

Education

Home to Punjab Agricultural University.
