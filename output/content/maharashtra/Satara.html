Satara: City of Seven Hills

The sacred Krishna River meets the Sahyadri ranges in the historic capital of the Marathas[cite: 612].

Overview

Surrounded by seven hills, Satara West Maharashtra is blessed with a natural gift, situated near the confluence of the River Krishna. It was once the capital of the Marathas and is bordered by Pune to the north, Solapur to the east, Sangli to the south, and Ratnagiri to the west.

Topography: Sahyadri and Mahadeo ranges stretch through the district[cite: 614].
Key Rivers: Krishna (holy river) and its main tributary, the Koyna[cite: 615,
Temp: Varies between 11.6°C and 37.5°C[cite: 623].
Avg. Rainfall: 1426 mm[cite: 623].

History & Heritage

Satara's rich history includes the belief that Wai, then called 'Viratnagari,' offered temporary refuge to the Pandavas during their 13th year of exile [cite: 621, 622].

The land is famous for many renowned personalities[cite: 617]:

  • Zanshichi Rani Laxmibai, the famed fighter[cite: 618].
  • Savitribai Phule, the first lady teacher in India[cite: 619].
  • Dr. Karmaveer Bhaurao Patil, founder of 'Rayat Shikshan Sanstha'[cite: 620].
  • Krantisinh Nana Patil, freedom fighter[cite: 618].
  • Yeshawantrao Chavan, the first Chief Minister of Maharashtra[cite: 619].

Major Attractions

Ajinkyatara Fort

Constructed by Bhojraj II, the fort stands 3300 feet tall and provides stunning city views. Within its walls are water tanks and temples dedicated to Devi Mangalai, Lord Shiva, and Lord Hanuman.

Thoseghar Water Fall

A renowned picnic destination, particularly popular in the rainy season, is the breathtaking 1000-foot-tall waterfall.

Chalkewadi

Rightfully quoted as the "Garden of Windmills," This website showcases numerous windmills generating thousands of megawatts of electricity [source: 633].

Kuraneshwar Temple

The ancient temple of 'Swyambhu Ganesh' in Satara is considered the 'Gram daivat' and houses shrines dedicated to Lord Ganesh and Lord Shiva [cite: 625, 626].

Other Points of Interest

Satara is a starting point for exploring the scenic hill stations of Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani[cite: 624]. Other notable sites include:

Yevateshwar Temple [cite: 634]
Shivaji Museum [cite: 634]
Kas Lake [cite: 634]
Wai Ganpati Temple [cite: 634]
Char Bhinti Smarak [cite: 634]

Travel Guide

Climate & Vegetation

Rainfall differs greatly, with Mahabaleshwar experiencing heavy rainfall and Tehsil Man receiving very little[cite: 623]. The vegetation ranges from lush monsoon forests in the western regions to sparse scrub in the east[cite: 623].

Strategic Importance

Ajinkyatara was an important stronghold that rulers utilized to oversee all of South Maharashtra in history [cite: 630].

Kuraneshwar Highlights

Visitors can enjoy watching playing rabbits and brightly colored peacocks in the Kuraneshwar temple area [cite: 627].
