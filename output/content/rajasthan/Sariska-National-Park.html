Sariska National Park

An impressive tiger sanctuary nestled in the rugged Aravalli hills of Alwar.

Wilderness of the Aravallis

Just 37 kilometers away from Alwar, Sariska National Park is characterized by rugged cliffs and narrow valleys of the Aravalli hills. Established in 1955, it was brought under control. Project Tiger Established in 1979, the 765.80 sq km reserve is renowned as a top spot for observing the magnificent Royal Bengal Tiger.

Major Species: Tiger, Leopard, Wild Dog
Best Season: November to March
Location: Alwar, Rajasthan
Terrain: Dry Deciduous & Bamboo

Wildlife & Avian Treasures

Prominent Mammals

Dwelling place for Chital, Sambhar, Nilgai, Wild Boar, and predators such as Leopard, Hyena, Jackal, and Jungle

Avian World

A haven for bird enthusiasts with sightings of the Crested Serpent Eagle, Great Indian Horned Owl, Peafowl, and Golden Backed Woodpecker.

Historical Landmarks

Sariska Palace

Originally constructed in 1892 by the Maharaja of Alwar as a hunting lodge, this building is now a heritage hotel located within the sanctuary.

Kankwari Fort

A Mughal building from the 10th century that served as a prison for Dara Shikoh, brother

Ancient Temples

Discover the ancient Neelkanth Shiva temples dating back to the 6th-13th century, along with the nearby Budha

Taalvriksh

Thermal springs renowned for their healing properties are situated close to the park.

Adventure Safaris

Experience the rugged beauty of the park either on Jeep Safaris or by riding on the back of an elephant. Safaris provide amazing chances to observe wildlife and take stunning photographs in the heart of the reserve.

How to Reach

By Air

Jaipur International Airport is the nearest, located 115 km away.

By Rail

Alwar, located 36 km away, serves as the closest major railhead with good connectivity to both Delhi and Jaipur.

By Road

Sariska is located 200 km away from Delhi, which is a 5-hour drive, and 107 km from Jaipur.

Climate

Winter: 10°C - 25°C
Summer: Up to 45°C
