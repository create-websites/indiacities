Rishikesh Tour

The Yoga Hub of the World - Where spiritual enlightenment meets thrilling adventure.

Spiritual Hub & Gateway to Himalayas

Rishikesh, known as the "Yoga Capital of the World", sits on the shores of the "the world capital of Yoga." It provides a peaceful setting for those in search of tranquility, as well as exciting options for adventure enthusiasts such as White Water Rafting, Cliff Jumping, and Kayaking.

Landmarks and Temples

  • Lakshman Jhula: Constructed in 1939, this iron suspension bridge provides breathtaking panoramic views of the city.
  • Bharat Mandir: Adiguru Shankracharya constructed the town's oldest temple in the 12th century.
  • Triveni Ghat: The primary ghat where pilgrims bathe in the holy waters and experience the evening Ganga Aarti.
