Dachigam National Park

An exquisite alpine haven and the final sanctuary of the Kashmir Stag.

Park Snapshot

Location: 22 km from Srinagar
Area: 141 sq. km.
Altitude: 1700m - 4300m
Climate: Winter Min 10°C / Summer Max 32°C

History & Legacy

Originally established as a wildlife sanctuary in 1910 and managed by the Maharaja of Jammu & Kashmir, Dachigam National Park was upgraded to National Park status in 1981.

The park is split into two separate regions due to its significant range in altitude, spanning from 5,500 ft to 14,000 ft.

  • Upper Dachigam: Best visited from May to August due to harsh winters.
  • Lower Dachigam: Accessible year-round, though September to December is the peak time.

Flora & Landscapes

Located in the Western Himalayas, this park showcases a varied terrain of sloping grasslands, rocky cliffs, and coniferous forests. Alpine pastures, waterfalls, and blooming meadows adorn the mountainsides throughout most of the year.

Marsar Lake

A stunning lake located at a high altitude within the sanctuary, ideal for fishing trout.

Forest Variety

Oak, Apricot, Walnut, Chinar, Pine, Wild Cherry, and other diverse tree species call this place home.

Wildlife & Birdlife

The Kashmir Stag (Hangul)

Dachigam is the final natural home of the Hangul deer, along with other wildlife such as Leopards, Musk Deer, Himalayan Black Bears, and Himalayan Marmots.

Avian Diversity

The home of 150 bird species, such as the Golden Eagle, Paradise Flycatcher, and Bearded Vulture. Prime bird-watching months: March to May.

How to Reach & Important Tips

By Air

Srinagar Airport, situated only 22 kilometers away, is the closest air link available.

By Rail

The park is located approximately 310 km away from the nearest railway station, Jammu Tawi.

Mandatory Permit

Prior approval from the Chief Wildlife Warden is necessary to access the park.

Essential Tips

Timings

5:30 AM to 6:30 PM daily.

Entry Fee

No entry fee is required (only the mandatory permit).

Clothing

Pack warm woolen clothing as the weather can be very cold.
