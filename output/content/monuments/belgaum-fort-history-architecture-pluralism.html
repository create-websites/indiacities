A Confluence of Faiths

The fort stands out for its blend of major religious sites, serving as a vibrant showcase of India's diverse heritage.

🕉️

Hindu Temples

Honoring Lord Ganapathi and Goddess Durga, these temples grace the fort's entrance, symbolizing the deep Hindu roots of the region's heritage.

जैन

Jain Basadis

The elegant Kamal Basadi, dating back to 1204, highlights the refined Chalukyan architecture and the enduring Jain heritage fostered by the Ratta dynasty.

☪️

Islamic Mosques

The Safa Masjid, constructed in 1519 by the Adil Shahi Sultanate, blends Deccan and Indo-Saracenic designs, incorporating pillars salvaged from ancient temples.
