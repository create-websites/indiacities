Katrnighat Wildlife Sanctuary

A Gateway to the Untamed Terai Wilderness.

Embrace the Wilderness

Katarniaghat Wildlife Sanctuary is a vital sanctuary in Uttar Pradesh, providing a haven for various species and a peaceful escape into nature's beauty.

This sanctuary is a key stop on the bustling wildlife trail in Northern India, offering a genuine glimpse into the diverse ecosystem of the Terai region.

Conservation and Beauty

The sanctuary's terrain consists of lush forests and tranquil water bodies, providing an ideal habitat for a variety of wildlife. This location is popular among bird enthusiasts, eco-travelers, and photographers seeking to capture the unspoiled beauty of Indian nature.

Eco-Tourism Hub

Discover the wonders of sustainable travel in Uttar Pradesh's notable wildlife reserve.

Photographer's Paradise

From unique avian species to stunning terrestrial creatures, each corner provides a fresh outlook for nature lovers.

Sanctuary Facts

Location

Uttar Pradesh, Northern India.

Primary Interest

Wildlife Observation & Nature Photography.
