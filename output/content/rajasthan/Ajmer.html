Ajmer: The Heart of Rajasthan

Established in the 7th century, this place serves as both a spiritual sanctuary and a historic stronghold.

Historical Legacy

Named after its founder, Raja Ajai Pal Chauhan In the 7th century, Ajmer served as the capital of the Chauhan dynasty until it was conquered by Mohammed Ghauri. Today, it remains a diverse cultural hub and a popular site for both Hindu and Muslim pilgrimages.

  • Significance: Famous for Dargah of Sufi Saint Chisti
  • Languages: Hindi, Urdu, Rajasthani
  • Best Season: October to March
  • STD Code: 0145

Prime Attractions

Dargah Sharif

The revered tomb of Sufi saint Khwaja Moin-ud-din Chisti, admired by Mughal rulers, showcases a gold-plated dome and architectural styles spanning from Humayun to Shah Jahan.

Adhai-Din-Ka-Jhopraa

An impressive example of Indo-Islamic architecture completed in only two and a half days, renowned for its stunning front screen wall featuring seven pointed arches and intricately carved pillars.

Taragarh Fort

This massive fortress stands guard over the city from the Aravalli hills. Inside, you will find the Miran-Saheb-ki-Dargah and breathtaking panoramic views of Ajmer.

Ana Sagar Lake

A man-made reservoir known as Anaji Chauhan Lake is a popular local destination, showcasing marble pavilions (Baradari) constructed by Shah Jahan.

Fairs & Shopping

Urs Fair

Observed over the initial six days of Rajab in honor of the Sufi saint, this sacred time draws pilgrims from diverse communities in a spirit of deep reverence.
