Overview of Coimbatore

City of Textiles, after Chennai. "Manchester of South India" The city's location on the Noyyal River has made it a prime center for cotton production, thanks to its thriving textile industry and fertile black alluvial soil.

Historical Legacy

The region, originally known as Kongunad, was a tribal territory dating back to the 2nd Century A.D. It was successively ruled by the Cholas, Pandyas, and the Vijayanagara Empire.

Established as a capital district under British rule in 1804, it played a significant role in the Second Poligar War of 1801.

Top Sightseeing Spots

Anamalai Sanctuary

An expansive sanctuary for wildlife at an elevation of 1,400 meters, inhabited by elephants, tigers, and a variety of bird species.

Marudamalai Temple

A temple on a hill dedicated to Lord Subrahmanya, known for the popular Thai Possam celebration.

Siruvani Waterfalls

Famous for its stunning landscapes and water abundant in minerals that are thought to possess therapeutic properties.

Black Thunder

An acclaimed amusement park in Asia, located amidst the stunning Nilgiri hills.

How to Reach

  • By Air

    Airport is 12km from the city with regular domestic flights.

  • By Rail

    Served by Coimbatore Junction and Coimbatore North stations.

  • By Road

    Connected to Bangalore (363km) and Chennai (526km) via highways.
