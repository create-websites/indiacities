Keoladeo Ghana National Park

A UNESCO-designated World Heritage site that bird enthusiasts will find to be a paradise.

An Artificial Haven

Bharatpur Bird Sanctuary, also known as Keoladeo Ghana National ParkThe sanctuary in India is one-of-a-kind, being the only one created artificially. Initially used as a hunting ground for the Maharaja of Bharatpur, the ecosystem of the area was established through the construction of a dam and artificial lake, intended to contain floodwaters.

Granted National Park status in 1982 and designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site In 1985, the sanctuary became a crucial winter refuge for the rare Siberian Crane and more than 300 other species of birds.

Flora & Fauna

Avian Species

A popular stopover for migratory birds traveling from Central Asia and beyond, where you can spot Siberian Cranes, Pelicans, Eagles, Hawks, Geese, Wagtails, and Flycatchers in the marshes and scrublands.

Wildlife Mammals

In addition to birds, the park is home to Sambar, Chital, Nilgai, Hyena, Jackal, Wild Boar, Blackbuck, as well as elusive wild cats such as the Fishing Cat and Jungle Cat
