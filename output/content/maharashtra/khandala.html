Khandala Hill Station

An enchanting gem hidden in the misty Western Ghats, ideal for weekend getaways.

Gateway to the Ghats

Located at the top end of Bhor Ghat, Khandala serves as a critical road link between the Deccan Plateau and the Konkan plainThis picturesque hill station in the Western Ghats is a popular weekend getaway for travelers from Mumbai and Pune.

3 km from Lonavala
Part of Sahyadri Mountain Range
Popular Health & Hill Resort
Famous for Chikki varieties

Major Points of Interest

Duke’s Nose

A grand cliff, reminiscent of the Duke of Wellington's nose, provides stunning panoramic vistas of the lush valleys and forests below.

Tiger’s Leap

The viewpoint gives the impression of a tiger leaping through the valley, with its unique echo created by dropping rocks into the crevasse.

Lohgad Fort

Renowned for its invigorating waterfalls and historic rock-hewn caves perched high on a steep staircase.

Korigad Fort

Situated at an elevation of 3050 ft. in Amby Valley, this historic site is reached by visitors trekking through rugged hills from the village of Shahpur.

Attractions Around Khandala

  • Bhushi & Valvan Dams
  • Kaivalyadhama Yoga Hospital
  • Ryewood Park
  • Karla & Bhaja Caves

The ancient Karla and Bhaja caves, carved out of rock, showcase some of India's earliest examples of Early Buddhist temple art.

Travel Guide

By Air

The closest airport is Pune Airport, located just 66 kilometers away.

By Rail

Trains from Mumbai Central and Pune Junction are connected and all trains stop at Khandala.

By Road

Via Mumbai-Pune Expressway.
Mumbai to Lonavala: 104 km.
Pune to Lonavala: 66 km.
