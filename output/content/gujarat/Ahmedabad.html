Ahmedabad Tour

In Gujarat's historic center, tradition blends seamlessly with modern architectural excellence.

City Overview

Situated on the banks of Sabarmati LakeAhmedabad, formerly the capital of the Gujarat Sultanate, is the seventh largest metropolitan city in India.

Known as the "Manchester of the East" With its advanced textile industry, this land is also home to Mahatma Gandhi, known as the 'Father of the Nation', and has been a witness to numerous Indian Independence movements.

Indo-Saracenic Architecture

Sultan Ahmed Shah enhanced the city with a magnificent blend of Hindu and Islamic architectural styles, adorning it with stunning monuments, mosques, pavilions, and mausoleums. Jama Masjid, Ahmed Shah's Mosque, and the famous Siddi Sayyad Mosque.

Green City Attractions

Law Garden

A central destination for avid shoppers seeking unique treasures and an assortment of authentic Gujarati attire.

Victoria Garden

Situated to the south of Bhadra Fort, this park is home to a statue of Queen Victoria and is a well-liked gathering place.

Bal Vatika

A kids' park filled with nature on the shores of Kankaria Lake, featuring both amusement and water attractions.

Award-Winning Greenery

Ahmedabad was honored with the 'Green City Commendation Award' for its 76 parks and 96 traffic islands.

Heritage & Spirituality

Ambaji Temple

A sacred destination for Goddess Amba devotees, revered for its stunning beauty and tranquil ambiance.

Sabarmati Ashram

Mahatma Gandhi's home and a focal point of the independence struggle.

Historic Forts & Step-wells

Explore the Bhadra Fort, Teen Darwaza, and the architectural marvel Dada Hari Vav.

Culture & Festivity

Fascinating Museums

  • Calico Museum of Textiles
  • Kite Museum
  • Veechar Utensil Museum
  • Shreyas Folk Art Museum

Major Festivals

Don't miss the world-popular Kite Festival celebrated every year on Makar Sankranti.
