Meaning & history

What Dussehra and Vijayadashami mean, the stories behind them, and the customs that define the day.

20 guides by Prashant Dhingra

  • Dussehra Festival: Complete Guide to History, Meaning, Traditions and Celebration Dussehra, also called Vijayadashami, falls on the tenth day of the bright half of Ashvin, right after the nine nights of Navratri.
  • Why Is Dussehra Celebrated? History, Mythology and Cultural Significance Dussehra celebrates victory: Rama's defeat of Ravana in the Ramayana tradition and Durga's defeat of Mahishasura in Shakta tradition.
  • Dussehra vs Vijayadashami: Are They the Same Festival? Yes: Dussehra and Vijayadashami name the same day, Ashvin Shukla Dashami, the tenth day after Navratri begins.
  • Why Is Dussehra Celebrated After Navratri? Dussehra comes after Navratri because it is the tenth day that completes the nine nights of worship of the Goddess.
  • The Story of Rama and Ravana Behind Dussehra In the Ramayana, Ravana, king of Lanka, abducts Sita, and Rama crosses to Lanka with Lakshmana, Hanuman and an army of vanaras to rescue her.
  • The Story of Goddess Durga and Mahishasura on Vijayadashami The Devi Mahatmya tells how the gods combined their powers to create Durga, who fought the buffalo demon Mahishasura for nine days and killed him on the tenth.
  • Why Is Ravana Effigy Burning Part of Dussehra? Burning Ravana's effigy acts out the climax of the Ramayana, Rama's defeat of Ravana, as a public symbol of good overcoming arrogance and wrongdoing.
  • What Does the Victory of Good Over Evil Mean on Dussehra? On Dussehra, 'good over evil' refers to Rama over Ravana and Durga over Mahishasura, and families also read it as an inner contest.
  • Why Is Vijayadashami Considered an Auspicious Day for New Beginnings? Vijayadashami is treated as one of the most auspicious days of the year, so many people start learning, business or a purchase without consulting a separate muhurat.
  • How Dussehra Is Celebrated Across India Dussehra changes with the region: Ramlila and Ravana Dahan in the north, Durga visarjan and Bijoya in the east, Simollanghan and Apta leaves in Maharashtra and Golu with Ayudha Puja in the south.
  • Dussehra Traditions: Ramlila, Processions, Puja and Effigy Burning The best-known Dussehra traditions are Ramlila performances, processions, puja and the burning of Ravana's effigy at dusk.
  • Dussehra Symbols and Their Meanings Common Dussehra symbols are Ravana's ten heads (faults to overcome), the bow and arrow (disciplined action), the Shami and Apta leaves (prosperity, given as 'gold'), and marigold garlands and torans (welcome and festivity).
  • Why Are Weapons and Tools Worshipped on Vijayadashami? Weapons and tools are worshipped because they make protection and livelihood possible, and the day remembers the Pandavas recovering their weapons from a Shami tree.
  • Why Is Shami or Apta Tree Worshipped on Dussehra? The Shami tree is worshipped on Dussehra because the Pandavas hid their weapons in one during their year in disguise and took them back on Vijayadashami.
  • Dussehra for Beginners: Everything You Need to Know Dussehra (Vijayadashami) is the tenth day after Navratri begins, usually in September or October.
  • Dussehra Puja Vidhi: Simple Home Celebration Guide A simple Dussehra puja at home has five steps: clean the space, set up an image of Rama, Durga or your family deity, offer flowers, lamp, rice and sweets, honor books or tools, then share prasad and take elders' blessings.
  • What to Do on Dussehra Day: Complete Celebration Checklist On Dussehra day most families clean and decorate the home, do a short puja, honor books and tools, wear new or festive clothes and take elders' blessings.
  • Dussehra Myths vs Facts The most common Dussehra myths are that every celebration burns Ravana, that Dussehra and Durga Puja are unrelated, and that Ravana is a villain everywhere.
  • How Dussehra Has Changed from Traditional Fairs to Modern Celebrations Dussehra has moved from village melas and royal processions to ticketed grounds, live streams and apartment events.
  • 25 Frequently Asked Questions About Dussehra Below are short answers to the questions people ask most about Dussehra: its date, names, stories, rituals, regional customs, food, greetings and safety.
