The Legend of Onake Obavva

In 1779, as Hyder Ali's forces laid siege, the fort's defense rested not on soldiers but on the bravery of a guard’s wife, now immortalized in Kannada legends.

The Secret Entrance

Hyder Ali's troops uncovered a hidden gap in the rocks, just big enough for one person to slip through at once.

A Vigilant Woman

Onake Obavva, the guard's wife, saw soldiers while drawing water. Rather than alerting others, she seized her wooden pestle, the 'onake'.

A Courageous Defense

As each soldier emerged from the hole, Obavva struck them down with the pestle, killing dozens single-handedly and saving the fort that day.

The hidden passage is famously called Onake Obavvana Kindi (Obavva's Crevice).
