The Summer Palace: A Teakwood Jewel

Dubbed 'Rashk-e-Jannat' (Heaven's Envy), the Summer Palace stands as the fort's crown jewel, a stunning showcase of Indo-Islamic design crafted predominantly from teak.

🏛️

Pillars & Arches

The palace boasts elegant arches and ornate pillars, masterfully crafted from teakwood, highlighting Mysorean artisans' expertise.

🎨

Floral Frescoes

The walls and ceilings, once adorned with stunning floral designs and artwork, still hint at the palace's former grandeur.

Royal Durbar

Tipu Sultan held court on the upper floor's east and west balconies, offering a sweeping view of his capital.
