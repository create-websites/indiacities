Thiruvananthapuram

The city of India known as the 'Evergreen City', where the blend of royal traditions and contemporary horizons is evident

City Overview

Formerly known as TrivandrumThiruvananthapuram, the capital of Kerala, is named after the deity Sri Padmanabhaswamy and is said to be the city of the massive serpent in mythology. Anantha, upon which Lord Vishnu reclines.

City of Seven Hills due to its location. "Evergreen City of India" Mahatma Gandhi transformed it into a thriving center for Information Technology, accounting for about 80% of the state's software exports today.

The Soul of the City

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

This stunning architectural marvel, built in honor of Lord Vishnu, combines elements of Kerala and Tamil design. It includes musical pillars, a golden mandap, and a 500-pillared corridor embellished with sculptures and murals.

The temple was selected as a finalist for the 7 Wonders of the World.

Monuments & Museums

Napier Museum

An impressive architectural marvel blending English, Saxon, and Travancore influences, this structure safeguards and displays precious relics from the area's past.

Cultural Hotspots

The main cultural hubs in the city include the Sree Chithra Art Gallery, Zoological Park, and the galleries of Kanaka Kunnu Palace.

Science & Space

Home to the Priyadarshini Space Planetarium (one of India's biggest) alongside the Kerala Science and Technology Museum.

Chachanehru Museum

A unique museum designed specifically for children, situated near prestigious royal landmarks such as Kuthiramalika Palace and Kowidar Palace.

Beaches & Recreation

The city's coastline is characterized by its sapphire seas and shimmering sand beaches.

Kovalam Beach

World-famous for its crescent coastline and water sports.

Shanghumukham

A hub for boating, sunsets, and evening strolls.

Veli Village

Tourist village offering horse riding and lush landscapes.

Visitor Info

Climate

Equatorial climate. Best time to visit: September to May.

Festivals

Don't miss the Swathi Thirunal Music Festival held annually at the Navarathri Mandapam.

Excursions

  • Padmanabhapuram Palace
  • Ponmudi Hill Station
  • Neyyar & Aruvikkara Dams
  • Peppara Wildlife Sanctuary
