Lucknow: The Golden City

An enduring story of Nawabi traditions, majestic architecture, and delectable culinary delights.

At a Glance

  • Area: 79 sq km
  • Altitude: 123 m above sea level
  • Rainfall: 44 cms (June-Sept)
  • Best Time: October - March
  • Languages: Hindi, Urdu, English
  • STD Code: 0522

The Legend of Lakshmanpur

Lucknow's history dates back to the ancient Suryavanshi Dynasty, with legends suggesting that Lakshmana, Lord Rama's brother, established the city near the Gomti River on a raised area known as Lakshmanpur.

Lucknow became a prominent city in the 18th Century when Mughal emperors appointed Nawabs to govern. Under the rule of Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula, the city prospered as a hub for arts, culture, and luxury living.

Architectural Wonders

Bara Imambara

Constructed in 1784 by Nawab Asafuddaula as a response to famine, this marvel includes the magnificent Bhulbhuliya, a complex maze of interconnected corridors.

Chota Imambara

Known as the Husainabad Imambara, this building is renowned for its shining brass domes and beautiful Belgian glass chandeliers.

Rumi Darwaza

The grand entrance to the Bara Imambara is a towering 60-foot-high door constructed without any wood or iron materials.

The Residency

The historic residence of British citizens, it was a battleground during the conflict of 1857 and continues to hold great historical importance.

Parks, Culture & Kothis

  • Kothis: Uncover the fascinating past of Kabooter wali Kothi (a palace for pigeons), Tara Kothi (an observatory), and Bibiapur Kothi (a retreat for hunting).

  • Gautam Buddha Park & Zoo: Famous amusement park with attractions and the Prince of Wales Zoo, founded in 1921.

  • Lucknow Mahautsav: A 10-day event (Nov 25 - Dec 5) featuring Awadhi cuisine, Gazals, and Kathak performances.

Plan Your Visit

By Air

Well connected by direct flights from major Indian cities.

By Rail

Lucknow Junction links to Delhi (Shatabdi), Mumbai (Pushpak), and Kolkata.

Shopping Hubs

Visit Hazratganj for luxury shopping or Aminabad and Chowk for authentic Chikan embroidery and fragrances.
