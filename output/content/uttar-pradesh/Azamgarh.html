Azamgarh Tour

A land of ancient rishis, philosophical movements, and world-renowned crafts.

Overview of Azamgarh

Established in 1665 by Azam, the son of Vikramajit, Azamgarh is renowned as the birthplace of rishi Durvasa. The sage's ashram is situated in the Phulpur tehsil, attracting thousands of students seeking his teachings throughout history.

A Hub of Theology and Poetry

Azamgarh, a key town in Eastern Uttar Pradesh, is renowned for its Azamgarh Nationalist Movement founded by theologian Maulana Shibli Nomani. The city is also known for producing renowned Islamic books, such as the biography of Prophet Muhammad, 'Seerat-ul-Nabi'.

Additionally, Kaifi Azami, a renowned poet and intellectual, calls this city home. The Darul Musannefin Shibli Academy, Asia's largest Arabic library, is also located here.

Places of Interest & Traditional Crafts

Benarsi Sari Udyog, Mubarakpur

The primary industry in the district is the village household industry, with looms installed in most rural homes, providing extensive employment opportunities. The Benarasi saris produced in Mubarakpur are renowned globally.

Black Pottery, Nizamabad

Pottery is a longstanding industry in the district, making a significant impact on the local economy. The exquisite pottery crafted in Nizamabad, such as ornate tea sets, sugar bowls, and sculptures of deities like Ganesh and Laxmi, is renowned worldwide.

Regional Economy

Azamgarh's industrial base focuses on specific crafts, while the district also boasts a strong agricultural foundation. To enhance the economy, it is essential to bridge the gap between the agricultural and industrial sectors through a coordinated approach.

How to Reach

By Rail

Connected to Mumbai, Delhi, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Jaipur, Ajmer, and Amritsar.

By Road

Regular buses operate to Lucknow, the state capital, with a few services also running to New Delhi.

By Air

Varanasi Airport, located 75 kilometers away, is the nearest major hub, with Gorakhpur Airport being another option at a distance of 80 kilometers.
