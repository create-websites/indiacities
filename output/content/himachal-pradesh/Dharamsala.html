Dharamsala

Ruler of the Hills & Urban Haven of Blue Mountains beneath the towering Dhauladhar Range.

At a Glance

Area: 29 Sq. kms
Altitude: 1380 m above sea level
Best Time: Mar-June, Sept-Oct
Climate: Summer (16°C-26°C), Winter (3°C-6°C)

Overview

Dharamsala, a crucial town in Kangra district, sits at the base of the towering Dhauladhar mountains, providing stunning vistas of snow-capped peaks, lush deodar and pine forests, tea plantations, and scenic hills.

Dharamsala offers a perfect holiday with its many streams, refreshing cool air, and close proximity to the snowline. It is a vibrant yet serene destination.

Prime Attractions

McLeodganj

Once inhabited by the semi-nomadic Gaddi tribe, this location is now renowned worldwide as the official home of His Holiness, the Dalai Lama.

Dal Lake

Encircled by towering deodar trees, this lake nestled in a mountain basin offers a tranquil picnic spot easily accessible by road.

Kangra Art Museum

A collection showcasing the valley's arts, crafts, and ancient history, including artifacts from as far back as the 5th century.

Bhagsunath Shrine

A revered Hindu temple with sacred fresh water springs nearby, cherished by devotees.

Spirituality & Heritage

Norbulinka Institute

Incorporating strong Japanese influences, this tranquil setting boasts winding paths, wooden bridges, and gentle streams. The neighboring nunnery offers courses in advanced Buddhist teachings.

Chinmaya Tapovan

A peaceful ashram featuring a 9m tall Hanuman statue, a temple dedicated to Ram, and a serene meditation hall.

Masrur Rock Temples

There are fifteen monolithic rock temples, intricately carved in the exquisite style of the Kailash temple at Ellora, originating from the 8th century.

Nature & Trekking

Triund

Nestled at the base of the Dhauladhar range, providing stunning vistas of mountains and valleys. Perfect for hiking.

Dharmkot

A charming picnic area located atop a hill, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the Kangra valley.

Other notable nature spots include the Tatwani hot springs and the massive waterfall at Machhrial, as well as the cave temples at Trilokpur.

Points of Interest

St. John's Church

Constructed in 1852 beneath deodar boughs, showcasing exquisite Belgian stained glass windows.

Andretta

A pottery center and gallery housing major works of art.

Kunal Pathri

Rock temples from which the surrounding area derives its name.
