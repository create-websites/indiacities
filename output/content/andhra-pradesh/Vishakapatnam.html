Visakhapatnam Trip

The Gem of the East Coast and the City of Fate.

Introduction to Vizag

Visakhapatnam, popularly known as VizagLocated between the Eastern Ghats and the Bay of Bengal, Visakhapatnam, named after the god of bravery, Visakha, has evolved from a small fishing village into a bustling industrial center with one of the best natural harbors in Asia.

The city is surrounded by three hills that contain shrines representing major religions, with the Godavari and Indravati rivers flowing through the area with pride.

Major Attractions

Kailashagiri

A picturesque park atop a hill provides breathtaking views of the Bay of Bengal and the city, reachable by ropeway, making it an enchanting spot for all visitors.

Simhachalam Temple

Built by the Gajapathi Kings of Orissa, this marvel is dedicated to Lord Narasimha and showcases intricately carved pavilions, including the 96-pillared Kalyana Mantapa.

Submarine Museum

Visit INS Kursura, the first submarine museum in Asia, a popular attraction on the beach road for those interested in technology and history.

Dolphin's Nose

A towering 357m high rock, nicknamed for its dolphin-like shape, boasts a striking lighthouse and is a must-see for travelers.

Nature & Wildlife

Indira Gandhi Zoological Park

Covering 250 hectares along Beach Road, this park is surrounded by hills and the ocean, hosting around 700 animals representing 89 species.

Pristine Beaches

The stunning Rishikonda and Ramakrishna Beaches are popular destinations that attract a large number of visitors looking for relaxation and seaside fun.

Beyond the City

Araku Valley

Situated 1000m above sea level on the outskirts of the city, this hill station offers stunning vistas of lush gardens, cascading waterfalls, and meandering mountain streams.

Travel Snapshot

Climate

Tropical Savanna climate. Temperatures range between 23°C and 32°C year-round.

Best Time to Visit

November to February is ideal for moderate temperatures and minimal rain.

Key Facts

Originally created by the British as a key port for exporting mineral riches, it continues to stand as a major industrial center in Andhra Pradesh.
