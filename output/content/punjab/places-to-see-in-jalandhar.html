Jalandhar

India's Sports City where ancient myths intersect with cutting-edge industry.

Sports City of India

Jalandhar is widely known as the 'Sports City of India'It is home to the nation's top sports sector and has nurtured a significant amount of skilled athletes. Particularly, the village of Sansarpur alone has given India 12 of its greatest hockey players.

rivers Beas and Sutlej. Sutlej and Beas rivers.

Places to Visit

Tulsi Mandir

In the Kot Kishan Chand locality stands an ancient monument honoring Vrinda, the wife of Jalandhara. Adjacent to it is the Sheetla Mandir, believed to have been built around the same time as the city.

Shaheed-E-Azam Sardar Bhagat Singh Museum

Situated in Khatkal Kalan, this museum pays tribute to revolutionary martyrs by preserving their memorable belongings, such as the ashes of Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, and Sukhdev.

Gurdwara Chhevin Padshahi

Located in Basti Shaikh, this Gurdwara commemorates the meeting between Guru Hargobind Ji and the Muslim saint Shaikh Darvesh, which took place during the Guru's visit to the Doaba region.

Devi Talab Mandir

A well-known religious site in the city is the 800-year-old Imam Nasir Mosque, along with the two-century-old Jama Masjid and other nearby historical monuments.

Historical & Religious Landmarks

Shiv Mandir

Situated in Gur Mandi, this temple was constructed by a Nawab of Sultanpur Lodhi near Masjid Imam Nasir, reflecting the rich cultural heritage of the city.

Ancient Mosques

In the heart of Jalandhar stands the ancient Imam Nasir Mosque, dating back 800 years, alongside the historic Jama Masjid, showcasing centuries of architectural legacy.

How to Reach

By Air

Nearest Airport is RajaSansi Airport, Amritsar, located approximately 90 km from the city.

By Road

Well-connected via the Delhi-Amritsar Highway. Distance from Chandigarh: 146 km; from Delhi: 350 km.

Surrounding Areas

Surrounded by Ludhiana to the East, Kapurthala to the West, Hoshiarpur
