Tirupati: Spiritual Gateway

Home of Lord Venkateswara on the sacred peaks of Tirumala.

Glorious History

The rich history of Tirupati Venkateswara Temple dates back many centuries, with literary evidence and inscriptions showcasing its ancient origins and frequent visitors. Chola, Pandya, and Pallava dynasties.

Major contributors include Sri Krishnadevaraya, who bestowed the temple with lavish gifts, and Maratha commander Rajhoji BhonsleAfter being under Muslim and British oversight, he set up a lasting endowment fund. Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) Committee assumed control in 1933, succeeded by a Board of Trustees in 1951.

The Temple Experience

This ancient temple, situated on the seventh peak of Tirupati Hill, stands as a symbol of architectural excellence. Gopuram is inscribed with figures of Vaishnava gods.

Notable features include the Ranga Mandapam (Vijaynagara style), the Aina Mahal (Mirror Palace), and the Dhvajasthamba MandapamThe pillars at the center produce musical tones when tapped, while statues of Krishnadevaraya and his consorts decorate the portals.

Darshan & Pilgrim Systems

Vaikuntam Queue Complex

Clean, spacious corridors guide visitors to the central temple. Amenities consist of discounted dining areas, fresh dairy products, healthcare services, and spiritual video broadcasts.

Sudharsanam Token System

You can get tokens for free at different places, such as the choultries at Tirupati Railway Station, which show your darshan time and help reduce wait times.

Special Darshan

Includes ticket categories priced at Rs. 40 and Rs. 50, along with a designated gate at the venue. Maha Dwaram for the physically disabled and the aged.

Pilgrim Facilities

Free Meals & Aid

Temple coupons allow for free full meals from 10 AM to 11 PM, while Aswini Hospital offers complimentary medical aid.

Transport & Luggage

Complimentary luggage transportation is provided to and from Tirumala. Four complimentary buses run within Tirumala, servicing key locations.

Kalyanakatta

Services available for completing tonsure vows at a set price of Rs 2.00 at Kalyanakatta and Rs 10.00 at cottages

Automobile Clinic

A van equipped with spare parts and staffed by mobile mechanics is ready to help vehicles stuck on the Ghat roads.

Connectivity

By Air

Renigunta Airport (15 km) connects to Hyderabad, Chennai, and Bengaluru.

By Rail

Tirupati is the primary station with daily trains to Mumbai, Puri, Varanasi, and Chennai.

By Road

Key connections: Bengaluru (248 km), Chennai (151 km), and Hyderabad (617 km). SVBS bus station if arriving by train.
