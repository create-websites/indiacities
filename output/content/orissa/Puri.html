Puri: The Abode of Lord Jagannath

A sacred coastal city where the spiritual meets the sea.

Quick Facts

Area: 2750 sq.km
Altitude: 559.31 meters
Best Time: Nov to March
Rainfall: 1160 mms
Languages: Oriya, Bengali, Hindi, English
Known As: Nilachal, Nilagiri, Niladri

Overview

Puri, one of India's four revered religious hubs, is among the oldest cities in the eastern region, renowned for its religious significance. Jagannath Puri Following the construction of the renowned Jagannath Temple in the 11th century.

Located on the Bay of Bengal coast, Puri is a well-known beach destination that offers the rare opportunity to witness both sunrise and sunset from its shores. The name 'Puri' translates to town or city in Sanskrit, but it is more commonly associated with the town's main deity, Lord Jagannath.

Major Attractions

Temples in Puri

The Jagannath Temple is a top Hindu pilgrimage site with four gates and a 16-sided monolithic pillar called 'Arunastambh'The temple houses the world's largest kitchen, where 10,000 devotees are served daily.

Other notable shrines include the Gundicha Mandir, Narasimha temple, and Tota Gopinath temple.

Beaches in Puri

Puri Beach in India is revered for its holiness, with millions of visitors seeking a holy bath year-round in hopes of achieving eternal salvation, or Moksha.

Fairs and Festivals

Chandana Yatra

In April, deities are honored with boat rides on Narendra Sarovara after being bathed in sandalwood-infused water.

Snana Yatra

The Bath Festival includes a grand procession where 108 pots of water are poured on the deities.

Rath Yatra (Chariot Festival)

The renowned festival, taking place in June-July, features three enormous chariots carrying Jagannath, Balarama, and Subhadra. The chariot of Lord Jagannath reaches a height of 45 feet and is equipped with 16 wheels. More than 4,000 individuals are responsible for pulling the chariots to Gundicha Mandir

Anavasara: A time when Lord Jagannath is believed to be ill with a fever and is given healing herbal juices.
Nava-Kalevara: A rare ritual when Lord Jagannath receives a new body.

How to Reach

By Air

Nearest airport is Bhubaneswar (60 km), with flights to Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, and more.

By Road

Easily accessible via a network of roads connecting to nearby areas and major cities.

By Rail

Linked to key cities such as Bhubaneswar, Kolkata, Varanasi, Hyderabad, and Chennai.
